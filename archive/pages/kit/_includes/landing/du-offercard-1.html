{% comment %} ============================================================= du-offercard-1 — the price card on a pre-sell. Next's bottomcta blocks show a product and a button but never a price, so a pre-sell either stays silent about cost or sends people to the offer page to find out. Norse puts the whole thing on the page: tiers, per-unit maths, what is included, what the guarantee is. This shows the same, and still hands off — there is no cart here. Naming the price before the click is the point: it filters, and it means the offer page is a confirmation rather than a surprise.
