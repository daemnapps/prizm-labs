## THE VIBE HOLDS

This is social media. We are technically writing an ad, but it has to read as
content, and the source earned its reach partly on how it feels. The vibe
above names that feel. Hold it in every line you write:

Straight is the default. A straight source stays straight unless the culture read or the culture map gives a receipt AND the piece is not a testimonial, a before/after, a founder story, a demonstration, or about pain, health or money.

1. **A funny source stays funny.** Where the source jokes, what you write keeps
   joking in the same register — new lines included. Keep: no joke on the proof
   or the offer. Allow: the twist beat may be the product doing its job, shown,
   not claimed. A plain beat with the proof always follows it.
2. **A planted joke comes from the room.** A straight source may take a planted
   joke only when it comes from the room: a meme, format or reference the
   culture read or the avatar's culture map shows this audience already laughs
   at. Name the receipt. A joke the writer invented is still a finding. The
   avatar culture maps (watch and listen · trust · references and slang · care
   about · laugh at · off-limits) count as the receipt for each avatar until
   the avatar's own research replaces them, and nothing on the culture map's
   off-limits list is ever planted. Name each receipt after the piece, never
   inside the copy: one line,
   `PLANTED: <the line> · RECEIPT: <the culture read or culture map line it comes from>`.
3. **Every line sounds like it came from the same person**: the same register,
   the same reference world, nothing from the AVOID line. A line that reads like
   an ad dropped into the piece is a defect, however true it is.
4. If the vibe is empty or `not shown in the source`, hold what the source's own
   words do.
