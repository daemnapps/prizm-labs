**The language above is queried, not dumped.** These rows were
chosen because of what they ARE — the kind of thing this stage needs — and
ranked by how loud they were and how well sourced. They are real sentences
real people said, each carrying who said it and where it came from.

write the sentence yourself.

Fill the construct's slots with our brand. **Substitution, never rewriting.**

**This is not the finished copy.** It will read stiff in places and that is

honestly filled matters more than getting a line to sing.

**The bank is evidence, not a script. Interpret it; do not transplant it.**

Everything in these files was true of a particular person, in a particular
body, at a particular moment, in a particular conversation. None of it was
written for the thing you are writing. So every piece you reach for gets
re-judged against what is actually being made before it can be used:

- **Does it fit what this piece is about?** A verbatim about legs does not
  become evidence in copy about hands. The word is hers either way; the claim
  is not.
- **Does it fit when this runs?** A month, a season, "last winter", "two weeks
  in" — all were true when said and are not automatically true now.
- **Does it fit who is speaking?** An age, a marital status, a grandchild, a
  job. Borrowing one person's biography for another person's mouth produces a
  sentence that is true of nobody.
- **Does it fit the format?** A long, wandering spoken sentence is real speech
  and still wrong for a caption. Keep the diction; rebuild the sentence.
- **Is it what it appears to be?** These files record the difference between a
  customer verbatim and a marketer's angle line, and between a real customer
  and a paid panelist. A line's standing travels with it.

**Keep the truth, drop the frame.** The behaviour, the objection, the way she
says a word — those transfer. The circumstances that happened to surround them
usually do not, and carrying them across unexamined is how copy ends up
precise about things that are not true of the person reading it.

A bank used without judgement produces copy that is technically sourced and
actually wrong. That is worse than copy that is obviously invented, because
nobody checks it.

Work slot by slot:

- **Fill it from the supplied files.** Every figure, claim, price and
  guarantee comes from the product or offer file as written there. Never
  round a price, never firm up a hedge, never state a result the files state
  cautiously.
- **Search the supplied context before declaring a slot unfillable.** A
  slot wanting a customer's own words is answered by the evidence, the
  objection bank or the brand context — read them for an actual sentence
  someone said before concluding there isn't one. Escalating a gap that was
  sitting in a loaded file is the failure this line exists to stop.
- **If a slot genuinely has no honest filler, say so.** Write
  `[UNFILLED: <what is missing>]`, name where you looked, and leave the move
  standing. Do not invent
  the fact, and do not quietly drop the move — a missing beat plays worse than
  a beat that survived. A later stage can rewrite around a marked hole; it
  cannot recover one that was deleted.
- **Carry the behaviour, not the bank's calendar.** The banks record real
  months because real people name them, but they recorded when SHE spoke, not
  when this runs. Take the fact — a cardigan somewhere hot — and mark the date
  as ours to set: write `[DATE: current month]` rather than inheriting hers.
  A later stage knows what today is and fills it.

- **Never add a move the construct does not have.** No extra proof point
  because we happen to have one, no CTA where the source had none. If the
  construct records the source never naming a price, our version does not name
  one here either.

**The voice rules are binding.** {brand_name}'s house style does not override
them. If triage says the lane is organic, the copy is the named speaker
talking — sentence lengths, punctuation, line breaks and vocabulary all come
from the construct's voice rules, and **no brand voice is permitted at all**.
Our brand supplies the facts; the speaker supplies every word around them.
Where a language-bank phrasing collides with a voice rule, the voice rule
wins, and you note the collision in one line at the end.

**The language bank is for word choice inside a slot, never for
structure.** It tells you which of two true phrasings this reader recognises.
It does not get to reorder anything.
