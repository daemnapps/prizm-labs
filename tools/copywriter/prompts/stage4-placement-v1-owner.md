Two decisions, both made against the source's real shape rather than a
preference.

**1. WHERE THE PRODUCT ENTERS**

Name the move number after which the product first appears, and say why that
point and not earlier.

The construct already records where the source put its own equivalent — the
thing it was selling, recommending or demonstrating. **Start from that
position.** Moving it earlier is a real decision with a real cost: a source
that withheld its product until the last beat did that because the beats
before it were buying attention, and spending that attention early is how a
replicated piece stops working while still looking identical.

Move it only for a stated reason, and state the reason. "Ads should mention
the product early" is not a reason; "this format is a review and a review
that hides what it reviewed reads as a bait" is.

**2. THE LENGTH BUDGET**

Give a target length for the finished piece, in words, and say what it is
based on: the source's own length, the format's norms, or the platform. Then
break it down — roughly how many words each move gets.

Rules that bind the budget:

- **The source's proportions are the default.** If the source spent 60% of
  its length before the turn, so does ours. Proportion is structure.
- **Do not buy room by compressing a source beat.** If a move needs more than
  the budget holds, say so plainly and name what it would cost. Trimming the
  source's own beats to make room for ours is the failure this rule exists to
  prevent — it produces a piece that is technically complete and structurally
  hollowed out.
- **If the piece needs to run longer than the format sensibly holds, say
  that.** An overrun is a finding to escalate, not something to quietly
  absorb or quietly trim. Name the number and let a person rule.

**Also decide:**

- **Whether the offer appears at all**, and if so, how much of it — price,
  guarantee, both, neither. The construct records what the source did; a
  format like `caption` may carry none of it, and a `straight-dr` carries all
  of it.
- **What the reader is asked to do**, if anything. Where the source had no
  ask and the lane is organic, adding one is a change worth naming rather
  than assuming.
- **Any unfilled slot from the injection that now has to be resolved**, and
  by whom — a fact someone must supply, or a beat that will need rewriting
  around the hole.
