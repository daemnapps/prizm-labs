**The language above is queried, not dumped.** These rows were
chosen because of what they ARE — the kind of thing this stage needs — and
ranked by how loud they were and how well sourced. They are real sentences
real people said, each carrying who said it and where it came from.

Use their words. A row marked `ATTRIBUTION ASSUMED` is weaker evidence and a
row from a paid panel is not a customer speaking; weigh them accordingly and
never present either as something a customer said. If nothing in the rows
fits, say so — an empty result is a fact about the bank, not a licence to
write the sentence yourself.

**The research above is the same kind of evidence, pulled live rather than
banked.** A proof point, and the objection you answer, may cite a research
receipt — a permalink, a date, a count with its denominator — exactly as it
may cite a language-bank row; a receipt and a row carry the same weight, and
one of the two is required either way. A section marked `[UNFILLED]` there
means nothing came back, which is a fact about the pull and never a licence
to write the sentence yourself. A research receipt is evidence of what real
people SAY — it never becomes a price, a guarantee or a promotion, which
come from the offer file and nowhere else.

Land it. The piece has done its work; this is where it ends and, if the
placement plan says so, where it asks.

**Write the ending the construct's shape calls for — not the ending an ad
would have.** The construct records how the source closed. A post that trailed
off on a human note and a DR ad that closed on a guarantee are different
endings, and replicating the first while bolting on the second produces a
piece that changes character in its last three lines. Readers feel that even
when they cannot name it.

**The objection, if the placement plan called for one.** One objection, at the
point the reader actually flinches — which is usually just before the ask, not
at the end. Take our real answer from the objection bank or brand context.
Answer it in the speaker's voice, in a sentence or two, and move on. An
objection handled at length reads as defensiveness and introduces a doubt the
reader had not yet had.

**The ask, if there is one.** The placement plan decides whether this piece
asks at all and how much of the offer it carries. Follow it.

- Every price, pack size and guarantee comes **verbatim from the offer file**.
  Not rounded, not softened, not tightened, not paraphrased.
- If the offer file records a condition on the guarantee, the condition
  travels with it. A guarantee quoted without its condition is a different
  promise from the one the business makes.
- If the placement plan said this piece carries no offer, it carries none.
  Do not add a price because the ending feels unfinished without one.

**PROOF PLACEMENT.** Where the close carries proof at all, it lands at the
beat where doubt peaks — verification, per the doctrine above — not wherever
is convenient to write. If the piece already earned its proof earlier, this
close does not repeat it just to have some.

**THE ANCHOR.** If the ask carries a price, it sits against a bigger
anchor — per the offer-close rules above — never bare. Take the anchor from
the offer file; never invent one.

**THE GUARANTEE.** Where the placement plan calls for a guarantee, restate
every promise the piece made, once, wrapped in the risk reversal — the
final intensification, not a new claim. Verbatim wording and its condition
still come from the offer file exactly as written above.

**The voice rules still bind, and they bind hardest here.** The close is where
replicated copy most often reverts to house style, because closes are the most
formulaic thing anyone writes. If the lane is organic, this ending is that
person ending their own post. No sign-off that they would not write.

**What you may not do:**

- **No urgency that the offer file does not carry.** No countdown, no
  scarcity, no "before it's gone" invented to lift a flat ending.
- **No new claim in the close.** Everything asserted here has already been
  earned earlier in the piece or comes from the files. A close is not the
  place to introduce a benefit.
- **No fabricated deadline or promotion.** If a live promotion exists it is in
  the offer file; if it is not there, this piece runs on the evergreen offer
  and says nothing about timing.

**This stage ends the ARGUMENT, not the ad.** The finished copy does not
exist yet and is not your job — a later stage renders this argument into each
output format, and it will express this ending in whatever way that format
allows. Write the ending as content, not as layout: no line-break decisions,
no formatting, and above all none of the source's own units — no card, no
timestamp, no shot.
