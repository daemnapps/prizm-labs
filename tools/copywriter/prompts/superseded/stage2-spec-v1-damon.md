Turn the record into a **brand-free construct** — the same piece with every
trace of whose it was removed, and every removed thing turned into a labelled
slot.

This is the stage my chain did not have, and its absence is why output kept
arriving sounding like our brand rather than like the source. Writing straight
from a record means writing *about* our brand in roughly the source's
direction. Writing from a construct means putting our brand *into* the
source's actual shape. Those produce different copy and only one of them
replicates anything.

**The construct is the source with the nouns pulled out.** Same beats, same
order, same lengths, same voice markers. What changes: the product, the
person, the category, the specific claims and figures all become slots. What
must not change: the moves, their sequence, or how much room each one takes.

Give me:

**MOVES** — numbered, in the record's order. Each carries:

1. **What the move does** — its job, stated so it could be performed for any
   product in any category. "Names a belief the reader holds and contradicts
   it" — not "says brown spots aren't permanent."
2. **The shape it takes** — length, whether it is one line or several, where
   it breaks, its register. Enough that someone could write this move without
   having seen the source.
3. **Its slots** — every piece of specific content it needs, as
   `[SLOT: name]`, with one line on what would legitimately fill it. A slot
   is a question the injection has to answer, so name it by what it needs
   (`[SLOT: the belief being contradicted]`), never by what the source
   happened to put there.

**THE SPINE** — two or three sentences on why this piece works as a sequence:
what the opening buys, what the turn spends it on, why the close lands where
it does. If a move were removed, this is what would tell you what breaks.

**VOICE CONSTRUCT** — the voice markers from the record, restated as rules
that could be followed rather than observations. "Sentences run four to nine
words; no semicolons; lines break on the beat, not the clause." Where the lane
is organic, note here that these rules are **binding, not stylistic** — the
copy is that person speaking and the markers are what makes it them.

**LOAD-BEARING** — the two or three elements this piece cannot survive losing.
Name them and say what each is carrying. Injection is allowed to substitute
into these; it is not allowed to cut them.

**FREE** — anything genuinely incidental, which a later stage may drop if the
format or the length demands it. If nothing is free, say so — a tight piece
with no slack is a real finding and stops a later stage from trimming
something structural.

**Nothing brand-specific may survive into this document.** No product name, no
category, no figure from the source, no claim. If you find yourself writing
one, it belongs in a slot instead. The test: this construct should be equally
usable by a skincare brand, a supplement, and a piece of software — and if it
is not, it is still a record, not a spec.
