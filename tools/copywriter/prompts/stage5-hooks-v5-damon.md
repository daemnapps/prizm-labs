## THE VIBE HOLDS

This is social media. We are technically writing an ad, but it has to read as
content, and the source earned its reach partly on how it feels. The vibe
above names that feel. Hold it in every line you write:

1. **A funny source stays funny.** Where the source jokes, what you write keeps
   joking in the same register — new lines included. Keep: no joke on the proof
   or the offer. Allow: the twist beat may be the product doing its job, shown,
   not claimed. A plain beat with the proof always follows it.
2. **A planted joke comes from the room.** A straight source may take a planted
   joke only when it comes from the room: a meme, format or reference the
   culture read or the avatar's humor map shows this audience already laughs
   at. Name the receipt. A joke the writer invented is still a finding. The
   avatar humor maps (laughs at · tropes · creators · never do) count as the
   receipt for each avatar until the avatar's own research replaces them, and
   nothing on the humor map's never-do list is ever planted. Name each receipt
   after the piece, never inside the copy: one line,
   `PLANTED: <the line> · RECEIPT: <the culture read or humor map line it comes from>`.
3. **Every line sounds like it came from the same person**: the same register,
   the same reference world, nothing from the AVOID line. A line that reads like
   an ad dropped into the piece is a defect, however true it is.
4. If the vibe is empty or `not shown in the source`, hold what the source's own
   words do.

## THE JOKE LOCK

If the vibe's `THE JOKE:` line is not `none`, the joke is locked like a fixed
word. Every hook, the control included, carries the same mechanism. Where a
hook's words carry the joke, fill them with a fresh instance of the same joke —
an exaggeration with another exaggeration of the same size, an understatement
with another understatement — taken from the room's own words in the language
above, never with the product's literal benefit. The true result is the body's
job; the hook's job is the laugh that makes the reader want to find out what is
really true. No two hooks use the same instance.

Keep: no joke on the proof or the offer. No hook jokes over a claim, a number,
a price or the guarantee. Allow: the twist beat may be the product doing its
job, shown, not claimed. A plain beat with the proof always follows it — so a
hook whose twist is the product at work hands straight to a body that opens
on the plain proof. A planted joke in a hook names its receipt like any other
(THE VIBE HOLDS, rule 2).

**THE JOKE TEST.** For each hook, one line: `LANDS` (same mechanism, fresh
instance, from the room — the product shown doing its job counts) or `FLAT` (a
literal benefit claimed, a joke on the proof or the offer, a repeat, or the
mechanism lost). Every `FLAT` hook is rewritten and printed again, corrected.
