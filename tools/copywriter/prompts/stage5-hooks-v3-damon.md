**THE VARIATIONS**

Then {hook_count} more, each doing a genuinely different job — not the same
idea reworded. Before each, name in one line the move it makes and what it is
betting on.

Draw them from things the source and the brand context actually contain:

- A different beat of the source promoted to the front
- A customer's own words, quoted, where the language bank or context carries
  them
- The turn stated up front instead of withheld
- The objection said out loud before the reader can think it
- A concrete number or duration the files support
- The belief being contradicted, stated flatly as the belief

**Rules that bind every one of them:**

- **The voice rules are absolute.** If the lane is organic, every hook is that
  named person speaking. A hook that sounds like a brand — even a great one —
  is disqualified, and no amount of performance argues it back in.
- **Never spend a hook on a claim the files do not carry.** A hook is the most
  quoted line in a piece; an unsupported one is the most expensive place to
  put a claim we cannot stand behind.
- **Match the format.** A `caption` hook and a `long-story` hook are different
  objects; the format triage named binds here.

**The machine never picks.** All of them ship. Do not rank them, do not name
a favourite, do not mark one recommended. Choosing is a human act and a stage
that pre-empts it is deciding something it has no evidence for.
