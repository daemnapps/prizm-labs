**This is the GENERAL lane.** The formats came from swipe research — proven
videos by other people — not from any one creator's page. The brief is
therefore written **to no one**: a production-ready specification any
qualified maker could shoot after the matching step assigns one. Nothing
in it references a specific person, their posts, their habits, or their
style; where the creator lane says "which of her posts it came from," this
lane says **what the concept demands of whoever carries it**. Creator
matching is a separate pass with its own prompt — never done here.

Turn the outputs into the maker document. Everything upstream was working
material; this is the only thing that leaves the building.

Five rules govern everything below.

**Nothing unresolved crosses into the maker's document.** If a script still
carries a `[SLOT: …]`, either the fact exists **in one of the files above**
and you fill it from there, or the line is cut. A maker who meets an
unfilled slot will skip the line or invent something — both are worse than
not having the line. Every slot you cut gets listed in the internal section
so nobody thinks it was handled.

There is no third option. **Generalising a slot is filling it.** Turning
`[SLOT: current promotion]` into "the bundle deal" has announced a
promotion nobody approved. If the fact is not in a file, the line is cut —
say so in the internal document and move on.

**This document adds no facts.** Every claim traces to a supplied file.
Watch for the ones that arrive as flavour rather than as claims: a country
or heritage the product file never mentions, a named method, an ingredient
count, a manufacturing story, a founder detail, a mechanism stated more
confidently than the file states it. If it is not in a file, it does not
appear — not in a concept name, not in a hook option, not in an aside.

And a phrase appearing in a file is not automatically ours to use. The
language bank records what customers say **and what they were shown not to
say** — a phrase inside a sentence that reports its absence is evidence
against that phrase, not a source for it.

Prices come from the offer file exactly as written — character-exact for
the guarantee. Where the same product sells at different prices on
different surfaces, say which surface each price belongs to.

**Directions unless the format demands a script.** Concept-and-beats by
default, so an assigned creator keeps her own words. Where a format is a
performed story that lives or dies on its exact lines, say so plainly and
carry the script as written — and mark which mode each concept uses at the
top of its section.

**Every concept names its source format and what it demands of the
maker.** The maker requirements are the matcher's raw material: delivery
mode (spoken monologue, deadpan comedy, performed drama, text-overlay),
age range the format assumes, body-visibility comfort (which zones appear
on camera), and any physical or production demands. Concrete, checkable
lines — not vibes.

**The maker must never have to ask a question.** If whoever gets assigned
would have to guess a length, a shot, a product, or what they are allowed
to say, the brief has failed and the fix is in the brief.
