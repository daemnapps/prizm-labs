## The whole line

- **Brand record** (Before any month) — Check the brand can run, mine its old sends for moments, file them. *Triggered by:* Control Room: check-brand · mine-moments · file-moments. **Works**
- **The calendar** (Once a month) — Nine layers: holidays → moments → who's live → dates → concepts & arguments → email types → offers → affiliates → order. Two of them are AI. *Triggered by:* Control Room: plan-month. **Works**
- **Review** (Once a month) — The PM reads the month on the calendar page and rewrites or cuts sends. Those edits are kept separate, so a re-plan never wipes them. *Triggered by:* The calendar page, localhost:8788. **Works**
- **Hand-off** (Per send) — Turns each calendar send into the exact instruction the writing chain gets, and flags anything that would fail before money is spent. *Triggered by:* copy_plan.py (the check) · brief.py (the instruction). **Works**
- **Writing** (Per send · ~12 min) — Twelve steps, below. Five on Opus, three on Sonnet, four on Haiku. *Triggered by:* tools/email-production/run_month.py — not in the Control Room yet. **Tune**
- **Pictures & layout** (Per send · ~2 min) — GPT makes the hero and the offer picture; the email is laid out in Brand B's look. *Triggered by:* simple_email.py. **New today**
- **Price check** (Per send) — Every price in the email must be one the offer bank sells today for that send's offer, or nothing goes to Klaviyo. *Triggered by:* simple_email.py — runs before every push. **New today**
- **Klaviyo** (Per send) — A draft campaign, to the send's own segment, with the main subject line. Never sent by the machine — a person presses Schedule. *Triggered by:* simple_email.py --push. **New today**
- **Scheduling** (Per send) — Each draft is set for its calendar day at 1pm in the recipient's own time zone — Brand B's best-earning hour across 209 of its own sends. *Triggered by:* brands/brand-b/email/simple.json → send_time. **New today**
- **Learnings** (After sends) — Klaviyo results joined to what each email was; next month's calendar plans from them. *Triggered by:* pull/learnings.py. **Works**
