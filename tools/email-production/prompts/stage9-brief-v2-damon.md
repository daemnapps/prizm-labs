**THE OFFER**

What this email carries, verbatim from the offer file, with any guarantee's

What this email is betting on, and what would tell us the bet was wrong. Not

**UNRESOLVED**

Every `[UNFILLED: …]`, every place the plan could not be executed, every
collision logged upstream, and anything a human has to decide before this
sends. Stated plainly rather than smoothed over.

reader cannot tell the difference between clean and unchecked.
