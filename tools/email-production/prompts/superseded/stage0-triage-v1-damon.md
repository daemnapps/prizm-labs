Four answers. Everything downstream binds to them, so be decisive and say
where you were unsure rather than hedging in the answer itself.

**LANE:** `ALREADY AN AD` or `ORGANIC`

The test is **not polish**. Every marketing email is polished. The test is
whether this email was **built to sell inside itself** — whether it carries
the argument that makes the case, or whether it is content with a link at the
end of it.

A sale, a launch, an abandoned-cart, a last-chance: `ALREADY AN AD`. A
newsletter, a how-it-works, a founder's note, a customer's story told for its
own sake: `ORGANIC`.

When genuinely between, answer `ORGANIC`. Building structure that turns out
redundant costs less than assuming structure that was never there.

**FORMAT:** one key from the list above.

What the source **is**, not what we might turn it into. If nothing fits,
answer `unlisted` and describe the shape in one line — never force it into
the nearest key, because every stage after this binds to the key.

**VOICE:** whose mouth this comes out of, and the binding that follows.

Name the speaker, then state the rules a later stage has to obey to keep
sounding like them. Where the lane is organic these are binding, not
stylistic.

**SENDER:** the from-line, and what it licenses.

Not metadata — part of the argument. Answer with which of these it is:

- `BRAND` — the company is speaking
- `PERSON` — a named human is speaking, and they are identifiable
- `PERSON-AT-BRAND` — a named human, speaking for the company
- `SUPPORT` — an operational address; a different kind of trust entirely

Then one line on what that sender **may claim**. A named identifiable human
can say "I used it for three weeks" and stand behind it. A brand cannot. This
line is what stops a later stage writing a first-person claim nobody can own.

Then, briefly:

- **ABOUT** — what it is about
- **DOING** — what it is doing to the reader
- **LENGTH** — roughly how many phone screens it runs
- **IDENTIFIABLE** — is the speaker a real, named, traceable person: yes or no

Give me the four labelled answers first, then the brief lines. Nothing else.
