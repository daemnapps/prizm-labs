# The slot

What the calendar hands production. **A slot is fully specified before anyone
writes a word** — that is what makes writing a mechanical step rather than a
blank page.

A slot is a **move in a sequence**, never a standalone send. It knows what it
earns, what it sets up, and what has to follow it.

**The unit of planning is the cell, not the day** (RULED, the owner, 2026-08-29:
"the email-per-day concept is dead"). A cell is segment × avatar × sub-avatar.
The composer's question is never how many emails a day — it is which cells
exist, which are cold, and what each cell is owed. Volume is an output of how
many cells the database carries and how deep each one runs.

## What fully specifies one

| Field | What it is | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| `date` | when it goes | the composer |
| `hour` | what time, and whether local | derived — 17:00 earns most, 13:00 carries most sends |
| `segment` | exactly who receives it | the eight core segments |
| `avatar` | whose language it resolves through — or **none** | the segment, or declared for a broadcast |
| `sub_avatar` | the specific person inside the avatar this speaks to — or **none** | the avatar's sub-avatar roster |
| `category` | which of the five it is made of | the composer, balancing the mix |
| `type` | one of the 45 | the composer, from the category |
| `role` | earns · sets-up · asks · recovers · closes | the type carries it |
| `occasion` | the specific reason it exists now | a cultural moment, a problem, a customer, an offer |
| `product` | what it is about, or explicitly none | the brand's products |
| `offer` | what it may offer, or explicitly none | the offer bank, narrowed |
| `follows` | the slot this one answers, if any | the arc |
| `then` | what this one obliges next | the type's `then` |
| `spent` | ground it may not reuse | the 308-line subject ledger |
| `source` | the swiped email whose shape it takes | the sends library, or a swipe |

## The rules a composed month obeys

**Every ask is earned.** A slot with `role: asks` must have at least one
`earns` slot to the same segment before it. An ask standing alone is an ask
with nothing behind it.

**Every obligation is met.** If a slot's type names a `then`, that follow-up is
scheduled or the slot is not. A question with no answer email was extraction; a
launch with no anticipation arrives cold.

**Nothing repeats spent ground.** The ledger holds 308 subject lines with their
preview text. A slot proposing ground already covered is rejected before it
reaches production.

**Coverage over repetition.** The composer fills toward the cells that have
been cold longest — which sub-avatar has not been spoken to, which pain point
has not been addressed, which type has never been sent.

**Fit before granularity.** A slot cannot target a lifecycle whose language
does not exist. Nine-tenths of customer language is unsegmented, so a slot
claiming to speak to loyal customers specifically is claiming more than the
brand can carry.

**Three streams planned, one held open.** Fixed, sports and seasonal moments
are scheduled. Live ones cannot be — so a month leaves slots deliberately
empty, because the trend sends that worked could not have been on any calendar
written in advance.

## What a slot is not

It does not carry copy, a subject line, or a layout. Those are production's
job, and a calendar that writes them has taken a decision it has no evidence
for — the machine writes every subject line and ranks none.
