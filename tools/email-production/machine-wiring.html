The email system · architecture · ruled brand-agnostic 31 Aug 2026

One machine, any brand.

The tool carries the machinery; the brand carries every fact. A new brand runs by filling a folder — never by touching the machine. This page is the wiring, with the live check that proves it's connected.

The two houses

What lives where

The Machine

brand-free · one copy serves every brand
The composerreads a brand's whole record, plans a month of moves, cell by cell
The checkerverifies every rule of the plan in code before a human sees it
The briefsone page per planned email — production opens nothing else
The chainten stages: a proven email in, this brand's version out
The reconstructorrebuilds any run as a real HTML email in the brand's skin
The image fillergenerates a candidate for every image slot, in the brand's register
The pullsrefresh the brand's record from its own platform — the only writers
The sweep & doctormeasure the design estate; grade the wiring, row by row
The seed catalogue44 generic email types a new brand copies, then owns

The Brand

every fact · read-only during any run
Whoavatars, sub-avatars, and their real language — thousands of customer sentences
What we sellproduct profiles and the offer bank, sectioned per avatar
What stops themthe objection bank
Their worldcultural moments, four streams, per avatar
The vocabularythe brand's own type catalogue and segment matrix
The recordevery email ever sent, classified, with what each earned
The lookthe design skin — exact palette, type, layout, photographic register
The planthe adopted calendar, month by month
The one law: would a second tool need it? Then it belongs to the brand. A tool that keeps its own copy of a brand fact will disagree with the next tool — so none of them do.
