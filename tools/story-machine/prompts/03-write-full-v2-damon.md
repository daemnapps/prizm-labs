# Stage 3 — Write the Full Story (v2)

The long version. On the reference channel the Shorts are the bait and the
"[FULL STORY]" videos are where watch time and ad revenue pile up.
Organic lane only.

**v2 (6 Oct 2026): no length limit.** v1 targeted 2,600–3,000 words; the
story now runs as long as its rounds need.

---

You are expanding a story narration into the full version, with more rounds
and more of the aftermath.

## What you are given

- `{short}` — the finished Stage 2 narration
- `{seed}` — the seed it came from
- `{voice}` — the narrator

## How the full version is built

The Short is the spine. The full version keeps every beat of it and adds
**rounds** — the story doesn't end at the first payoff; the villain strikes
back.

| Part | | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Cold open** | 1 | The Short's title line + the single worst moment, told fast. Then: rewind. |
| **Life before** | 2 | Who they were, what they gave up, what they were promised. Specific money, years, sacrifices. |
| **The discovery** | 3 | How they found out. A chance encounter, a letter, an overheard call. |
| **Round 1** | 4 | They confront. The villain doesn't apologise — they double down. |
| **Round 2** | 5 | It gets worse: other people take the villain's side, a lie spreads, something is taken. |
| **The turn** | 6 | Proof surfaces, an ally appears, the quiet one speaks. |
| **Round 3 — the reckoning** | 7 | Public, specific, earned. Consequences land on the villain. |
| **After** | 8 | Months later. What they rebuilt. The villain's attempt to come back, refused. |
| **Sign-off** | 9 | One dry line to the viewer ("If you stayed until the end, I assume curiosity got the best of you.") |

## Rules

1. Everything from Stage 2's rules still applies: spoken, concrete, dialogue,
   no and-then, show reactions.
2. **Each round ends on an open question** that the next round answers.
   A viewer should never reach a natural stopping point before the end.
3. **The villain gets real lines.** A villain who only does bad things is
   flat; a villain who explains themselves ("lots of parents support their
   kids' dreams") makes the viewer furious.
4. **Money, dates and places stay consistent** with the Short.
5. Fiction. No brand, no product, no claim, no advice. Nothing graphic
   involving children; no sexual content involving minors in any form.
