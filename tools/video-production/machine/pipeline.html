Variation

9 stages × every format

The multiplier. One approved film, restyled into any visual format — screenplay, blocking and dialogue frozen, only the rendering swapped. The restyle step is mechanical by design, because a model asked to restyle re-plans the film.

v0 Style gatev1 Style formulav2 Convert assetsv2g Generate converted assetsv3 Restyle the promptsv4 Generatev5 Variation QCv5r Re-rollv6 Assemble

Two axes, not one

A variation changes how the film looks. That is the cheap, unlimited axis — the expensive work of deciding and proving the film is already paid for.

Changing the avatar, the problem or the angle changes what the film says. That is a new production, back through the brief with different inputs. Both scale; conflating them is how a variation quietly becomes a remake.
