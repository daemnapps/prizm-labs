## What got thrown out, and why

Four clips scored well on text and would have shipped. Each was caught by
looking at the actual frame.

| Rejected | Why |
|---|---|
| "Close-up of a player's neck showing large dark bumps" | **NBA broadcast footage.** Not ours, not licensable. |
| A real device box in real hands | a screen-recorded repost with someone else's caption and the platform UI burnt in |
| A man holding the device, talking | mid-word. Under a voiceover a speaking mouth reads as a muted talking head. |
| Every one of the esthetician's 28 clips | a `RECORDED ON BIGVU` teleprompter badge, bottom right — cropped out, not blurred |

**Three cutaways were dropped entirely rather than faked.** They wanted a named
competitor product in a hand. The library doesn't own them, and a generator
letters anything it's asked to letter with garbage. A brand name belongs in her
mouth, not in the frame — her delivery carries those lines and the picture stays
on her face.

**Where product has to be legible, the brand's own photography does it.** The
fix for "I still don't see our logos on the bottles" was never a better prompt.
