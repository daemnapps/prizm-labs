# Set plates — v1 (2026-09-23)

The prompts `machine/make_set.py` sends. Every place starts FROM A REAL
REFERENCE (a real photo or real video frame of a real place) and the
ADJUSTMENTS that make it ours (the owner, 2026-09-23: "ALL elements in this flow
must be processed from a real reference"). The wide plate is an edit of the
real references; each camera angle is an edit of the wide plate, so the room
stays the same room. Plates are EMPTY — no people — and carry no readable
words. Standard: `PRODUCTION-ELEMENTS.md`.

Variables: {place} what the place is, after the adjustments · {adjustments}
what we change from the real place · {time} time of day · {light} where the
light comes from · {dressing} what must be in the place · {angle} one camera
position · {real} the realism block below.

## realism
Real unretouched phone photograph of a real place: true-to-life materials, wear, slight clutter, mild sensor grain in the shadows, natural colour, nothing styled or staged, no CGI look, no perfect symmetry. No people anywhere in the frame, no hands, no reflections of people. No readable words, signs, labels, logos or numbers anywhere — any sign or packaging is blank or turned away. No watermark, no frame border. Vertical 9:16 framing.

## wide
Establishing photograph of a place built from the real place in image 1 (the other images are the same place or the same kind of place). Keep from the reference: its materials, its wear, its light quality and its ordinary realness. Change, exactly: {adjustments}. The place: {place}. Time of day: {time}. Light: {light}. In the place: {dressing}. The whole space visible from where a person would stand to film it, at chest height. {real}

## angle
The exact same place as image 1 — same surfaces, same objects in the same positions, same light, same time of day — photographed from a different camera position: {angle}. The place: {place}. In the place: {dressing}. {real}
