# Stage 2 · Chain a line / lock an accent — v1

Every line after the base take. Reference the best take(s) — one for normal
lines, the two best stacked when the accent drifts. Never re-describe a
voice from scratch mid-project.

---

@Audio1 and @Audio2 are recordings of the SAME speaker: [PERSONA restated]
with [THE ACCENT, in caps, phonetics named — vowels, final consonants,
prosody] — never [what it must not slide into]. Generate the next recording
in the identical voice and identical accent. [He/She] says: [THE LINE]

---

Escalation ladder for stubborn lines (proven): "match it EXACTLY" → "IS the
same person" → "CRITICAL: EXACT clone". Expect up to ~14 takes on a hard
line.
