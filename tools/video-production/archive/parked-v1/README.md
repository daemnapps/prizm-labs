# PARKED — the describe-then-rebuild approach (2026-09-03)

**the owner's call.** Nothing here is deleted and nothing is wrong in
principle; it is parked because the shape was wrong for the job.

## What this was

A machine that wrote a full production document describing every fact of a
film, assembled an eleven-section prompt per scene from it, and generated
each frame from those words.

## Why it is parked

**The source video already IS the specification.** Every frame exists, with
the framing, blocking, lighting and pacing already solved by someone who
spent money proving it works. Describing all of that in words and rebuilding
it is the expensive way to get a worse result — a 58,000-character brief and
4,000-character frame prompts that contradicted themselves.

What replaces it: **transform the source frames** — clean the plate, swap the
cast against per-character anchors, swap the product — and let the picture
carry everything nobody needs to re-derive. Proven 2026-09-02/03; the
account is in `../method-06-frames-by-edit.md`.

## What is still live and NOT parked

- The teardown chain — still needed, for the WORDS (script, lyrics, beats,
  offer) and the READ (who this speaks to).
- `method-02-identities.md`, `method-04-handoff-and-edit.md`,
  `method-05-format-variation.md`, `method-06-frames-by-edit.md`.
- The sound stages, the variation chain, the format profiles.

## When to unpark

If transforming source frames turns out not to carry a piece — an original
production with no source to transform, most likely — this is the approach
that builds one from nothing. It was never wrong; it was aimed at a job we
do not have yet.
