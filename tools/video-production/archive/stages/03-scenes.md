# Stage 3 — Scenes (multi-reference composition)

Status: **baseline only** — to be built out in detail.

## Job

One still per beat of the script — the exact first frame of the future
clip. Faces and product stay consistent because scenes are composed by
stacking banked images, never by re-describing people.

## Baseline (from the proven run)

- 1–6 stacked references per job: cast images + product photo + wardrobe
  boards, addressed by position ("the woman from the first reference, the
  product from the second").
- Setting, light, and framing written out; identities never re-described.
- Banked stills are durable — reused weeks later across new scenes.
- Optional prep that pays off: studio turnarounds and wardrobe boards per
  character, generated once.

## The slot

Any image model that can: accept multiple reference images in one job and
keep each identity faithful, be addressed by reference, and hold a
product's label and shape exactly.

**Audition:** two known faces + the product in one frame, all three
faithful, label legible.
