**Shape.** One presenter carries the same problem to three consultations in
turn. Each consultation ends in a price card and a fixed cash-register cue —
the anchoring is the argument: a shock-low number, a shock-high number, then
the real number landing small in between. The third "expert" is not a
person — it is the product, presented by the presenter straight to camera
(`<run>-ai-brief.md`: "**THE PRODUCT is Expert 3.** The third 'opinion' is
the scrub itself, presented by NOVA to camera with the tube in hand").

**What a scene contains.** One presenter takes the same problem to several
experts in turn; the price of each answer is the argument, the product is
the last expert.

**The beat shape.** The hook (a four-line card plus a spoken premise,
`<run>-ai-brief.md` Frame 1) → consultation one (a low price) → consultation
two (a high price, and the dissenting "cop out" line that keeps the experts
disagreeing — `injection-v8.md`: "If the experts agree, the video becomes a
repetitive testimonial") → consultation three, which is the product (the
real price, landing small on purpose — `<run>-editor-handoff.md`: "the small
number after two big ones IS the punchline") → an engagement CTA, not a
purchase CTA (`reading-v3.md`: "A format whose final three seconds ask for
an opinion cannot deliver a most-aware viewer, and the injection keeps that
card word for word"). It never slows down for a proof beat — the record's
own awareness read caps this format at a shallow climb: "How many rungs it
carries: one — solution-aware to product-aware" (`reading-v3.md`).

**Stations used.** cast (the presenter and the two experts) · still ·
motion · voice. No restyle noted in the record. No audio station — the
cash-register cue and the graphics are composed at edit, never generated
(`<run>-editor-handoff.md`: "Type is a layer... Never generated").
