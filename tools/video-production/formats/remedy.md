**Shape.** A presenter at a counter shows a remedy from tradition, step by step, then the product as today's version of
it. The words teach and turn; the pictures prove every step and land one gross, unforgettable hook.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Cast** | One presenter from the brand's AI cast (a grandmother, a healer, a founder) with their own voice, life marks and gestures. A format consumes a character, never builds one. |
| **Places** | One continuous set: the presenter's kitchen counter, made once per presenter and every shot edited from it. |
| **Scenes** | 9–13 shots of 3–6 s, one step per line: open (the pain, on the hook) → the old way → why it keeps coming back → today's version (the product) → the result → the ending (the ask). |
| **Sound** | The presenter's voice read line by line (the Model House voice model), fitted to the clip's lips; kitchen room tone under every join. No music bed by default. |

**What a scene contains.** What people used to put on it at home — the kitchen, the jar, the older hands — then today's
version: the product; one step said is one step shown.

**The beat shape.** Hook (the oversized gross model of the problem at the lens, the remedy already pouring onto it) →
the old way → why it fades and comes back → today's version → the result → the ask. It slows down on the payoff: one
close, 5–6 s shot of the result that people replay.

**Stations used.** cast (reuse) · still (set once, then one first frame per shot) · voice · motion (talking clips). No
restyle, no song.

**The references it needs.** The presenter's cast master and look sheet · the set picture · one master picture per prop
at its real size · the real product photo (the product is never drawn) · the previous shot's picture (carry-over).

**The sections that carry the weight.** hook, problem, failed-solutions (the old way that fades), unique-mechanism (why
it works), product (today's version), transformation, call-to-action. The rest are near-boilerplate.

**Ledgers that apply.** Cost per reel (clip seconds, picture count) and the content ledger (topic, hook levers, length,
then views, saves, shares, comments). The swipe-match ledger applies when the script came from the teardown chain.

**Post (edit-time).** Captions in the app's own style (`tools/video-edit` look `edits-glow`, aimed at the neck,
moved off any face or mouth), cuts between shots only (pace `breathing`), match cuts and whips from the shot plan, room
tone under the joins. The model never generates text.

**Gates it adds.** None — `RUN-PROTOCOL.md` carries them. This format leans hardest on three of them: the picture check
(impossible objects, prop size), the clip check (the shot kept its object), and the line check.

**The failure this format invites.** Mechanical: too many short cuts, a voice stretched onto lips, the same excited face
on every shot, a step said but not shown, a prop that changes size. The fix is video spice (`../VIDEO-SPICE.md`) at
level 3 and the playbook `playbooks/remedy.json`.

**The run it was proven on.** The Grandma Yai reels, 2026-10-20 (the reel line in the owner's sandbox, `grandma-yai/`),
edited through `tools/video-edit` as `runs/video-edit/<brand>/remedy-*`.
