# The video rulings: one register for the whole video system

Every ruling the owner has made about how video is made, quoted and dated, in one place. Teardown, writing, production
and edit are audited against this file by the `video-system-sync` skill (`.claude/skills/video-system-sync/`).
A new ruling is added here first, then the audit finds every place in the chains that must change.

How to add one: next free number, the date, his words verbatim, then the rule in one plain line. A ruling that
replaces an older one says so (`replaces V#`) and the older one is marked **superseded**, never deleted.
