# Taste and delivery — the plan (2026-09-18)

Damon: "at the spice level, do we have levels of humor, delivery styles? I want
to get deeper into taste and actual creativity with delivery." And: "at the
teardown this should also be some level of information for the records before
we even get into the rest of the stages."

## What exists today, honestly

The spice pass (4g) sets four things: a cast voice cloned then adjusted, a
lookalike-type character for one B-roll beat, styling from the avatar's world,
and a rhythm per section from the mood chapter. No humor levels, no delivery
styles, no reference world, and no research question about taste or trust.

## The layer, as data in the doctrine

`components/marketing-doctrine/delivery.json` → slice `{delivery}`. Dials with
allowed values; every value says what it does, when it fits, its risk, and the
receipt it needs. No coined names.

| Dial | Values |
|---|---|
| Humor | none · dry · wry · self-deprecating · observational · absurd · broad |
| Delivery style | deadpan · confessional · teacher · rant · storyteller · hype · plain testimonial · deadly sincere · understatement · interview · reaction · hands-and-voiceover |
| Register | staccato-urgent · plain-flat · warm-unhurried · clinical · playful · intimate-low |
| Pacing | fast-open-slow-body · escalating · one-breath · beat-and-pause · list-stack |
| Reference world | filled only by research: who they laugh at · who they trust · what they watch and quote · the visual world · the sound world |
| Avoid | what the room cringes at in an ad; the ad-voice tells to strip |

Three rules. Humor comes from what the room laughs at, never from the writer,
and stays out of proof and offer unless the room jokes there. A reference
directs timing, cadence and world, never a likeness or a voice; the voice is
always a cast voice cloned then adjusted, rights on file. Every dial value
carries a receipt or it is not set.

## Where it runs, in order

| Where | What happens |
|---|---|
| **1c · the doctrine read of the swipe** | THE DELIVERY READ: the source's humor, style, register, pacing, references, avoids — cited to timestamps; into `doctrine.json` and the framework bank (filters: humor, style, register) |
| **research, every run** | five new questions: who they laugh at · who they trust · what they watch and quote · how the room talks · what makes them cringe — answers land in the sub-avatar's bank with receipts (tags: taste, cringe, trust, subculture, in-word) |
| **4g · spice** | per dial, per section: KEEP the source's delivery or SWAP it for the avatar's world, with the receipt; then THE DELIVERY SCRIPT — every line byte-identical, performance direction in brackets |
| **5 · the brief** | each scene carries Delivery beside Technique |
| **production · scene prompts** | the dials go into the performance bracket and the shot |
| **production · director** | reading 8: THE DELIVERY LANDED, with a `delivery_landed` flag |
| **the framework door** | no source to read: the record says so; delivery comes from the avatar's world only |
