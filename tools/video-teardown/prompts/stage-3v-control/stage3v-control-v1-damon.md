# 3v · CONTROL — what this stage does

The Variation video chain starts from a proven ad of ours. Stage 3's job on
the New video chain is to substitute our brand into somebody else's script.
Here there is nothing to substitute: the script is already ours, and it
already won.

So this stage writes the proven ad's own script in stage 3's exact shape, and
every stage that reads the baseline injection reads VERSION 0: the control.

**What it writes, with no model:**

1. **THE INJECTED SCRIPT.** Every line of the verbatim transcript (T1, T2, …),
   timestamp and speaker. The "Source line" and "Our line" columns are the
   same words, because nothing was changed.
2. **WHAT THIS IS.** One paragraph: this is the control, the proven ad
   unchanged, with its proof numbers from the proven gate (0p).
3. **ELEMENT LEDGER.** Every row is `Kept`.
4. **STRUCTURE QUESTIONS FOR DAMON.** None.

**The rules it keeps:**

- Every transcript line is carried. A missing line is a failure, never a
  trim.
- No word is changed, corrected or improved, not even a filler word or a
  slip. The control is what earned the numbers.
