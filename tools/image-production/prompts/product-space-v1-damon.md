# The product space — the scene is made WITHOUT the product

Added to the standing rules of a picture whose product is drawn from its 3D
model (the owner, 2026-10-14: "you need to use the 3d models moving forward for
product because its the foundation of everything now"). The picture model
drew the product wrong every time it was asked to draw it — stood upright on
a round bottom it cannot stand on, the wrong shape, invented parts — so it is
no longer asked. It makes the room; the product is rendered from its kit's 3D
model and put into the spot this rule keeps clear.

Slots: `{what}` the product in plain words (the kit's own `what`), `{holder}`
what it rests in or on when the pose has one, `{spot}` where the spot is.

---

THE PRODUCT IS NOT DRAWN IN THIS PICTURE. Where the direction above mentions
{what}{holder}, it is added afterwards from a real model — so do not draw it,
do not draw anything shaped like it, and do not draw a stand, cradle, case,
box or packaging for it. Keep {spot} as a clear, empty, flat stretch of the
surface it would rest on, in focus and evenly lit, with nothing standing on
it, nothing leaning into it and nothing in front of it. Everything else in the
direction — the room, the light, the other objects — is drawn as asked.
