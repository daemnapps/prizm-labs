# The product boxed — the frame shows the product only as its packaging

For a frame whose product appears as its packaging (retail boxes, mailers,
cartons) — outside the 3D path, because the 3D model is the product itself,
not its box. Found 2026-10-14 on the first chain run with the 3D path: a brief
asking for "FLEX retail boxes mixed through" a pile of mailers came back as
dozens of loose devices drawn by the image model — the exact thing the
ruling (the owner, 2026-10-14) stopped. So here the image model is told the
product itself never appears.

Slot: `{what}` the product in plain words.

---

THE PRODUCT APPEARS ONLY AS CLOSED PACKAGING. Where IMAGE 1 or the direction
shows products, ours are closed, plain, unbranded boxes and mailers — never
{what} itself. {what} is not drawn anywhere in this picture: not out of its
box, not loose, not in a pile, not half-unwrapped, not on any surface. No
picture of the product is printed on a box. If a box would show the product,
it is closed and plain instead.
