# Motion Vocabulary — Damon's visual dictionary

Editing & motion-design terms. Examples on the HTML page are real moments cut from the finished AI Slop video; terms with no moment in AI Slop get a short demo built from our own footage and 3D objects, marked "demo, not in the video".
Times are on the finished video's clock (42.3s: the 3.6s opener, then the 38.7s loop). Clips live in `tools/moves/vocab/clips/`.

## Cuts

_How one shot hands over to the next, with no effect in between._

| Term | What it is | You'd say it like | Where in AI Slop / visual |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hard cut** | One shot stops and the next starts instantly, with nothing in between. | “Hard cut from the falls straight into the next shot, no fade.” | 0:27.9 — one shot straight into the next (`clips/hard-cut.mp4`) |
| **Match cut — shape** | The cut lands so a shape in one shot sits in the exact same spot as a similar shape in the next. | “Match cut the stone him into the stone him — same size, same spot.” | 0:32.7 — stone him into stone him (`clips/match-shape.mp4`) |
| **Match cut — motion** | A movement that starts in one shot carries on in the same direction and speed in the next. | “Swap the outfit mid-move so the gesture carries straight through.” | 0:24.7 — robe into silk shirt, same move (`clips/match-motion.mp4`) |
| **Match cut — colour** | The last colour of one shot is the first colour of the next, so the eye doesn't feel the jump. | “Colour-match out of the purple iris into the purple galaxy.” | 0:06.8 — purple iris into purple galaxy (`clips/match-colour.mp4`) |
| **Jump cut** | Cutting inside the same shot so it skips ahead (or back) in time, and the picture visibly jumps. | “Jump-cut him on the kick — skip a beat of the shot.” | 0:35.1 — skips ahead on the kick (`clips/jump-cut.mp4`) |
| **Smash cut** | A sudden cut from one mood to the opposite — calm to loud, or loud to silent — with no warning. | “Smash cut from the quiet river to his face pulling a tongue at the lens.” | 0:12.9 — calm river into the tongue (`clips/smash-cut.mp4`) |
| **J-cut** | You hear the next shot before you see it: its sound starts early, under the current picture. | “J-cut it — let the crowd noise come in before we cut to the court.” | _demo, not in the video_ — the court's sound starts before the picture cuts (`clips/j-cut.mp4`) |
| **L-cut** | The sound of a shot keeps going after the picture has already cut away to the next one. | “L-cut the falls — keep their sound running over the court.” | _demo, not in the video_ — the falls' sound runs on over the court (`clips/l-cut.mp4`) |
| **Cutaway** | A short shot of something else (what he's looking at, his hands, the place) dropped in, before returning to the main shot. | “Give me a cutaway to his hands, then back to the wide.” | _demo, not in the video_ — main shot, a close shot of his hands, back (`clips/cutaway.mp4`) |
| **Insert / B-roll burst** | Short extra shots dropped in to add texture — details, places, objects — often a fast run of them. | “Hit me with a B-roll burst — quick shots of the city, one per beat.” | 0:10.1–12.9 — the city burst (`clips/insert.mp4`) |
| **Cut on the beat** | Every cut lands exactly on a beat in the music, so you feel the edit in your body. | “Cut on the kick — every change lands on the beat.” | 0:19.8 / 0:20.6 — cuts on the kick (`clips/cut-beat.mp4`) |
| **Flash frame** | A single frame of white (or colour) between two shots — a blink of light on a hit. | “Flash the president in for four frames on the kick.” | 0:19.0 — 4 frames of a persona (a picture, not white) (`clips/flash-frame.mp4`) |

## Transitions

_Moves that carry you from one shot or place into another._

| Term | What it is | You'd say it like | Where in AI Slop / visual |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Whip pan** | The camera swings sideways so fast it blurs, and comes out of the blur somewhere new. | “Whip pan off the monument and land on the falls.” | _demo, not in the video_ — the monument whips to the falls (`clips/whip-pan.mp4`) |
| **Zoom transition** | The camera zooms into or out of something (a screen, an eye) and that carries you into the next picture. | “Suck everything into one point and zoom out of his eye.” | 0:03.6 — into one point, out of his eye (`clips/zoom-transition.mp4`) |
| **Punch-in** | The frame jumps a little closer in one hard step, usually on a beat, then settles. | “Land every cut punched in, then let it settle.” | 0:16.6 — lands close, then settles (`clips/punch-in.mp4`) |
| **Object wipe** | Something passes across the lens and fills the frame, and when it clears we're in a new shot. | “Fly the helmet into the lens and wipe us onto the monument.” | 0:18.1 — the helmet fills the lens (`clips/object-wipe.mp4`) |
| **Object hand-off** | An object leaves one shot through the edge of the frame and arrives in the next shot, so it carries you across. | “Hand the koi off — out of space and into the pond.” | 0:08.5 — the koi, space into the pond (`clips/hand-off.mp4`) |
| **Morph** | One shape or face melts smoothly into another, with no cut. | “Morph the stone statue into him, flesh coming through the stone.” | 0:33.3 — stone becomes him (`clips/morph.mp4`) |
| **Iris transition** | The picture opens or closes through a circle — here, the eye's iris opens into another world. | “Push into his eye and open the iris into space.” | 0:06.0 — space in the iris (`clips/iris.mp4`) |
| **Dissolve** | One shot fades out while the next fades in on top of it, so for a moment you see both. | “Dissolve the phone screen into the opening frame.” | 0:41.6 — the screen dissolves into frame one (`clips/dissolve.mp4`) |
| **Speed-ramp transition** | The shot speeds up hard into the cut, and the next shot comes in fast and slows down. | “Ramp into the cut and ease out the other side.” | 0:06.0–6.8 — speeds up into the cut (`clips/ramp-transition.mp4`) |
| **Invisible cut** | The cut is hidden in a moment where the frame goes fully dark or blocked, so it looks like one continuous shot. | “Hide the cut in the black of his sunglasses so it feels like one take.” | _demo, not in the video_ — the cut hides in the black of his sunglasses (`clips/invisible-cut.mp4`) |

## Time

_Playing with how fast, which way and how often time runs._

| Term | What it is | You'd say it like | Where in AI Slop / visual |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Speed ramp** | The speed changes inside one shot: fast, then suddenly slow on the key moment, then fast again. | “Ramp the Goja shot — backwards, forwards, stop, go.” | 0:03.6–6.8 — Goja, −3.2× to 5.1× (`clips/speed-ramp.mp4`) |
| **Slow motion** | Time runs slower than real life so you can see every detail of a moment. | “Put the forehand in slow-mo so you see the ball leave the racket.” | _demo, not in the video_ — the forehand at real speed, then ¼ speed (`clips/slow-mo.mp4`) |
| **Time freeze / bullet time** | Time almost stops, and the camera keeps moving around the frozen moment. | “Freeze him in stone and let the camera drift round him — bullet time.” | 0:29.5–32.7 — bullet time (`clips/bullet-time.mp4`) |
| **Reverse** | The footage plays backwards. | “Reverse the falls so the water climbs back up.” | 0:26.3–29.5 — played backwards (`clips/reverse.mp4`) |
| **Seamless loop** | The last frame hands straight back into the first, so it plays forever with no visible seam. | “Make the end hand back into the start so it loops forever.” | 0:42.3 → 0:00 — the seam (`clips/loop.mp4`) |
| **Stutter** | The same few frames repeat quickly, so the picture looks like it's skipping or stamping. | “Stutter three frames of him on the kick.” | _demo, not in the video_ — the same three frames, four times over (`clips/stutter.mp4`) |
| **Echo / trails** | A moving thing leaves fading copies of itself behind it. | “Give him an echo trail as he dances.” | _demo, not in the video_ — fading copies of him trail behind (`clips/echo.mp4`) |

## Motion

_How things move inside the frame: the feel of the movement itself._

| Term | What it is | You'd say it like | Where in AI Slop / visual |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Keyframe** | A point where you pin down where something is at a certain moment; the computer fills in the movement between the pins. | “Pin the temple at the lens at both ends and let it travel between.” | 0:18.2–19.7 — the temple, lens to lens (`clips/keyframe.mp4`) |
| **Linear** | Movement at one constant speed from start to stop — feels robotic. | “It feels linear — give it some ease.” | _demo, not in the video_ — the headphones at one speed: evenly spaced dots (`clips/linear.mp4`) |
| **Ease-in** | Starts slow and speeds up — like something being pulled away. | “Ease-in so the bag accelerates out at the lens.” | 0:29.3 — the bag speeds up and leaves (`clips/ease-in.mp4`) |
| **Ease-out** | Starts fast and slows down to a stop — like something landing. | “Ease-out as the basket lands in the shot.” | 0:23.0 — the basket slams in and settles (`clips/ease-out.mp4`) |
| **Ease-in-out** | Starts slow, speeds up in the middle, slows down at the end — the most natural feel. | “Ease-in-out on the headphones so they don't jerk.” | _demo, not in the video_ — the headphones ease in and out: dots bunch at the ends (`clips/ease-in-out.mp4`) |
| **Anticipation** | A small move in the opposite direction just before the main move, like winding up before a throw. | “Give it anticipation — the racket drops back before it swings.” | 0:21.5 — the racket winds back (`clips/anticipation.mp4`) |
| **Overshoot** | Something goes a bit past where it should stop, then settles back — it feels bouncy and alive. | “Pop the headphones in with a little overshoot.” | _demo, not in the video_ — the headphones pop past full size, then settle (`clips/overshoot.mp4`) |
| **Follow-through** | When the main thing stops, the loose parts (hair, a strap, a tail) keep moving for a moment. | “Let the headphones keep swinging after they stop.” | _demo, not in the video_ — the headphones stop; the swing carries on (`clips/follow-through.mp4`) |
| **Motion blur** | Fast movement smears into a streak, the way a real camera sees speed. | “More smear on the disco ball so it feels fast.” | 0:11.7 / 12.4 — the disco ball smears (`clips/motion-blur.mp4`) |
| **Parallax** | Near things slide past faster than far things, which makes a flat picture feel deep. | “As the camera drifts, the railing should slide faster than the falls.” | 0:30.2 — railing vs the falls (`clips/parallax.mp4`) |
| **Camera shake** | The picture jolts as if the camera took the hit. | “Shake the camera on the hit.” | _demo, not in the video_ — the frame jolts on the hit (`clips/camera-shake.mp4`) |
| **Orbit** | The camera circles around the subject while he stays in the middle. | “Orbit him while the objects float.” | 0:35.9 — the objects circle him (`clips/orbit.mp4`) |

## Tracking & compositing

_Putting things into footage so they look like they were always there._

| Term | What it is | You'd say it like | Where in AI Slop / visual |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Motion tracking / match-move** | The software follows a point in real footage so a 3D object can be stuck to it and move with it. | “Track the shot so the headphones ride his selfie like they're really there.” | 0:13.3 — the headphones ride his selfie (`clips/match-move.mp4`) |
| **Rotoscoping / matte** | Cutting a person or object out of the footage, frame by frame; the cut-out shape is called the matte. | “Roto him out so the objects can go behind him.” | _demo, not in the video_ — the footage, his matte, him on a new background (`clips/roto.mp4`) |
| **Keying / chroma key** | Removing one flat colour (usually green) from the picture so you can put anything behind. | “Put him on green and key it out later.” | _demo, not in the video_ — him on green, then the green keyed out (`clips/keying.mp4`) |
| **Compositing / layering** | Stacking separate layers — footage, 3D objects, text — into one finished picture. | “Composite the 3D layer over the footage.” | 0:18.3 — before the layers (left), finished (right) (`clips/compositing.mp4`) |
| **Occlusion** | When something closer blocks something further away — getting it right is what makes a 3D object feel really there. | “The koi should pass behind him, not over him — fix the occlusion.” | _demo, not in the video_ — the koi passes behind him (`clips/occlusion.mp4`) |
| **Colour grade / colour match** | Adjusting colour and contrast for a mood, or so different shots and layers look like they belong together. | “Grade it warmer, with more punch.” | _demo, not in the video_ — before and graded, wiping across (`clips/grade.mp4`) |
| **Depth of field / rack focus** | How much of the picture is sharp; a rack focus moves the sharp spot from one thing to another. | “Shallow focus — only the carousel sharp, the lights behind melting.” | 0:39.1 — only the carousel is sharp (`clips/dof.mp4`) |

## Sound-led

_Picture decisions made by the music._

| Term | What it is | You'd say it like | Where in AI Slop / visual |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hit point** | The exact moment in the music a picture event must land on — a hit, a drop, a snare. | “The ball has to reach the lens right on the hit point.” | 0:23.0 — ball at the lens on the bar (`clips/hit-point.mp4`) |
| **Downbeat** | The first and strongest beat of each bar — the "ONE" in 1-2-3-4. | “Land the big change on the downbeat, not the 3.” | 0:26.3 — new bar, new shot (`clips/downbeat.mp4`) |
| **Sync** | Picture and sound lined up so they happen at exactly the same moment. | “Every skip and every flash lands on a kick — keep it in sync.” | 0:15.1 / 15.5 / 15.7 — on the kicks (`clips/sync.mp4`) |
| **Sound-off design** | Making the video work even with the sound off — captions, clear visuals, the story told in pictures. | “It has to read sound-off — put the words on screen.” | _demo, not in the video_ — the words on screen carry it with no sound (`clips/sound-off.mp4`) |

## Camera

_Where the camera is and how it travels._

| Term | What it is | You'd say it like | Where in AI Slop / visual |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Dolly in / dolly out** | The camera physically moves closer or further — near things grow faster than far ones, unlike a zoom. | “Dolly back off the carousel, don't zoom — let the table open up.” | 0:40.1 — the 3D camera travels back (`clips/dolly.mp4`) |
| **Push-in** | A slow, steady move toward the subject that builds tension or focus. | “Slow push-in on his eye.” | 0:05.6 — into the eye (`clips/push-in.mp4`) |
| **Pull-back reveal** | The camera pulls back to show something we couldn't see — often that the world was smaller or different than we thought. | “Pull back to reveal the whole thing was a toy carousel.” | 0:39.1–41.6 — it was a toy (`clips/pull-back.mp4`) |
| **POV** | Point of view: the camera is someone's eyes, so we see what he sees. | “Give me a POV off the back of the moto — road rushing at us.” | 0:16.6 — riding pillion (`clips/pov.mp4`) |
| **Locked-off** | The camera doesn't move at all — only the subject and the world move inside the frame. | “Locked-off on the footbridge — only he changes.” | 0:23.0 — the footbridge (`clips/locked-off.mp4`) |
