# Stage 7 — put the pictures in

Stage 5 wrote the shot list with `![Scene N](—)` under every scene. Stage 6
made a picture for every scene. This swaps the dash for the picture and saves
the result as `brief-final.md`.

That is the whole job.

Scenes are matched on concept and scene number together — concepts each restart
at Scene 1, so the number on its own would put the wrong picture on the wrong
scene.
