## What carries the value

The crowd. The visible count of strangers with the same trait, gathered in public and posed for a group photo. Nothing is explained, nothing is sold, and no text tells you what to think — the group shot at 10.06s is the whole argument, and the bookends only give you someone to stand next to while you look at it.

## Shoot it

- **Camera:** a phone, vertical, handheld throughout. Bookends at arm's length, held at face height, the natural sway left in. Middle beats shot standing in the crowd at chest height — one wide from far enough back that the *count* of people reads, then two closer frames of people greeting each other. No tripod, no gimbal, no zoom.
- **Light:** whatever the day gives you. This one is flat overcast, sky blown white in the last frame, wet pavement. Do not add light and do not colour-grade it — the daylight is what makes it read as a real event rather than a shoot.
- **Wardrobe:** plain and personal. Dark tee, everyday glasses, the jewellery he already wears. Nobody in this video is styled, including the crowd, and styling anyone would break it.
- **The one hard requirement:** a real gathering with real numbers. This format cannot be faked with five friends — the group frame either shows a crowd or the video has nothing in it.
