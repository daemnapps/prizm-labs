# Captioned screen walkthrough

**1,114,731 views proved it** · 2 of Damon's swipes use this format

## What it is

A hook card on one face beat, then the rest of the runtime is a phone held over a real monitor at an off-axis angle — glare, moiré, the room visible behind the lid — stepping through the tool one screen at a time under a clause of speech caption per beat, and closing on the machine's own output.

## Why it works

Filming the screen rather than capturing it is the credential: the glare and the angle are what make it read as a machine actually doing the work rather than a claim about it. The closing readout is the payoff, so the viewer stays to see what the thing produced.

## The beats, in order

1. one arm's-length face beat under a hook card stating the equation or the promise
2. phone over the laptop: the first screen, the caption naming the step
3. the next screen, tighter, one clause further into the sentence
4. a bare hand enters the frame on the keyboard — the machine is being driven live
5. the output: the result table or the finished thing, almost filling the frame

## On-screen text

a hook card in the top third on the opening beat only, dropped and never brought back; every beat after it carries one clause of speech caption in the same position, each cut off mid-sentence

## Camera

one selfie beat at arm's length, then a phone handheld over a real monitor at an off-axis angle for the rest of the runtime, framed tighter beat by beat; no screen capture, no cutaway
