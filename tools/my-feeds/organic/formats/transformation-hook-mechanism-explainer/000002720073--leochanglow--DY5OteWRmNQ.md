**Shoot it**
Two setups, one phone.
- **Setup A (the cold open):** phone low and very close, at the rim of the bowl, so the water fills the bottom of the frame and the face comes down into it. Shoot the dunk for real and use the moment the head lifts — the drips are the shot.
- **Setup B (everything else):** phone on a tripod at seated eye height, locked, subject centred behind a desk, prop on the desk in shot the entire time. It never moves and never cuts to a different angle. Let the subject hold the mechanism up to camera at least once and touch their own face at least once.
- **Light:** the frames don't show the light source. His face is evenly lit while the window behind him is blown out, so there's something lighting him from the front — a second window, a lamp, or a bounce — but it isn't visible and shouldn't be guessed at. Practically: put a big soft source in front of the subject and let the background window go bright.
- **Wardrobe:** plain olive button-down, buttoned, sleeves down, watch. No branding, nothing that dates it.
- **Room:** the office, skyline and flag are this creator's set dressing, not part of the format. Any real room with a window behind and a surface to put the prop on will do — the format needs a desk, a prop and a locked frame, nothing else.
- **Captions:** burn in auto-captions, three words at a time, one word highlighted. Don't hand-write titles.
