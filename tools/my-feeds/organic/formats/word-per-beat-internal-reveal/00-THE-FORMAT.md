# Word-per-beat internal reveal

**25,202,363 views proved it** · 10 of Damon's swipes use this format

## What it is

One bold word per beat builds a single sentence across a run of fully rendered shots of a body, with the working part painted in or lit up inside it. The camera reframes freely between beats — nothing is locked — and the payoff is an interior view no real camera could ever get.

## Why it works

The unfinished sentence holds the viewer while the render does something footage cannot: shows the inside. Seeing a mechanism from inside the body converts an assertion into something that looks observed.

## The beats, in order

1. word 1 — the exterior of the body part, rendered photoreal
2. word 2 — the camera moves in or through the surface
3. word 3 — the working part is picked out in colour inside
4. word 4 — the mechanism acts, still inside
5. word 5 — the interior payoff shot, glowing or resolved
6. word 6 — held, sentence still unfinished

## On-screen text

one bold word per beat, centre frame, building a single sentence in order; no other text at any point

## Camera

fully rendered — the camera reframes between every beat, moving between angles and travelling into the body; never locked, no hands, no product, no room

## Higgsfield — image prompt

```
vertical 9:16 photoreal render of {body part described exactly}, {the working structure described exactly} picked out in {colour} inside it, clean studio render, even light, no on-screen text
```

## Higgsfield — motion

```
camera moves between angles beat to beat and travels into the interior on the payoff beat; keep the subject identical across every generation (same seed)
```
