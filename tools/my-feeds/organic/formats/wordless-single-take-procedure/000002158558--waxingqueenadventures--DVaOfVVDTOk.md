**What carries the value**
The wax on a place nobody waxes. A thick vivid teal mass spread across a bare human head, then gripped — and the pull you are braced for. No claim, no label, no promise: the material on the body is the entire argument.

**Shoot it**
- **Camera:** phone, vertical, handheld, held close and down at the reclined subject's own level — beside and slightly above the head. It drifts with the hands, it does not sit on a tripod. No cut to a face, no second location, no establishing wide beyond the first beat.
- **Light:** whatever the room already has. Plain even overhead salon light, no fill, no colour. Leave the equipment and the tiled wall in shot — the working room is the credential.
- **Wardrobe:** operator in gloves (a saturated colour that reads against the product), tattoos and all — hands and forearms are the only cast. Subject face-down, face out of frame; keep their top consistent if you want it to read as one session.
- **Product on camera:** the substance must be a colour nothing else in the frame is. Teal against skin, silver bed and white tile is why every frame reads instantly.
- **Rules:** zero captions, zero labels, zero hook line. Open on the untreated site with the hands entering. Tighten as the work goes on. Put every word in the post caption instead.
