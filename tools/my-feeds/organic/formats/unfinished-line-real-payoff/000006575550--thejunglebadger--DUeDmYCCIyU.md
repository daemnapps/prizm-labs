**Shoot it**
- **Camera:** phone, vertical, four setups. (1) Hook: phone almost on the pillow beside the subject, lens at face height, framed so tight the room disappears — hold it near-still, handheld drift only. (2) Product macros: get as close as the phone will focus on the ear, blank wall behind, subject still, no camera movement. (3) Use shot: standing over the bed, subject on their side, mid-shot from the shoulders up. (4) Screen recordings: capture the app on the phone, drop it full-frame or over a wide of the bedroom.
- **Light:** hook is dark — one dim warm source out of frame, most of the picture in shadow. Macros are the opposite: flat even light against a plain wall so the product is the only thing to look at. Use shot is soft daylight-neutral on white bedding. Night bedroom is a single bedside lamp.
- **Wardrobe:** nothing. Bare shoulders and white bedding in the source; anything with a pattern or a logo pulls the eye off the ear. Hair pushed back so the product is not hidden.
- **Rules:** the caption appears once, at the top, and never returns. No talking-head cut. No text over the proof — if a claim needs a caption, it belongs in the app screen or the post caption instead.
