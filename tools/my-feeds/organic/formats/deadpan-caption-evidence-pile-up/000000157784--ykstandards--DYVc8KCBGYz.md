**Shoot it**
You mostly don't shoot this — it's assembled. But if you are shooting your own instances:

- **Camera:** phone, vertical, handheld, no tripod. Every beat is its own self-contained shot with no camera relationship to any other: no matching framing, no establishing shots, no transitions. Cut hard on every beat.
- **Do not match anything.** Different day, different room, different clothes, different time of day, different light. Mixed image quality *helps* — a grainy or letterboxed clip in the middle sells the "found" read. Resist the urge to grade them alike.
- **Light:** whatever that place actually has. Overhead room light, stage light, daylight — never lit for the shot.
- **Wardrobe:** must change between every beat. Same outfit twice reads as one shoot broken up, and the format dies.
- **Length:** six instances in ~17 seconds — roughly 2.5–3s each. No summary, no CTA, no talking head, no cast reacting to camera.
- **The line:** write it once, pin it, never touch it. Plain white sans-serif, centre frame, soft drop shadow. It is a label, not an argument.
