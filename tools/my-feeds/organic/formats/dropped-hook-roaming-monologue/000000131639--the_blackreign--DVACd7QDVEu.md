## What carries the value

The presenter himself as the picture of the life the hook is inviting you into: the wardrobe, the empty golden-hour course, and the proof that he can swing. The single opening line does nothing but name the invitation — everything after it is him being the reason to accept it.

## Shoot it

- **Camera** — phone, vertical, handheld at the subject's own height, square-on. No pushes, no pans, no zooms inside a shot. The variety comes from *moving the phone between beats*, not from moving it during one: close waist-up, then a tighter seated setup, then a full-body wide, then the action shots, then back to close. Every beat is the same person in the same location, so the change of distance is all the edit needs. You need a second person to hold the phone, or a tripod moved between setups.
- **Light** — golden hour, low sun raking in from one side, long shadows on the grass. Shoot with the sun roughly camera-left as here. This is the whole look; a flat midday version of this video does not work.
- **Wardrobe** — one outfit for the entire runtime, unchanged, so every setup reads as one session: a plain knit crew-neck over a collared shirt, a logo cap, dark sunglasses, a watch, and whatever glove or gear the activity actually uses. The gear on the body is what makes him credible in the place.
- **Location** — a real venue for the activity, empty, with an open horizon and a treeline or similar backdrop. Nobody else in frame.
- **Talent** — this needs an on-camera performer who can hold direct-to-lens delivery *and* credibly do the thing. It is not replicable without one.
- **Text** — one line, upper-left, on the opening beat only. Do not bring it back and do not add captions anywhere else.
