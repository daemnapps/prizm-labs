**Shoot it**

- **Camera:** phone on a tripod at seated eye height, roughly 45° off the subject's line of sight. Medium-close: head to mid-chest, subject slightly off-centre with the mic breaking into the bottom-left corner. Shoot wide/horizontal if you want a second angle later, crop vertical. Do not move the camera — no push, no pan, no handheld.
- **The off-camera listener is the trick.** He never looks at the lens. Put a real person just outside frame on the camera-left side and have the subject talk to them. It's what makes it read as a podcast someone is overhearing instead of an ad.
- **Set:** a plain textured wall — vertical wood slats here — and a low couch. Nothing on the walls, nothing on the couch. Put a boom mic into the edge of frame whether or not you're recording through it; it's the signal that says "podcast".
- **Light:** one soft warm key from the front-left at the subject's height, even across the face, gentle falloff on the wall behind. No window, no flare, no coloured practicals.
- **Wardrobe:** plain dark tee, one watch, nothing else. Same clothes for every take — the whole format depends on nothing changing.
- **Text:** the headline card lands on the first beat only and then drops. From there, small white all-caps speech captions low in frame, one broken-off clause at a time. Never let a caption complete a sentence.
