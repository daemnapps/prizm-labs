## What carries the value

The pinned claim itself. "THIS mindset made me a MILLIONAIRE" is a promise with its subject deliberately withheld, held on screen unanswered for the full 36 seconds while the answer exists only in speech. No product, no proof, no demonstration — the unresolved "THIS" is the entire retention device, and the delivery is the only thing selling it.

## Shoot it

- **Camera:** phone front camera, held at arm's length at roughly face height. Close enough that the head fills most of the frame and the top of it is sometimes cropped. Let it drift and reframe between takes — lower and tilted up, then closer and level — but never put it down and never cut away. One room, one subject, no second angle on anything else, no B-roll.
- **Light:** whatever the room has. This one is flat overhead fluorescent with a hard falloff — unflattering, and that is fine. Do not light it.
- **Wardrobe:** plain, everyday, whatever the speaker was already wearing. Nothing styled.
- **Room:** a real working space with something behind him that reads as a place he actually is — a wall, a ceiling, equipment. It is never explained and never established with a wide shot.
- **The line is the whole job.** Write the headline before you shoot: a payoff big enough to be worth 36 seconds, with the cause held back as "THIS". Then talk it out in one unbroken take. This format cannot be rescued in the edit — it needs someone who can hold a lens for half a minute.
- **Not replicable without talent.** There is nothing on screen but a person and a claim.
