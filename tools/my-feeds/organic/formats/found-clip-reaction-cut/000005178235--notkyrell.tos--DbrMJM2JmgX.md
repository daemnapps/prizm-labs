**Shoot it**

- Two locations, one cut. The found clip is not something you shoot — it's something you pick. Choose footage that gets *harder to read* as it runs, not clearer.
- The clip half: handheld phone, vertical, no tripod, no stabiliser. Start at standing height looking into the scene, then physically move in until the camera is inside the action and motion blur takes over. Available room light only — the source here is a normal lived-in room, daylight plus lamps, nothing set up.
- The reaction half: front camera, arm's length, face filling the frame, shot slightly above eye level. Whatever room you're in, plain background, ordinary ceiling light. One unbroken take, no cuts inside it.
- Wardrobe: nothing. Plain t-shirt on the reactor; the found clip's cast wear whatever they were wearing. Styling reads as staged and kills it.
- Ratio: roughly 12 seconds of clip to 4 seconds of face. The cut lands late — that's the whole mechanic. Cut on the loudest moment of the clip, not after it settles.
- No overlays at any point. Put the line in the caption as short all-caps shouts.
- This one needs a person who can land a reaction on camera. It is not replicable with hands-only or product-only footage.
