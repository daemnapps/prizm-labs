**Shoot it**

- **Camera.** Two sources and one hard cut, nothing else. Act one: a clip you did not shoot, full-frame, no overlay, credited in the caption. Act two: your own phone at arm's length or propped, tight chest-up, one continuous take. Do not cut when you break, do not reframe, do not cut back. If the laugh runs eleven seconds, keep eleven seconds.
- **Light.** Whatever is outside. Flat overcast for act one, direct daylight for act two — the two sources not matching is part of how the cut reads.
- **Wardrobe.** Street clothes you already own, nothing branded to the offer. Headphones on camera earn their place: they make it read that you are hearing something the viewer is not yet.
- **Text.** None on screen, ever. The caption carries the whole hook, in one flat line: `I know {subject} is {state} #{tag}`.
- **The casting note.** This does not work with a performed laugh. You need someone who actually breaks at the clip, and you need to be rolling when they do — which usually means rolling before you play it.
