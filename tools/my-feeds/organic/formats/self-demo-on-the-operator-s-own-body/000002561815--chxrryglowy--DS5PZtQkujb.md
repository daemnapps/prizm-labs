# Self-demo on the operator's own body — rebuild sheet

**Why it works**

She offers the worst possible close-up of her own skin first, before anything is applied, so the before-state is volunteered rather than staged — nobody hired that face. The text names the problem twice at the top and then disappears, which leaves the macro skin as the only thing on screen to judge, and the viewer keeps watching to see what it turns into.

**What carries the value**

Her own congested skin at extreme macro. The pore texture is named in the opening frame and then shown again under a slick of product with her own fingers working it — the persuasion is entirely that she put the ugliest version of her own face first.

**Shoot it**

- **Camera:** phone front camera, vertical. Two distances only, alternating: extreme macro (lens close enough that the chin or the nose fills the frame) and a mid selfie at arm's length. No tripod lock required — it's handheld and it varies; what matters is that the macro beats get close enough to read texture.
- **Angles:** the two macro nose beats are shot off-axis and from slightly above/below, not square-on. That awkwardness is what makes it read as caught rather than composed.
- **Light:** soft, even, frontal — no visible hard source, no shadow across the skin. Background goes dark and out of focus behind the face for five of the six beats.
- **Setups:** one dark backdrop for the working beats, and one lighter grey backdrop with a white bow headband and damp hair for the mid beat at 18.89s. That change of setup and headband is the only signal in the frames that time has passed.
- **Wardrobe rules:** plain dark top for the main setup, plain grey-blue top for the second. Nothing patterned, nothing branded, nothing that competes with the skin. Hair down and dry in the first setup, back and damp in the second. Painted nails are in almost every frame and act as a visual constant across the cuts.
- **Skin:** absolutely no retouching, no filter, no smoothing. The whole thing collapses if the opening macro looks corrected.
