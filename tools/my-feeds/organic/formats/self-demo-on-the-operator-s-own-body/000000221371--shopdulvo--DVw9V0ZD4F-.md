## What carries the value

The peeled strip off his own lip, pinched between two fingers and held into the lens as an object. The closing bare frame — smooth, glossy lip, no product in shot — is the after it gets measured against. The tube being held up twice is credit, not proof; the peel is the proof.

## Shoot it

- **Camera:** phone front camera, held at arm's length, vertical. One position for the whole video — same distance, same height, same room. No cutaway, no second location, no tripod move. The only "coverage" is the hand coming toward the lens on the payoff beat.
- **Light:** whatever is overhead in the room. This one is a plain warm ceiling light in a hallway — no ring light, no window setup. Slightly blown highlights on the forehead are correct; it should look like a phone in a corridor.
- **Wardrobe:** one plain dark top, worn for the whole runtime. A hat or beanie is fine and helps hold continuity. Nothing changes between beats.
- **Room:** one narrow interior with something small on the wall behind. It must be the same wall in every beat — the sameness is what makes the lip change readable.
- **Performance:** talk to the lens the whole time, unscripted register. Open on the worst-looking state, don't clean it up first.
- **The one shot you cannot miss:** the removal held up to the lens, in focus, in front of your own face. If the extracted material isn't legible, the video has no payoff.
