# Self-demo on the operator's own body — rebuild sheet

**Why it works**

The line does not make a new claim — it grants one the viewer already accepts (everybody is managing their odor) and then tells them they have been guarding the wrong one, which is an accusation they have to check on themselves rather than an argument they can disagree with. The first thing he does on camera is breathe into his own cupped hand, a private gesture everyone has done, so the before-state is established in the viewer's own memory rather than on screen — which is the only way to sell a payoff that a camera can never show.

**What carries the value**

The pinned line reassigning an anxiety the viewer already has, plus the cupped-hand self-check in the opening beat standing in for a before-state that cannot be filmed. Not the products — none of them is ever held to the lens or named on screen. Nothing visibly changes across the whole runtime, and it does not need to.

**Shoot it**

- **Camera:** one phone, front camera, propped on the counter or a shelf at chest height. It does not move, does not reframe and does not cut for the whole 15 seconds. Frame waist-up with the person slightly off-centre and let the room fill the rest. Do not flip the image — the mirrored shirt print is part of why it reads as someone's own phone rather than a shoot.
- **Light:** daylight from the room's own window only. No ring light, no lamp, no fill. The bathroom's overhead downlights are on and that is all.
- **Wardrobe:** whatever the person actually wears — one plain oversized tee, no styling, no change of clothes. Everything must look like the morning it claims to be.
- **Performance:** none required. No lines to hit, no reactions to sell. The person does their own routine in step order in front of a phone that is already recording — the self-check gesture first, then each item raised to their own face in turn, then they walk out of it. Do not end on a flourish; ending by turning away is what makes it read as caught rather than made.
- **Runtime:** 15 seconds, one take, roughly 2.4s a beat. Nothing is repeated and nothing is explained.
