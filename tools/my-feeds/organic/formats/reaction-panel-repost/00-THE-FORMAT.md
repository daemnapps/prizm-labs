# Reaction-panel repost

**12,018,394 views proved it** · 8 of Damon's swipes use this format

## What it is

Nothing in the post was shot for the post. Someone else's clip carries the whole runtime while a locked reaction panel of the poster sits in the frame the entire time and is never cut to. The reactor's presence, not their performance, is what licenses the borrowed clip.

## Why it works

A real person visibly losing it is the fastest possible proof that the clip below is worth watching. The panel costs nothing to shoot and turns a repost into a post.

## The beats, in order

1. composite opens — borrowed clip plus the reactor panel, pinned punchline caption across the seam
2. the borrowed clip escalates, reactor visibly responds in the panel
3. the borrowed clip's own hook or timer registers
4. the reactor breaks — the panel is the payoff, not the clip
5. the clip resolves under the same unchanged composite

## On-screen text

one pinned punchline caption sitting across the seam for the whole runtime, or the borrowed clip's own burned-in caption; the reactor adds no text of their own

## Camera

a locked vertical split or three-band composite that never changes — the reactor's webcam frame is static, the borrowed clip plays inside its band with whatever camera it came with

## Higgsfield — image prompt

```
vertical 9:16 composite, top band {borrowed clip described exactly}, lower band {reactor described exactly} chest-up at {their fixed set}, framing locked and identical every beat, no on-screen text
```

## Higgsfield — motion

```
reactor band absolutely static (same seed every beat); only the borrowed band advances
```
