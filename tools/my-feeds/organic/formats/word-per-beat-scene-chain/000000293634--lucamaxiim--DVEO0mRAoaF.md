Filed under `new:narrated-rendered-scene-chain`; the structure is the same, but note the worlds here are **shot on real locations with real bystanders**, not rendered.

## Why it works

The line is never finished — every beat hands you two or three more words and then cuts to somewhere completely new, so there is no clean place to leave and no frame you have already seen. And because nothing is shared between the scenes except the man in them, the viewer does the joining themselves: six unrelated places stop reading as a montage and start reading as one person's world.

## What carries the value

The accumulation itself. The same man turns up in one specific real world after another, among people who are plainly not cast — a shop full of waiting men, a street crowd, kids with drums — while one sentence chopped across the cuts makes six unrelated places read as a single continuous thought. There is no product demo, no claim and no argument anywhere in the frames; the brand is present only as a small logo on the shirt the lead is wearing.

## Shoot it

- **Camera:** handheld, vertical, chest height, one continuous take per location. No tripod, no locked frame, no returning "home" shot — every beat is a new place with its own framing and the camera never goes back.
- **Locations:** six of them, visibly unrelated, all real and all in use. The people in the background must be actually there rather than placed — the unpaid crowd is doing half the work.
- **Light:** whatever the location has. Warm lamps indoors, hard midday sun outdoors, overcast on the street. Do not correct between beats; the mismatch is what makes them read as separate worlds.
- **Wardrobe:** one lead in every beat, and the wardrobe is allowed to change between them (polo in three beats, printed tee in another). Keep the brand small — a chest logo or a printed tee, never held up to the lens.
- **Text:** write the whole line first, then cut it into 1-3 word fragments, one per beat. White bold sans, soft drop shadow, no box, dropped wherever that frame happens to be empty — not in a fixed position. No beat may complete a sentence, including the last one sampled.
- **Talent:** this one needs a person. There is no hands-only or product-only version — the recurring lead is the only constant in the piece.
