**Shoot it**

- **Camera** — one phone, vertical, handheld the whole way. Act one at subject height with the second person left in frame; act two close on the contact point, one static-ish shot per zone, cut hard on every change. No tripod, no reframes mid-shot, no cutaway to a face.
- **Locations** — a genuinely public one for act one (outdoor shower, park tap, poolside) and one ordinary street or curb for act two. Do not clean either up: the soda can, the storm drain and the bins are what make it read as caught rather than staged.
- **Light** — plain daylight only, no fill, no reflector. Act one in hard sun; act two flat overcast or open shade is fine. The two acts are allowed to look nothing alike — the mismatch is the cut.
- **Wardrobe** — everyday and unstyled: tank top, shorts, bare feet, sandals kicked off in shot. No brand-matched styling, no fresh-out-of-the-bag look on the product.
- **Talent** — no performance needed. Nobody speaks to camera, nobody has to act. One pair of hands, one body, and a second person who is simply present.
- **Text** — nothing persuasive at any point. If you carry a disclaimer, pin it in one position from the cut into act two and never change it.
