**What carries the value**

The reach. A strap pulled taut across a back nobody can otherwise get to, then run across zone after zone on one body, with the lather as the visible proof of coverage. The witness's flat reaction to the absurd tool in act one is what buys the attention the run then spends.

**Shoot it**

- **Two people, one phone, no tripod.** Person A is the body, person B is both the witness in act one and the pair of hands in the second beat. Neither says anything on camera.
- **Camera:** handheld at the subject's own height. Act one is a wide holding both people and the setting. From there the camera closes in and physically walks around the subject — behind him, down at the calf, round to the other side. Cut on every new zone, never inside one.
- **Light:** flat overcast daylight outdoors. No lamps, no reflector, no colour grade. Shoot in a real driveway, yard or bathroom — somewhere with visible domestic clutter, not a set.
- **Wardrobe:** the demo body is bare above the waist in plain shorts so the coverage is readable. The witness wears ordinary house clothes — t-shirt, shorts, flip-flops. Nothing styled, nothing branded.
- **Act one needs one absurd wrong tool** and one person watching it without laughing. That flat non-reaction is the whole beat; if they mug for the camera it dies.
- **Lather it before every zone** — the foam is what makes coverage visible, so no zone gets shot dry.
- **End on a rinse.** Water over the body washing everything off closes the run without a word.
- **No on-screen text at all** beyond a legal line if your category needs one. No hook, no labels, no captions.
