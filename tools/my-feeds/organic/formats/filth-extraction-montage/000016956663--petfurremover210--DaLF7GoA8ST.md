**What the frames don't show — do not invent these**

- **No audio or dialogue.** Six stills only. Whatever the two men say in the skit is unknown, and the caption ("Flash Sale Ends Soon | Big Discount For First Order Buyers!" plus shopping hashtags) is boilerplate — it tells you nothing structural.
- **The officer's shoes are never shown being cleaned.** You get the dirty before, then the clean after with foam on the road. The act happens between the two sampled beats.
- **No debris.** The parent format usually pays off with a mat of extracted gunk. Nothing like that appears in any frame here.
- **No finished result on any of the four hands-only shoes.** Every one is caught mid-clean; the stills never show one done.
- **No end card and no rinse beat.** The video simply ends on the neon pair, as far as the sampled beats go.
- Six stills across 55 seconds can't rule out text appearing between them, but nothing typed appears at any sampled beat.

**What carries the value**

The range, not the debris. One bottle on four materials — smooth leather, cream nubuck, canvas, neon leather — in four rooms, back to back, with the dirt left visible in frame every time and a red arrow pointing at the last stain in case you missed it. The skit gets the stop; the repetition does the persuading.

**Shoot it**

- **The opener needs two people.** This is the one part you can't do alone — a lead and a reacting second person in a confined outdoor location, plus whatever the dirty item is on one of them. Rebuild the *shape* (a stranger reacts, the fix is the punchline) rather than copying the cop; the costume is not the mechanic.
- **The four demo beats are hands only.** Bare forearms, no face, one small piece of jewellery is fine. Shoot each one over your own hands, phone held roughly overhead, and let it breathe — no tripod, no zoom.
- **One cut per surface.** Four beats, four different rooms, same tool visible in every one. Different location each time is the whole point; two beats in the same room reads as one demo, not a run.
- **Light:** whatever the room has. Overcast outdoor daylight for the opener, window light for the interiors. No lamps, no flash, no colour grade — the phone-camera look is the credibility.
- **Keep the mess in shot.** Dirty sole facing camera, stained toe in frame, the grubby second item sitting in the background of its own beat. Nothing styled, nothing tidied.
- **No typed text.** Resist the urge to caption it. One red arrow on the final beat, pointing at the worst flaw, is the entire graphic budget.
