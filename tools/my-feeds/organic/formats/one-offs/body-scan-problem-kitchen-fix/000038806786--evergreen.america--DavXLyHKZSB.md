**Why it works**
It opens *inside the body* on a problem you can't normally see, so the scroll stops on a state rather than a product. Everything after is a payoff loop the viewer completes themselves — real hands, a real pot, then the same interior view repaired — so the claim is made entirely in pictures and never has to be said out loud.

**What carries the value**
The two anatomy renders, and specifically the gap between them: clogged and dripping at the top, gold and clear at the bottom. The cook is not the payoff — it is the credibility that makes the second render feel earned. Strip the renders out and this is a recipe video with no reason to finish it.

Three things the frames do not settle, said plainly:
- Six stills of a 29.2s video show no cuts between beats, no audio, no voiceover, and no captions that may appear and disappear between these timestamps. Beat order is certain; cut count is not.
- The camera in frame at 7.01s could be deliberate "we really filmed this" set dressing or a generation artifact. The stills cannot tell you which.
- The caption itself walks the visual back — *"While it won't instantly 'wipe away' gunk like a 3D animation"*. The video makes a claim the words then hedge. If you rebuild this for a brand with regulated claims, that gap is your problem to solve before you shoot, not after.

**Shoot it**
- **Camera** — phone, vertical, four real-world beats only. Beat 2 at board height, hands entering from the left, camera close enough that the face never gets in. Beat 3 straight down into the pot, close enough that steam reads. Beat 4 locked on the glass at counter height, nothing else moving. Beat 6 a medium at seated eye-level. No pans, no zooms.
- **Light** — window light or a warm practical, always from behind or to the side of the subject so steam catches. The kitchen is warm and slightly dim; the closing bedroom is bright and blown-out at the window. That contrast is doing work — keep it.
- **Wardrobe** — plain, no logos, sleeves rolled. Dark neutral for the working hands, off-white or cream for the person drinking. Nobody speaks and nobody looks at the camera.
- **The two renders you cannot shoot on a phone** — buy, license, or generate them. They must be the same body, same style, same angle family, and read as one before and one after. If you can only have one, keep the opener.
