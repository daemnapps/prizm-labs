**What carries the value**

The gap between the two worlds. Not the tool, not the render, not the person — the fact that the edit only ever shows the job as a screen in the dark, and shows the operator in the sun. The client request card is the credential that makes the gap mean something.

**Shoot it**

You need one good location, a screen, and no performance at all.

- **Camera.** Every beat is a different handheld setup — no tripod, no repeated framing, and no shot where the subject talks to the lens. Lifestyle beats are wide and shot from a distance across the space; the last beat is a POV over your own open laptop looking back at yourself. Let the moving beat actually move: swing past a foreground object so the motion is visible.
- **Light.** Two lighting states and nothing in between. Outside: hard midday sun, hard shadows, no diffusion, no grade. Inside: kill every light so the screen is the only source and the room is genuinely black around it. The contrast between the two is the format — a dim outdoor beat or a lit indoor beat breaks it.
- **The screen beats.** Film the screen with the phone rather than doing a clean screen recording. The bloom, the drift and the slight off-angle are what make it read as caught rather than presented. Do not make the software legible — the point is that the work is opaque and brief.
- **The request.** Build the inbound message as a real mockup on the screen you film: sender name, a date, a location line, an "Important" flag, a two-line ask, an attachment with a filename someone would actually be curious about, and one key word highlighted. This is the only beat carrying words, and it does all the setup.
- **Wardrobe.** Swimwear or nothing much — cap, shorts, no styling. Anyone else in frame stays incidental and never acknowledges the camera.
- **Overlay.** One lowercase two-word fragment on a sunlit beat, centred, white rounded sans with a soft shadow. Do not pin a line and do not escalate one.
- **Caption.** The gate lives here, not on screen: this post ran "comment '3D' for free pdf guide on this!".
