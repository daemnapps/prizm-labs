The spoken delivery alone. One face, one voice, nothing on screen to help them. There is no debris, no glow, no collection, no unfinished sentence — the retention device is entirely in the script and the performance, which means this format only works if the person and the words are genuinely good. **Not replicable without talent.**

- **Location:** open outdoor ground with a plain, uncluttered background — a tree or shrub filling one third, sky for the rest. Nothing in the background asks for attention.
