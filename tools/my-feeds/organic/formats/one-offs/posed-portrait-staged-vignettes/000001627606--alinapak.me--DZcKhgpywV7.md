**Shoot it**

- **Camera:** tripod, locked, chest height, vertical. No push, no pan, no handheld anywhere. Every beat change is a cut to a new pose or a new distance — that cutting is the only movement in the piece.
- **The plate:** one seamless white backdrop with a hard light *behind* the subject, aimed at the backdrop, so it blows to pure white and the subject falls into near-silhouette with a rim on the hair. Keep fill low — the face should be readable but dark. Shoot four setups on this plate: standing medium-wide, seated low in a dark armchair, standing medium, leaning on the chair back. Put one dark leather armchair in the frame and nothing else.
- **The vignettes:** two real rooms, no styling, shot flat and ordinary so the contrast with the plate does the work. One is a desk tableau, front-lit and warm — the only warm frame in the piece, and the one that carries the word. The other is a sofa two-shot in a living room with a second person.
- **Wardrobe:** plain, dark, no pattern, no logo, same top in every beat. The backlight only works if the silhouette is clean.
- **Text:** exactly one word, once, on the desk beat. Nothing else on screen for the whole runtime.
