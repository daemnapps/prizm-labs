**Shoot it**
Phone, vertical, no tripod needed. Four separate short clips, each in a visibly different room — that difference between rooms IS the format, so do not shoot all four in one laundry room. Two of them are just the machine running: stand at machine height, frame the door glass so the product is the only colour moving, hold ten seconds, do not zoom. One is POV from above the washer lid, arms in frame from the bottom, loading the dirtiest item you have — leave the mess on the lid, do not wipe it. One is the mechanism: flat surface, plain wall behind, two hands stretching the product open toward the lens, static camera. No faces, no wardrobe styling — hands and forearms only, plain sleeves or bare, so any pair of hands can carry it. Existing room light in every shot: fluorescent in the shop, window or ceiling light at home. No captions, no titles, no lower thirds — the writing all happens in the post caption.
