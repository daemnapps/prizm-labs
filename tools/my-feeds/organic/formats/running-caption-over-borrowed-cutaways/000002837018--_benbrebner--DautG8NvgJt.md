**Shoot it**

- **Camera:** phone, vertical, handheld throughout. Every beat is a different shot in a different spot — no tripod, no matching framing. Distances vary: one wide-ish establishing shot, two tight faces, one interior. Shoot the talking beats at arm's length, just below eye level.
- **Light:** all natural. Two beats are low golden sun off water, two are open shade under a canopy against hard midday sun, one is near-dark, one is warm interior. Deliberately inconsistent — mixed light is what makes it read as found footage rather than a shoot.
- **Wardrobe:** whatever the activity actually requires — here that's shirtless/wet with dive gear, an open shirt, a plain grey tee. No styling, no change of look for the camera. If wardrobe looks chosen, the format breaks.
- **Text:** small white serif, low centre, same position every beat, one fragment per beat. Fragments are short — one to four words — and the last one is where the sentence lands.
- **The rule that matters:** shoot the footage first for its own sake, write the line after, then cut the fragments onto beats. Footage shot to illustrate the line will look like it, and that's the whole difference.
