There is no product, no overlay, no edit and no performer doing anything — strip the subject out and there is no video left.

**Shoot it**

- **Camera:** phone, vertical, held at the subject's own height — not looking down at it. Set the frame so the subject fills most of it and then do not move: no zoom, no pan, no reframing when it shifts. Hold the shot well past the point it feels finished; the length is the format.
- **Light:** low hard sun from the side, early or late. The long shadow and the rim on the fur are the whole look — flat midday or overcast kills it.
- **Location:** ordinary outdoor ground, unstyled. Dead leaves, dry grass, a path and a bench visible behind. Nothing dressed, nothing cleaned up.
- **Wardrobe / cast:** none — nobody is on camera but the subject.
- **Text:** none on screen. All the words go in the caption.
- **Honest warning:** this one is not replicable on demand. There is no device here to copy other than "point at something remarkable and hold". If your subject isn't inherently arresting for 16 seconds, this structure has nothing to hide behind.
