**Shoot it**

- **Camera:** phone, vertical, chest-up at standing height, one room for the whole runtime. Frontal for every beat except one — the turn-around behind the head, which is the only angle change and the only reason to move. Framing drifts a little between beats; it does not need a tripod.
- **Light:** one window on the far wall, daylight only. Shoot at the same time of day for the before and the after so the two halves match.
- **Wardrobe:** shirtless, or one plain top worn identically in both halves. Nothing that dates the footage or reads as styled — the room stays lived-in, not dressed.
- **The rule that matters:** shoot the before-state first, all of it, then make the change and shoot the rest in the same spot. If the two halves don't match on room and light, the proof stops working.
- **Captions:** burn in one spoken word at a time, all caps, white, centre-low, identical position every beat. Auto-captions set to single-word display do this.
