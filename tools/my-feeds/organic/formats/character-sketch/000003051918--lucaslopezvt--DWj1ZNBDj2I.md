**What carries the value**
The character. The makeup — bald cap, heavy drawn brows, thick mustache — plus a full-body performance to a close lens. There is no product, no demonstration, no reveal and no text in any sampled frame, so the persona and his dialogue do all of the persuading. The frames cannot tell us what he actually says; the audio was not part of this read, so treat the script as unknown.

Because six stills over 82.7 seconds sample only every 13 seconds, a lot of the runtime is unseen. Nothing here rules out cutaways or a second person between the beats — none appeared in what we have.

**Shoot it**
- **Casting first.** This does not work without a performer. Everything else is cheap; the person is the entire budget.
- **Makeup:** bald cap on a clean-shaven head, heavy dark brows drawn well above the natural line, thick dark mustache. It reads as deliberately fake — the seam at the temple is visible in close-up and it does not hurt the video.
- **Wardrobe:** one outfit for the whole runtime — plain navy zip-up hoodie, grey sweatpants, red trainers. Never changes, which is what makes six locations read as one continuous person rather than six sketches.
- **Camera:** a phone at head height, handheld. Alternate two moves only — push in close enough that his hands break the frame edges for the direct-address beats, then drop back to a locked-off or slowly-following wide and let him walk away from you for the travel beats.
- **Light:** all available daylight. Hard midday sun straight on for the exteriors, whatever the corridor's ceiling lights give you indoors, low warm sun on the alley wall for the closer. No lights, no bounce.
- **Locations:** four to six ordinary public spots within walking distance of each other — a park, water if you have it, an unglamorous interior hallway, a wall with something painted on it. Change location every ten to fifteen seconds.
- **Cutting:** hard cuts between performance beats, and at least one cross-dissolve on the way into a wide — that dissolve is visible in the source at 46.3s and gives the piece its one bit of polish.
