**What carries the value**

The gap between a flat résumé card and a truck leaving the ground. Nothing is said, nothing is argued, no face sells it — the claim `Can bench 10,500 lbs / Can take a hit` is settled by the physics on screen. Strip the stat card and this is a stunt clip; strip the stunt and the card is a joke with no punchline. It needs both, and it needs them at the same time, which is why the text never moves.

**Shoot it**

- **Camera.** Phone, vertical, handheld, two positions only: one wide at standing height that holds still and lags the action, and one right at the lens for the pass-by. No zoom, no reframe, no drone look. The sloppiness is load-bearing — it reads as someone filming, not producing.
- **Light.** Open daylight, hard high sun, blue sky, no kit. Shoot midday in an empty grass field or park with a tree line for depth.
- **Wardrobe.** Plain white t-shirt, black shorts, nothing branded, no styling. The face is never the subject and is never held — this works with anyone in frame.
- **Structure.** Open on the payoff, then go back to the run-up, then the launch, then an unhurried aftermath walk-away. Never react to what happened.
- **Text.** Set both blocks before you cut, and do not touch them again. Small white sans-serif, no box, no animation, no emoji.
