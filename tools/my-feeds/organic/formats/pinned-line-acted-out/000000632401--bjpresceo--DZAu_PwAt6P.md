# Pinned line acted out — rebuild sheet

**Why it works**

The pinned line makes a promise the picture has to settle physically, so every second after it lands is the viewer checking the claim against what is in the room. Because the frame is locked and the partner never reacts, the only thing changing on screen is how far the bit has gone — and it goes somewhere no one expected in a kitchen.

**What carries the value**

The bare foot raised into a locked domestic frame directly over the food she has just been seasoning. It pays off the pinned line without a word, and the arms-crossed partner who never reacts is what makes the escalation readable — he is the fixed point everything else moves against.

**Shoot it**

- **Camera:** phone on a tripod or propped on the far side of a counter, chest height, one wide that takes in both people, the room behind them and the food in the foreground. Set it once and never touch it — every beat after the open is the identical frame. One closer front-camera shot for the opening card only.
- **Cast:** two people. One performs and escalates; one stands beside them with arms crossed and does not react, does not move, does not look away from the lens. The non-reaction is half the joke, so cast for someone who can hold it.
- **Light:** plain daylight from a window, no fill, no lamps, no colour. Overcast is ideal — it looks like a Sunday afternoon, which is the whole register.
- **Wardrobe:** house clothes only. A plain knit top, a fleece hoodie, hair as it actually is. Nothing that reads as styled, nothing branded on camera.
- **Set:** do not tidy. Magnets and photos on the fridge, bottles on top of it, a calendar on the wall, the real cooking mess in the foreground. The line only works if the room is plainly someone's actual house.
- **Beat structure:** land the premise card over the closer opening shot, cut once to the locked wide, then build in four steps — nothing → a hint of the thing → the thing raised to camera → the thing being dealt with, held. Each beat has to go one notch further than the last.
