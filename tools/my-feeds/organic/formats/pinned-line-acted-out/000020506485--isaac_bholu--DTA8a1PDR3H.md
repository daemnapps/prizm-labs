**What carries the value**
The escalating physical act. The caption only names the premise; the persuasion is entirely "he actually moved the television onto the furniture." No product, no reveal, no talking — the room does it.

**Shoot it**
- **Camera:** phone on a tripod or propped on a shelf, vertical, standing height, shooting across the room over the sofa. Two setups only, one per act, each absolutely locked — do not reframe between beats within an act. Frame wide enough that the whole room reads; the subject is small in frame.
- **Light:** flat daylight through an unseen window, no lamps, nothing corrected. It should look like a phone in a real room, grain and all.
- **Wardrobe:** whatever someone genuinely lounges in at home — dark tank and shorts, barefoot here. No styling, no colour coordination with the room.
- **Performance:** back to camera or lying down. Nobody has to act, speak or be attractive on camera, which is why this replicates without talent.
- **Structure rule:** act 1 gets ~3 beats of the mild version, then one hard cut to act 2 in a different corner of the same home, with the escalated version already in progress. Never explain the cut.
- **Text:** one line, pinned top-third, identical from frame one to the last frame. If the words change, you've built a different format.
