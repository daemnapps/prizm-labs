**Shoot it**

- **Camera:** phone on a tripod or propped on a shelf, vertical, lens at seated eye height, framing a person behind a desk with a plain wall and a door behind them. Set it once and never touch it — same framing for every beat, no zoom, no reframe, no handheld. Let the performer move in and out of a frame that stays put.
- **Room:** the plainest wall you have. Off-white, a door, a light switch, an office chair edge in the corner. The blankness is the point — nothing competes with the person.
- **Light:** flat, even, ordinary indoor daylight, no fixture in shot, no fill, no colour. It should look like a real spare room, not a set.
- **Wardrobe — this is the whole gag:** correct and pressed from the waist up (button-down shirt, waistcoat, sleeves down), and comically wrong from the waist down. Keep the bottom half hidden behind the desk for the first four beats so the standing shot is the first time anyone sees it.
- **Performance:** one person, no cast, no cutaways, no shot-reverse. Escalate physically beat by beat — gesture, bigger gesture, plea, collapse — and finish by leaving the frame rather than by landing a line. **This one needs a performer.** It cannot be shot around a weak lead; the whole 40 seconds is carried by one person being funny on camera.
- **Text:** burn one line above the frame, top-centre, white on the black bar, plain sans-serif. Write it once and never change it.
