## Shoot it

- **Camera:** phone, vertical, handheld at chest height, square-on to the wall. Reframe by walking a step, not by zooming. One hard side angle in the back half so the product's depth off the wall reads.
- **Light:** whatever the bathroom has. Flat overhead light is correct here — do not add a lamp or a fill. Grain and a slightly blown wall are part of the credibility.
- **Wardrobe:** hands and forearms only. A watch and a sleeve are fine and read as real. No face, no styling, no manicure.
- **Room:** a bathroom actually in use. Real bottles, real toothpaste, a visible outlet, a real counter. Do not clear the surface before shooting — the clutter is the argument.
- **Sequence:** open on the finished, fully loaded state (the best-looking frame first), then go back and show the mount going onto the bare wall, then close on it loaded and in use again.
- **Text:** one line, top left, on the opening beat only. Never bring it back, never add captions.
