# Sticky-line maker demo — rebuild sheet

A 15.6s faceless home demo: one promise pinned on screen the whole time while a piece of doorway hardware gets carried in, mounted, and used — 3,137,889 views, 48,767 likes, by gravityrelease.

**Why it works**

The line never changes, so there is no second thing to read and no talking head asking for permission to keep watching — the viewer re-reads one promise while the footage works as evidence against it. The payoff is a physical act that cannot be faked or argued with: a whole body hanging fully upside down inside a normal doorway, held on a still frame long enough to register as real.

**What carries the value**

The inverted body in an ordinary doorway, held still and long. Not the hardware, not the graphic — the fact that a person is visibly upside down in a room that looks like anyone's hallway. The spine illustration at the end is a small mechanism cue riding on top; it explains, it does not persuade.

**Shoot it**

- **Camera.** Two modes, in this order. Handheld vertical for carrying and mounting — move with the object, let it go soft when it comes close to the lens. Then put the phone on anything that will not move (tripod, shelf, stack of books) for a wide shot that takes in the whole doorway top to floor, and never touch it again. The stillness is what makes the inversion read.
- **Light.** Whatever the room has. Daylight from a window off to one side, no lamps, no fill, no colour grading. It should look like a phone in a hallway.
- **Wardrobe.** Loose sweatpants and a hoodie or loose top — deliberately, because when the body inverts the top falls over the head and the face disappears. No face means no personality to buy into and no casting problem.
- **Rules.** No face in any frame. No dialogue. One text line, top third, identical from frame one to the last frame — do not animate it, do not restate it, do not add a second line. Hold the final wide shot longer than feels comfortable.
