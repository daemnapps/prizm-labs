# Logo stamp, crowd coverage

**64,993 views proved it** · 2 of Damon's swipes use this format

## What it is

A small brand lockup stamps the opening beat and then drops for good; everything after it is handheld coverage of one real event from visibly different setups with no on-screen text, no demonstration and no address to camera. The product is worn, never held up or named, and the crowd around it does the vouching.

## Why it works

A room of people already treating the thing as an arrival is a stronger claim than any line, and it cannot be written. Because nothing is ever demonstrated or explained, there is nothing to disbelieve — the viewer is only shown who is there.

## The beats, in order

1. the brand lockup over a near-black opening frame, then gone for good
2. the wearers move through the room or the street, caught from across it
3. a tight jostled frame — bodies crossing between the lens and the subject
4. the scrum: onlookers with phones and cameras raised around them
5. a wider frame with the rope, the stanchions or the venue in shot

## On-screen text

one small logo lockup on the opening beat only — the brand over a short tagline — and no on-screen text at any point after it

## Camera

roaming handheld coverage of one real event from five or six visibly different setups at head height, pushed in among the bodies; no locked frame, no talking head, no establishing shot
