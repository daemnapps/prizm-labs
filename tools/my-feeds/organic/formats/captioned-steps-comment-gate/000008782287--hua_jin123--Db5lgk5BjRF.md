**Shoot it**

- **Camera.** Phone on a tripod, vertical, chest height, four to six feet back. Two framings only: a standing wide for the messy prep, a seated two-shot at the table for everything else. Do not roam — the stillness is what lets the CGI cuts land.
- **Light.** Flat daylight through windows, nothing added. The porch is doing all the work: white frames, green foliage behind, no lamps, no fill, no colour grade.
- **Wardrobe.** Plain dark high-collar tops on both, no pattern, no jewellery competing. Elder in navy, younger in green — the pairing reads as practitioner and student, and the second person never speaks.
- **Set.** One table, real objects, no styling: ceramic cups, a wooden tray, the burner, the ingredients in their own jars, and the book sitting in frame from the start so the ending isn't a surprise prop.
- **Captions.** Burned-in subtitles of the actual speech, one clause per beat, white with a soft shadow, lower-middle. Fragments, not headlines — start mid-sentence, keep doses in the text.
- **The close.** Hold the object up, cover to camera, and put the trade in the caption, not the video: "Comment {WORD} and I'll send you my {ASSET} — follow me first or I can't reach you."
- **The cutaways.** Two, no more, and always under a line of speech that explains what you're looking at.

Worth knowing: every frame carries a "genaicontent" watermark and the caption is tagged `#syntheticperformer` — the performers here are generated, not filmed. This format needs no talent and no location.
