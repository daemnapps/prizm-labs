The Language Index

When we write copy, it pulls real customer quotes from one engine. The engine is fed every morning and reads the brand's quote folders fresh on every pull, so copy always works from the latest words.

How it works

InHer own words
  • Support tickets
  • Comments on our ads
  • Surveys and store reviews
  • Interviews and panels
  • Forum research per sub-avatar
FedEvery morning, 05:30
  • New quotes pulled in
  • Each filed by avatar, sub-avatar and lane (customer, prospect, lead, churned)
  • Each labelled with her awareness level
PickedThe engine
  • Reads every quote folder fresh on each pull
  • Picks the quotes this stage of the copy needs
  • Every quote keeps its source
WrittenThe copy
  • Briefs and video scripts
  • Ad copy and landing pages
  • Emails and calendar
  • Outlier briefs

How a pull decides

By stageEach step of a chain asks for the kind of quote it needs: the problem, what she tried, the objection, the win.
By who she isAvatar and sub-avatar, so face copy pulls face words and body copy pulls body words.
By awarenessA stage can ask for problem-aware or product-aware lines. Quotes still waiting for a label stay in, ranked just after the labelled ones.
Fresh firstQuotes never used come first, and the ones shown least come before the ones shown most, so every run reads different words.
Proven words kept inUp to a quarter of each pull is quotes already used in an asset. Proven words still work; they just don't crowd out the rest.
Your calls stickA quote you retire or burn never comes back.
