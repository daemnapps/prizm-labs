Language Bank Upgrade

File each line by what the words say, not only by who said it. Put the doctrine's awareness level and a sophistication signal on every row. Then every hook, body and close pulls words spoken from the exact level it's writing for.

The doctrine's own definitions

Verbatim from components/marketing-doctrine/frameworks.json. Every row gets read against the "Knows" column.

LevelKnowsWhere the ad may begin
Unaware"Does not know the desire or the need exists — or will not admit it.""At the person — a portrait, nothing sold in the opening at all."
Problem-aware"Feels a need but has not connected it to any product category.""At the problem — the need, stated and felt, before anything is offered."
Solution-aware"Carries the desire and knows a result is possible, but does not know a product delivers it.""At the desire or the result — the product arrives after the proof."

Sophistication stageThe market has heard
1 · First in the market"Nothing — no one has made this claim here before."
2 · The claim is in the market"The plain claim, now from competitors."
3 · Claims exhausted"Claims pushed to their limit, and it discounts about half of what it hears."

In the doctrine, "the product" means our product. Someone who has tried a competitor knows a product category exists but not ours, so by these definitions they read as solution-aware, not product-aware.

1 · The lane follows the language

Decided in this order; the first match wins.

  1. Known relationship wins. An order on file goes to customers/ (by segment when known); refunded, cancelled or lapsed goes to churned/. Unchanged.
  2. Otherwise the words decide. Words that show they own or use it ("mine came yesterday") go to customers/unsegmented, marked attribution: "assumed". Words that show interest in getting it ("link?", "where do I get this", "how much") go to leads. Everything else (reactions, jokes, opinions, stories) goes to prospects.
  3. The reason is recorded. Every row keeps its id and gets "lane_by": "relationship" | "language".

First use: the 21 comment rows in customers/unsegmented. The joke reactions ("<a joke reaction>", "<another joke reaction>") go to prospects, and anything asking for the product goes to leads. Once the research gatherer can re-pull those two videos' comments with links, the linked rows replace them rather than the unlinked ones being moved.

Post comments: TikTok, Instagram and Facebook first

The gatherer already pulls all three, plus Meta's own ad-comments table. One shape per platform, with the platform's own post id last:

PlatformSource idThe post id is
TikTokpost_tiktok_<account>_<video id>the number in /video/<id>, e.g. post_tiktok_brand-b_7000000000000000001
Instagrampost_instagram_<account>_<shortcode>the code in /p/<code> or /reel/<code>
Facebookpost_facebook_<page>_<post id>the post number in /<page>/posts/<id>
  1. Account names follow your creator rule. Lowercase, with dots and underscores turned into hyphens: jayy_cross0 becomes jayy-cross0, because _ separates the values.
  2. A post running as a Meta ad also carries the ad, as live_meta_<ad id>. One ad usually has a Facebook post and an Instagram post; both carry the same ad id, so every comment rolls up to the ad and its spend.
  3. Every comment keeps its own link, its likes and the date it was pulled.
  4. Our accounts on each platform are listed in the brand's own-accounts file. A comment on any of them is internal.
  1. Internal or external reads off the property. A property on the brand's own-accounts list is internal; anything else is external. No guessing from free text.
  2. Stats per source. Rows, the awareness split and how much has been used, per survey, post and room, so you can see which sources carry hook-grade language.
  3. Joins. A post_ id is the same post the swipe files and teardowns name (#1 = 7000000000000000001), so language sits beside that post's views and saves.
  4. One registry per brand. Every source, its owner (ours or theirs), dates and row count. It's generated, never hand-edited.

Runs after Brand B's id rename (in its own session) finishes: two tools writing the same bank files at once would overwrite each other. post_ and copy_ are new kinds for the Naming Standard.
