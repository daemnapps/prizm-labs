The making of · 08๐๘ · How it was made

Footage in, a loop out.

  1. Real footagePhone clips from the trip — the base of every shot.
  2. TrackingEach clip's camera move is tracked so 3D can sit inside it.
  3. 3D objectsBuilt in Blender with lifelike materials.
  4. AI shotsGPT Image 2.5 stills, Seedance 2.5 motion, Genjutsu motion transfer.
  5. The editCut on the kicks, turns around the subject, jump cuts.
