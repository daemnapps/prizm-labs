Chat is a conversation. A workflow is a machine.

Most people use AI by typing into a chat box. You ask, it answers, and you carry the answer to the next place yourself. We built something else: a line of tools that does the whole job in order, the same way every time, and only stops when it needs your yes. This page lets you try both.

      02 · What's inside

      A workflow is four things a chat doesn't have.

      The AI model is the same kind you already use. What changes is what surrounds it. Here is each part, and the piece of our system that plays it.

      Memory

      The brand folder

      Product, customer, voice, claims we can and can't make. Written once. Every tool reads it before it starts.

      In a chat: you paste it again each time.In ours: one folder per brand, never explained twice.

      The recipe

      A chain of prompts

      The job broken into steps, each with its own written instruction. The output of one step is the input of the next.

      In a chat: one prompt, and you improvise the rest.In ours: 18 stages from video to brief, each one readable on GitHub.

      The keys

      Connections to your tools

      Developers call them APIs. Think of them as keys that let the AI open your apps and do the work there.

      In a chat: you are the copy and paste.In ours: Higgsfield for pictures and video, Meta for ads, Klaviyo for email, Google Drive for files.

      The checks

      Rules it can't skip

      Automatic checks after each step, and hard stops where a person has to say yes.

      In a chat: you are the quality control.In ours: 81 bad frames thrown out in one week, and nothing goes live without a click.

      05 · Where it stops

      A workflow is only as good as its stops.

      Running on its own is the easy part. Knowing when not to is what makes it safe to leave alone.

      It never publishes, sends or spends without a person's go. It never invents a customer quote or a medical claim. If the brand folder doesn't say something, it says "I don't know" instead of guessing. These are written into the prompts as hard stops.
