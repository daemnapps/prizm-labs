Prizm Labs app · Meta

Data deletion.

For the Prizm Labs app on Meta. Operated by Gradient Media LLC. Last updated 1 October 2026.

The Prizm Labs app holds no personal data about Facebook or Instagram users. It only works with the advertising accounts of businesses that shared them with Gradient Media LLC.

To remove a business's data.

  1. Remove the app's access

    In Meta Business Settings, remove Gradient Media LLC as a partner on the ad account, Page, pixel and Instagram account. The app can no longer reach them.

  2. Ask us to delete what we kept

    Email ddixon@gradientmediallc.com with the subject "Prizm Labs data deletion" and the ad account id. Ad ids, names and figures for that account are deleted from our records within 30 days, and we confirm by reply.
