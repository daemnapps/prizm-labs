Prizm Labs app · Meta

Privacy policy.

For the Prizm Labs app on Meta. Operated by Gradient Media LLC. Last updated 1 October 2026.

What the app is.

Prizm Labs is an internal tool. Gradient Media LLC uses it to place advertising for its own brands into Meta ad accounts those brands own and have shared with Gradient Media LLC. It has no public users, no sign-up and no login for anyone outside the business.

What it reads and writes.

  • Ad account, campaign, ad set, ad and creative settings, so ads go into the right place.
  • The brands' own Facebook Pages, Instagram accounts and pixels, so ads run under the right brand.
  • Ad-level delivery and spend figures, so the business can see which of its ads worked.

It uploads the brands' own pictures, videos and ad text, and creates ads in those accounts.

What it does not do.

  • It does not collect, store or process personal data about Facebook or Instagram users.
  • It does not read messages, comments or profiles of people.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer any data obtained from Meta to anyone.

Where data is kept.

Ad ids, names and performance figures are kept in the business's own internal records, used only by Gradient Media LLC and its brands, and deleted when no longer needed. Access keys are kept in an encrypted system keychain and are never published.
