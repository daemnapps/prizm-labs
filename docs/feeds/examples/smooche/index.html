Offer and stack

How the offer is built

  • Quantity breaks, not percentages: Buy 1 · Buy 2 Get 1 Free · Buy 3 Get 2 Free
  • Two sale framings by template: advertorials say "Up to 60% off + FREE gifts"; Shopify says "Summer Sale: 60% OFF + FREE Mystery Gift"
  • Shade-match guarantee, not a generic refund: "if it doesn't blend seamlessly with your tone, you'll get your money back"
  • Anchors are treatments, not competitors: $180, $220, $300, $500 as clinic/injectable prices
  • Scarcity furniture: "89% sold" bars, resetting countdowns, "FINAL 287 BOTTLES"

The stack

  • Shopify, custom theme smooche/main; quiz is a separate app on its own subdomain
  • Pixels: Meta, TikTok, Snapchat, Bing, Taboola, GTM — not a Meta-only advertiser
  • Attribution: Converge (runconverge.com) — server-side, keeps signal on iOS
  • Retention/proof: Klaviyo, Loox reviews, Intercom, Alia
  • Session recording: Microsoft Clarity on every page
  • ?trybe= on ad links is an attribution token, not a split test — variants captured under different tokens were byte-identical apart from the countdown
