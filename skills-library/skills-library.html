What the research found

28 Oct 2026 · sources in the mirror
  • Apple-style pages are a technique, not a template: GSAP ScrollTrigger pinning + a canvas image sequence or scroll-scrubbed video. GSAP is free incl. all plugins; its official skills have 17.4k stars.
  • Supabase: over 60% of new databases are started by AI tools, Claude Code the largest single source (CEO, Jun 2026).
  • Veterans (Census ACS 2024): 15.7M; 70.9% non-Hispanic White; 29.1% aged 75+; Vietnam era 31.3%, post-9/11 29.9%.
  • Supplement claims: FTC 2022 guidance asks for competent and reliable scientific evidence, generally human RCTs; FDA 21 CFR 101.93 sets the structure/function disclaimer.
  • Game features in trading apps: Massachusetts v. Robinhood (confetti, gamified prompts) settled Jan 2024 for $7.5M; SEC's 2023 engagement-practices proposal was withdrawn Jun 2025.
  • Skill limits: Claude reads every skill's description to pick one, and the list gets about 1% of its memory — so the 955-skill library is read on demand through the front door, not all loaded at once.
