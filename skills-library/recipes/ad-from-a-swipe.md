# Ad from a swipe (the core loop: swipe → strip → inject → asset)

**Higgsfield first:** this is what Ad Multiplier and Marketing Studio "ad reference" were built for.

| Stage | Tools | Output on the page |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Swipe | Apify / Firecrawl / Ad Library pull; or he drops the ad | The source ad, its numbers |
| 2. Strip | Higgsfield `video_analysis`; marketingskills `ad-creative`; the team teardown prompts | Structure: hook, beats, proof, offer, CTA — verbatim prompt + output |
| 3. Inject | Our brand's messaging through the language bank (row IDs) | New script on the same structure → **he picks** |
| 4. Asset | Ad Multiplier **recreate** (same structure, our product/cast/market) or **edit** (swap product/person/text in the source); Marketing Studio `ad_reference` / Reference-to-video; Ads Studio for statics | 3–5 versions, 9:16 |
| 5. Check | Frame-by-frame stills; brand + claim check | Contact sheet → **he picks** |
| 6. Bank | Finals to his phone; brief + prompts saved | Banked asset |

Company brands generate on company keys and workspace; personal and side work on his personal Higgsfield workspace. Established chains from the team workspace are read in place, never copied.
