# Start here

Four steps. No coding.

1. **Make your own copy.** On GitHub, press **Use this template** → **Create a
   new repository**, and set it to **Private**. Your brand's information is
   saved in *your* copy only — never in this public one.
2. **Your keys.** Every tool uses your own accounts. The list of keys is in
   `.env.example`; Claude copies it to `.env` for you and fills it in with you
   when a tool first needs a key. `.env` never gets saved to GitHub.
3. **Say: activate Prizm Lab** — open Claude Code in your copy and say it.
   Claude sets up your brand with you, one question at a time (about 20–30
   minutes; you can stop and say **continue onboarding** any time). The
   checklist it follows is in [`brands/_TEMPLATE/ONBOARDING.md`](brands/_TEMPLATE/ONBOARDING.md).
4. **Questions?** Add **admin@gradientmediallc.com** to your repo
   (Settings → Collaborators) and we'll take a look.

**Where your brand lives.** Your brand folder (`brands/<your-brand>/`) is kept
out of this public repo on purpose. In your private copy, "activate Prizm Lab"
switches on saving for your brand folder, so it's backed up in your repo with
everything else.
