How to undo anything

  1. Every change is one commit that names its issue, so git log --grep='<issue id>' finds it, and undoing it touches nothing else.
  2. Undo is always a new commit (git revert), never a reset or a rebase, so your other work and everyone else's stays exactly as it is.
  3. When you undo, the issue reopens with your reason on it. the team won't make that change again without talking to you first.
  4. A move undoes in one commit too, because the code and its readers moved together. Anything written in the new place since is listed on that change's card.
  5. There's no deadline to undo. The Wednesday date is only for objecting before anything else builds on a change.
  6. The trial stops itself. If you undo two of the trial's changes, or a change breaks one of your live runs twice, changes in your components go back to needing your yes first.
