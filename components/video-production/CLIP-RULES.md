# Clip rules — every AI clip plan, before any clip is paid for

Standing rules for every AI brief (owner direction, 2026-11-20, after the first run whose start pictures were finished
3D prints). The chain holds them in `machine/clip_rules.py`; `run.py start … --clips` applies them to every clip plan
right after the camera and motion plan, and the live clip door (`clips_run.run_live`) refuses a plan with any problem
left. Nothing here names a brand, product, person or brief.

| # | Rule | What the chain does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Who speaks is who is in frame.** A talking (lip-synced) call only when each speaker's face is in its start picture; otherwise the call is silent and the line is voice-over in the edit. One person's voice never goes on another person's mouth. | A scene that starts on its own 3D print keeps the brief's `to_camera`, faces and camera (the one-cut rule no longer forces it on camera). The start picture is read; a talking call whose speaker's face is not in it is refused. A silent call whose people are not in its picture loses the marks line that describes them. |
| 2 | **A render gets a render prompt.** A start picture that is a 3D render or diagram with nobody in it gets slow explainer motion, no body, hands, face or words, silent — never the phone-footage or talking-person preamble. A call never mixes a render and live action. | The call's prompt becomes the render prompt (its picture lines and camera and motion plan kept). A render call that talks, or whose shot pictures are live action, is refused. |
| 3 | **The product is the real one, from its casting sheet** (reworded 2026-11-20: the first wording made the planner drop the product instead of sending its sheet). A product line needs the product's casting sheet in the call. | `clip_refs.product_refs` sends the product's casting sheet, its formula and in-use photos and its views on every call whose scenes use it; a product line with no sheet in the call is refused. |
| 4 | **The beats match the picture.** A beat that contradicts the start picture (something already taken off, already poured) is caught at clip-plan time. | The picture read lists any beat the picture contradicts; each is refused by name, to be rewritten or dropped. |
| 5 | **Finished 3D prints are the start pictures.** A brief whose prints already exist uses them, a shot picture for every shot. | `take_prints.py` takes them (nothing generated, nothing spent); later shots' prints ride along as each shot's opening picture, named in the prompt in send order (`shot_pictures`); `bank_from_prints.py` banks the props and places the prints show. A split scene's call carries only the sides its pictures show. |
| 6 | **Nothing is added that is not in the pictures** (2026-11-20, the first finished run: a tube and jars appeared in hands and on tables). No container, tube, jar, bottle, box, label, packaging or product appears, is picked up or is handed over, and nothing is applied that the picture does not show. | Every call carries the line, a dry run included (`NO_ADD_LINE`). |
| 7 | **No words on screen** (same run: the end card's words and arrow were drawn into the clip). Every on-screen word, arrow, button or card is the edit's. | Every sentence asking for typography, a caption, an arrow or a call to tap is taken out of the prompt (a sentence that forbids them stays); a timed line keeps its seconds but no longer quotes the words it is timed to, so the model cannot draw them; every call carries the no-text line (`NO_TEXT_LINE`). |
| 8 | **The speaker lock holds in every shot** (same run: the listener faced the lens and moved her lips to the speaker's voice). | In a talking call, everyone in the start or shot pictures who is not speaking gets "never speaks: lips closed, eyes off the lens, in every shot"; a shot picture that shows no speaker's face while the voice plays is a stop — that shot goes silent in a call of its own, or its picture shows the speaker. |
| 9 | **A render keeps its liquids liquid and its surface on** (same run: droplets turned to fibres; a surface's plates lifted and flew off). | Every render prompt carries `LIQUID_LINE`: liquids soak in or run off; nothing grows, sprouts or forms fibres; nothing lifts, peels, flakes or flies off. |
| 10 | **One paid take per clip** (owner ruling, 2026-11-20: "we need to only have one shot. We cannot be wasting our credits ... the whole purpose of setting all this stuff up really well from the start is so we don't have to do this"). Nothing re-sends a paid call on its own. A failed or flawed clip is reported; the fix goes into the inputs and these rules; a remake happens only on the owner's word. | `takes.py` `ONE_PAID_TAKE` (one take, one try, priced at one, on a press and in the automatic run alike, whatever a setting says); `clips_run.make_picked` makes one call, keeps a take that did not pass flagged `below_criteria` and reports it; no automatic extra round, no backup model, no remake for a changed face, no resend after a content refusal unless the call carries the owner's word (`backup_on_word`, `remake_on_word`, a press on the board, `V2_EXTRA_TAKES`). The clip door never pays twice for the same request (`higgsfield_cli.find`). |
| 11 | **Every call carries the chain's own setup** (owner, 2026-11-20: "all of the AI video prompts always need to have the same setup that's already in the chain"; a storyboard-wide `picture_only` had stripped every reference from every call of a run). Each person's casting headshot and angles, the product's casting sheet with its formula and in-use photos, and the props in the scene go with every call, talking or silent. | `clip_refs.for_call` on every call (a silent call with references goes in reference mode, `seedance.motion`); `picture_only` is per call: on a talking long take it leaves out only the people's sheets, never the product or props. A call whose casting files are missing (all of them, or any of the product's) is refused (`missing_casting`). |
| 12 | **Every product the scene names sends its own two sheets** (owner, 2026-11-20: a cream named in a line was drawn as an invented jar; "you just send the texture sheets in with the product sheets"). Each product the scene's words or beats name is matched to the brand's product bank, and exactly two pictures go: its casting sheet (`model/product-sheet.png`) and its texture sheet (`model/formula/<product>-formula.*`), each named in the prompt. | `clip_refs.named_products` (full name, a pair of words no other product shares, or a product-type word only it has; a word that is part of the scene's own prop is the prop) and `product_refs`; a product word acted in the scene that names no banked product is a stop. |
| 13 | **The direction keeps the brief's never-rules, and a render is asked only what a render can show** (same run: a beat lifted plates off the skin a render was told never to show removed; particles grew into fibres). | The brief's never-rules are written into every prompt; each call's beats are read against them (and, on a render, against what a render can show) once per plan by the chain's judge door; a breaking beat is dropped and said, one that cannot be dropped is a stop. |
| 14 | **The taken prints fit the current direction.** | A scene that uses the product whose pictures show no product, or two of a call's pictures that are the same print, is flagged for a remake on the owner's word. |

| 15 | **The product in a picture is the real one, pasted** (owner, 2026-11-20: the picture model redraws a product's label whatever reference it gets — "look at the labels"). Every picture that shows a product, from any door, goes through `product_paste.py` for each product in it before a clip is made from it. | `take_prints.py` pastes each product the scene uses into every taken print (the print as taken kept in `media/versions/`), recorded as `<picture>.paste.json`; `take_prints.py <run> --paste` pastes prints already taken; the still step keeps its own paste (`run.py`). The checklist's "real product" row is ✗ for a product picture with no paste for its current bytes. |
| 16 | **A call whose inputs changed is a new call.** | `clips_run.stale_calls`: a made call whose picture bytes or prompt changed since its clip (its `inputs` fingerprint, or, before fingerprints, a picture newer than the clip) is set aside and made again on the run's go; an unchanged call keeps its clip. |

**The checklist before every paid clip** (owner, 2026-11-20: "there literally should be a checklist for every single scene and shot before it is generated"). `machine/clip_checklist.py`, run by `run.py start … --clips` after these rules: every picture a call sends (taken or made) answers the chain's fourteen-line picture inventory (THE-CHAIN.md; marketing-chain-checks `review.after_stage`, filed in `qc-log/`), and the clip model's input sheet (model-inputs) is filled per call, with the chain's own rows (each person's casting sheets, each product's casting and texture sheets, props, the camera and motion plan, the never-rules, no text, nothing added). Any ✗ is a plan problem the live clip door refuses; the clip gate reads the same `clips/checklists.json`; the Studio's Production page shows each clip's inputs in send order with its checklist. **Small miss, small fix** (owner, 2026-11-21): a picture-inventory finding at FIX level is a ~ row on the clip's fix list (`clips/fixes.json`, carried into the cut's `edit/EDIT.json` `fixes`) and never stops the paid run; a BLOCKER (wrong person or outfit, wrong product, a deformed body, an invented product) or an unread picture is ✗ and stops it.

**How the picture is read.** The chain's one picture door (`vision_judge.ask`: the owner's Claude plan first, a local
model, the paid door last) looks at each call's start picture and shot pictures once, with the brief's cast ids and the
call's beats: who is in frame, whose face shows, whether it is a render, which beats it contradicts. Each answer is kept
in `<run>/clips/picture-reads.json` by the picture's bytes and the question. A dry run reads nothing (rule 3 only); a
picture that cannot be read is a stop, never a pass.

Tests: `machine/test_clip_rules.py`, `machine/test_camera_motion.py`, `machine/test_take_prints.py`.
