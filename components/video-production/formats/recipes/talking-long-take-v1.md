# Talking ad recipe v1

Drawn from the two talking ads judged good. Their first-take report shows what still caused retakes, and each rule below answers one of those faults.

Keep what worked:
- One approved start picture for all talking scenes.
- Takes up to 15 seconds, with lines grouped.
- One movement reference: a real talking creator from a clean stretch, with no graphics.
- No extra face or expression sheets.
- Voice at a real talking speed.
- No overlay words in the clip direction.

Fixes for what failed:
- Words cut off or repeated: no take under 8 seconds with a full line, and the line fits the take at real speed.
- Warped hands: one hand action per take, fingers apart.
- Objects appearing or vanishing: everything is in the start picture and within reach.
- Actions not played: three beats at most in a take with hands on an object. A multi-step demo becomes separate takes.
- Product label changing: the product stays as still as the action allows, and a real insert covers a close-up.

Status: draft until one new ad passes on it.
