## Write down

1. **camera** — how the phone is held and moves: propped still, or held by a hand (a very subtle drift, a sway, a
   breath), push-ins, pans, tilts, re-framings, and when each happens (seconds into the scene).
2. **gaze** — where the person looks at each moment: the lens, down at their hands, at an object, away. Name the moment.
3. **props** — every object handled: its state at the start (lid on or off, full or empty, open or closed), how it is
   picked up, held, lifted (smooth, quick, careful), opened, used and put down, in order.
4. **continuity** — every ingredient, texture and object that changes: where each one goes and what it becomes (powder
   into a bowl, a slice into a cup, a paste onto a surface). Its texture at each step (dry powder, wet thick paste, thin
   liquid). Nothing may appear or vanish without being moved there.
5. **actions** — every action with the thing it lands on: what is rubbed, tapped, pointed at or applied, and ONTO WHAT
   (onto a model, onto a face, into a bowl).
6. **sound** — every sound effect and when (a drop, a clink, a pour, a tap), the room sound, music if any.
7. **atmosphere** — steam, light changes, reflections, condensation, smoke, dust in the light, and when.
8. **explainer** — any animated graphic (a cut-away, a see-through layer, an arrow, a diagram): what it is drawn ON (the
   object itself turning see-through, or a separate panel), its style (illustrated, glossy 3D, flat), its colours, and
   exactly what it shows happening, step by step.
9. **captions** — the on-screen words' look: font weight, case, colour, outline or box, size, how many words at once,
   and WHERE on the screen (top, middle, bottom; above or below the face).
10. **pace** — how fast the person talks and moves, and how long each action takes.
11. **manner** — HOW things are done, written so it fits ANY version of this scene with different objects: how items are
    lifted (smooth, slow, careful), how things are dropped or poured, where the eyes go during each action, the rhythm
    of the actions against the words. NEVER name the swipe's own objects, ingredients or product here ("lifts each
    item smoothly to chest height, eyes down on it, then back to the lens", never "lifts the jar of red powder").
