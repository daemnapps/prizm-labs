What it borrows and what it never does

  • Taken from the 17.5M swipe: two rival show-offs, one colour for each lair, a line of tiny clones singing back, a touch that changes a prop, a giant gauge, a short spoken hop between lairs.
  • Never taken: any lyric, melody, character, name, costume or show name.
  • Problem found: the cardigan line came from the first avatar sheet, with no customer record behind it.
  • Claim check: only “helps fade” is said. No date is promised. “Results vary.” shows on screen, and her spots stay as drawn on screen.
