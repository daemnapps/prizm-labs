## Coco brief: Treating Brown Spots Wrong Since the Beginning

**For Coco.** One video. Keep the direction, the hooks and the angle, and make it yours.

**Format:** Kitchen-table educator video
**Inspired by:** A calm presenter at a kitchen table cuts open the raw ingredient, then buries it under the processed stuff, to show how a natural recipe got lost and was brought back.
**Primary product:** Brilliance Body Scrub
**Suggested length:** 90–120 seconds
**Sound:** You talk to camera the whole way, with low, calm ambient music under it. Keep the knife through the root and the cream pouring audible.
**Swipe file:** https://drive.google.com/file/d/1cTOuaH3bUdQ-sXZVynqr28kKGXgbhRvR/view

**Core Idea**
You're a teacher at your own table: the scrub started as a turmeric recipe from your grandmother's kitchen, and years of correctors, laser and leg makeup buried it. You literally bury the turmeric in corrector cream, tell her she was never the problem, and bring the recipe back.

**Suggested Hook Directions**
- "What if I told you we've been treating brown spots wrong since the beginning?"
- "This scrub started with turmeric, mixed by hand in my grandma's kitchen."

**Key Story Beats**
1. You open at your table and ask whether we've been treating brown spots wrong, while tubes and jars land in a bin.
2. You show where it started: turmeric, moringa, ginger and chamomile. Skin renews every 28 to 40 days, so what's on top is only half of it.
3. You pour corrector cream over the turmeric until it's gone: the correctors, laser, long sleeves. Then you tell her she was never the problem, her skin just remembers.
4. The tube comes in: the recipe rebuilt, 87% saw spots visibly fade, with no bleaching, no zapping and no covering up.
5. You close on buy 2, get 1 free, plus a free travel pouch, then the guarantee, and ask her to tap the button below.

**Must-Have Shots**
- Corrector cream poured over turmeric until the orange is gone
- The tube shown clearly, label facing out
- Tubes and jars landing in a bin at the open
- Knife through a fresh turmeric root
- The paste rubbed between fingertip and thumb

**Product Points to Include**
Choose the points that best reflect your real experience:
- Sea salt clears the old skin off the top, then turmeric, moringa, ginger and chamomile go to work on the skin underneath.
- Twice a week, one heaping tablespoon.
- "Use it twice a week, consistently. If the spots do not fade, you get every penny back."

**How to Say It**
- Be warm and unhurried, like a teacher at your own kitchen table. Never selling, never hype.
- Slow down on "But you were never the problem."
- Put the weight on "underneath" and "remembers."

## Coco brief: Grandmother's Turmeric vs. the Bleach Shelf

**For Coco.** One video. Keep the direction, the hooks and the angle, and make it yours.

**Format:** Kitchen-table ingredient exposé
**Inspired by:** A fast talking-to-camera exposé where a raw natural ingredient is set against the shop products that ruined it, then won back.
**Primary product:** Brilliance Turmeric Body Scrub
**Suggested length:** 60–90 seconds
**Sound:** You're talking to camera the whole way. The source has a dark, sparse sci-fi-thriller electronic track that only comes in on the transitions and drops away while you talk. Make sure the mortar, the creams hitting the bowl and the water rinsing off can be heard clearly.
**Swipe file:** https://drive.google.com/file/d/1ik1oSoLqoHAaevISMgH9SEalxpJHiEFh/view

**Core Idea**
Your grandmother's turmeric scrub recipe gets set against a shelf full of bleaching spot creams. You show where the root comes from, dump the creams, say the viewer's doubts out loud, then rinse the scrub off clean. It works because you name the villain first and prove the fix with the senses.

**Suggested Hook Directions**
- "How did we turn one of nature's most powerful roots into a shelf full of bleach?"
- "Spot care was no longer care… it became bleach."

**Key Story Beats**
1. You open with the fresh turmeric root and ask how it turned into a shelf full of bleach.
2. You snap and pound the root, and say the recipe started in your grandmother Yai's kitchen. Turmeric tells the spot to stop and doesn't bleach a thing.
3. You tip the bleaching creams into a bowl, then circle a spot that came back in the same place.
4. You pair the turmeric with sea salt and ask whether it would stain yellow or just suck. Then you rinse it off clean and smooth.
5. You admit it's not overnight and show the tube. You close on buy two, get one free, plus a free travel pouch, then the guarantee and the tap.

**Must-Have Shots**
- A full bag of spot creams tipped into a big glass bowl
- Fresh turmeric root snapped and pounded in a stone mortar
- Red-marker circle on a brown spot in last year's hand photo
- Scrub rinsing off the forearm clean, then a close-up of the grains
- The tube held upright, label out, beside the root

**Product Points to Include**
Choose the points that best reflect your real experience:
- Turmeric tells the cells making the spot to stop without bleaching, and sea salt clears the old spotted skin off.
- Twice a week in the shower, a heaping tablespoon each time.
- "Use it twice a week, consistently. If the spots do not fade, you get every penny back."

**How to Say It**
- Short, definite lines, fast at the top, then serious once the creams come out.
- Say "Or would it suck?" flat, with nothing selling it.
- From "It's not overnight" to the end, keep it plain and level, like one honest breath.
- Start the close with the offer and say no dollar figure out loud.
