Why the cardigan keeps showing up

One unsourced line is pasted into every Brand B writing run before the swipe gets a say. Then the system counts its own output as proof.

The loop

1
Born in the first avatar sheet“She wore a cardigan to her granddaughter’s birthday party. In July.” No customer record behind it.
2
Copied into the language bank as her own wordsThe bank gives it no name or source. It sits there twice, once under “The Real Proof”.
3
Made the headline of the brand positionIt’s the pull quote under “The thing nobody in the category will say”.
4
Pasted into every runThe avatar, bank and position files go in whole, 18 to 24 mentions per run in the three clean runs I checked. That happens whatever the swipe is.
5
A rule forces the writer to reach for it“Every hook traces to a verbatim”, and a hook that can’t cite one gets cut. So the writer grabs a vivid line that’s already in the bank.
6
The output comes back as evidenceThe strategy file says the summer cardigan “recurs across six creators”. Its source is our own hook cards. The position file then calls the angle “receipted six times”.

Why the block never stopped it

A hook ledger was added in August after the line hooked four runs out of four. The current video writing prompts don’t read it, and its last row is from 18 August. It only ever covered hooks, so scripts, stories and concepts were never blocked.

What the language layer is doing

It’s plugged in, and it works. It rotates fresh lines each run and marks what’s been used. The cardigan isn’t in it at all. The trouble is what it holds and how little of the prompt it gets.

The 64 is a rough word match, and many of those are off topic. Most of the bank is support tickets and fan comments on creators’ posts, such as “Beyond gorgeous my friend.” There’s no tag for a lived experience, so a writing step can’t ask for one.

So when the writer needs a real moment from her life, the rotating rows barely have any. It takes the one in the fixed files. Real ones do exist, like “I don’t wear dresses or skorts anymore” and “I don’t do photos”, but they’re buried.
