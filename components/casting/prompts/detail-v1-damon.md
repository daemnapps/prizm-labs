# The detail board — step 6, the continuity the face does not carry

Whatever the format leans on that a face does not carry. For a presenter who
is mostly hands, it is **hands** — a wrist that changes between shots is the
tell. One brand's hands were banked separately after three product shots came
back with three different wrists, because the brief described the hand in
words and **words are not an identity**.

**The board outranks the prose.** One character's spec asked for the watch on
the left and the board came back reading right; handedness is the thing a
generator flips without noticing. Match the board, correct the board, never
the shot.
