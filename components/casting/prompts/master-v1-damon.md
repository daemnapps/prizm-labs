# The master sheet — step 4, the person

The one generation that makes a new person. It is a **reference edit**, not a
fresh roll: the baseline frame goes in, the room and the light and the framing
stay, and the person changes.

**Identity comes from an image, never from a paragraph.** A character written
as prose is a different person every roll, which is the exact failure the cast
bank exists to prevent. So the baseline is not decoration and not a style hint
— it is where the identity of the WORLD comes from.

What carries from the frame: the room, the framing, the light, the camera
register, the wardrobe category, so a generated shot cuts against filmed
footage without a visible change of world. **What must never carry is the
person.** Baseline, not likeness.

Lifted verbatim in form from `the team notes/avatar-builder/build.py`
(`master_prompt`), which is the course's own form, proven on the run that
closed a brand's male-authority gap on 2026-09-14. The slots are this
component's; the sentences are not new.

The ORDER is the contract. A slot with nothing in it is dropped entirely —
never sent as an empty line, never filled with a guess.
