# Owner notes

**New corrections go to the chain's one learnings store** (components/run-kit `run_kit/learnings.py`, `runs/learnings/`, since 2026-11-27): every v2 pass is handed the writing station's rules in force on top of its prompt, and a draft that breaks one goes back to the build. The notes below stay as they are and are still read.

What the owner corrected on finished scripts, as rules. Every writing pass reads this file and obeys it over its own habits. New corrections go on the end, newest last, as one plain rule with its date.

- 2026-11-02: A list of steps is not a script. Past the opening, a line that states a mechanism, proof, objection or offer as a fact reads as mechanical. Tell it as a moment.
- 2026-11-02: Show what you describe. Paint the specific thing in the words; a line that only names a thing fails.
- 2026-11-02: Keep the season and setting of the piece. Never carry a stock customer line from another season into it.
- 2026-11-02: Reuse is the enemy. No stock phrase, picture or joke from an earlier run unless this story earns it.
- 2026-11-02: Sentences join. No chopped fragments in a row.
- 2026-11-02: Never say a price in a video, only the offer: "buy 2, get 1 free with the travel pouch". This applies to voice, captions and the end card.
- 2026-11-02: One ad sells one offer. Do not move to a different product or bundle at the close.
- 2026-11-02: No repeated instruction. Say how to use it once, plainly.
- 2026-11-02: The outro is centered in the frame.
- 2026-11-02: The Glow Duo is not sold. Never offer it in an ad. Check an offer is really sold before writing it in.
