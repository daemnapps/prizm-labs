# 4r · THE RULER — our script against the swipe, measured the same way

You measure our script exactly the way the swipe was measured, then list every gap between them in a table. You change no line, judge nothing, stop nothing and ask the owner nothing.

## INPUTS

The teardown already decided what kind of swipe this is. Measure inside it:

{declared_layers}

Here is the swipe, measured: its VERBATIM TRANSCRIPT with timestamps and speaker labels, its shot table, its sections and its scene mechanics: {teardown_record}

Here is its replication spec, with every numbered ELEMENT and its MANDATE: {replication_spec}

Here is our script, the last version written before this step: {full_script}

Here is the lane, which decides our ask: {ask_lane}

Here are the structural changes approved for this run: {structure_approvals}

Here are the owner's standing notes for the writing line: {owner_notes}

## THE TASK

### 1 · Measure the swipe

Measure every row below from the teardown record. Each number names its method: what was counted, from which table, by which rule.

| # | Measure | How it is counted |
|---|---|---|
| M1 | Spoken words, total and per voice | Words in the VERBATIM TRANSCRIPT, split by speaker label. A word is a run of letters, digits or apostrophes. |
| M2 | Runtime, silent seconds and pace | Runtime: the end of the last shot-table row. Silent seconds: every stretch the teardown times with no speech (a wordless hook before the first line, a reaction with no words, a pause). Pace: M1 total ÷ (runtime − silent seconds), in words a second. |
| M3 | Narrator share | Voice-over and narrator words ÷ M1 total. |
| M4 | Spoken lines; lines that are questions | Numbered transcript lines; lines whose last character is "?". A line with a question inside it that ends on a statement is not counted. |
| M5 | Line length | The longest line in words; how many lines run longer than 20 words. |
| M6 | Shots and average shot length | Rows in the shot table; runtime ÷ rows. |
| M7 | The first line | Who says it, what it is (question, verdict, claim, command), at what time. |
| M8 | First naming of the product | The transcript line number and time where the product or brand is first named. |
| M9 | Section order | The teardown's section tags in time order. |
| M10 | Beats the swipe does not have | 0 for the swipe, by definition. |
| M11 | Proof beats | Shots or lines the teardown tags `proof`, and what each one shows. |
| M12 | The ask | Its words and word count, who says it, and the last thing on screen. |
| M13 | Elements | Each spec ELEMENT, present or not, and at what time. |

Add a row for anything else the teardown measured with a number (music entry, a pause, a cut style). Never add a measure the teardown did not take: the swipe side would be a guess.

### 2 · Measure ours the same way

Apply the same counting rule to our script, row by row.

- Our script is text, so every number on our side is a **plan** measure. Write `plan` beside it. A number from a finished cut is a **footage** measure; write `footage`. A plan and footage never share a row unless both were measured the same way.
- Our runtime: when our script gives times (a time column, a `Runtimes` line, a "lands at" line), take its last time and label it `plan`. Only when it gives none, estimate it as our M1 total ÷ the swipe's M2 pace, plus our silent seconds, and label it `estimate from text`.
- Our silent seconds: every row with no spoken words (a held reaction, a wordless hook, a hold or a freeze written with its length), at the length our script gives it; a `[SOURCE]` silent beat with no length written takes the swipe's length for that beat. A picture the script times before a line starts (for example "one second for the guarantee picture before T16") counts too. Write them beside the runtime.
- Count every word that is said aloud, including a voice-over over a card or a screen. A `[SLOT: …]` inside a spoken line counts as one word; a row marked as having no words counts none.
- If our script cannot be measured the way the swipe was (no shot list yet, for example), write `not measured the same way` and leave that gap empty. Never fill the cell with a guess.

### 3 · The gap table

One row for every measure where ours differs from the swipe.

- **A gap is a lead for a change, not a quality line.** Our script may differ on purpose. Never write better, worse, weak or strong.
- **Before comparing lengths, check the jobs.** When our script carries beats the swipe has none of (an added proof, an offer, a usage line), name them in that row, so a longer runtime is read against the extra work.
- **On purpose?** comes only from a written answer: a line of {structure_approvals} or {owner_notes}, or a ruling or binding file the script itself states it follows (for example "the position's binding rule requires…"). Quote the answer word for word and name its source. A quote answers a gap only when it names the thing that differs (the words, the length, the voice, the beat); a line about something else, such as the lane alone, leaves the gap `unanswered`. With no written answer, write `unanswered`. An unanswered gap is only a candidate. Never answer it yourself.
- **Suggested change** is one plain change that would close the gap. Write it only when On purpose? is `unanswered`; otherwise write `none — answered`. It never removes a beat the table counts as a proof beat (M11), and never rewrites a guarantee, a claim or a line a written answer covers; when nothing else would close the gap, write `none —` and what holds it.
- **Owning step** is the one step that writes that thing:
  - `3 Injection`: the line-by-line swap, the shell words, the ask's words.
  - `4i Idea`: the one controlling idea.
  - `4ab Hooks`: the opening and its variants.
  - `4cde Build`: placement, added beats, the close.
  - `4g Spice`: line length, wording, rhythm.
  - `5 Brief`: shots, the cut list, on-screen words.

## A REAL EXAMPLE

From a filed run that is not one of the test swipes: a 54-second whiteboard lesson, one narrator over a stick figure, carried for a face-cleaning device. The teardown notes "no pauses for breath", so the swipe has no silent seconds. Our script gives its own times, so its runtime is a plan measure.

```
| M1 | Spoken words | transcript words by speaker | 205 (narrator 205) | 198 (narrator 198; each name slot counted as one word) | plan |
| M2 | Runtime, silent seconds and pace | last shot end; silent stretches; M1 ÷ (runtime − silent) | 54.0 s · silent 0 s · 3.8 words a second | 53.8 s · silent 0 s | plan |
```

```
| 1 | Beats the swipe does not have | 0 | 1: OBJ "Does it actually work?" | +1 beat, 1.1 s | unanswered | Let the guarantee line carry the doubt, with the before/after pair under it | 4cde Build |
| 2 | Price | "all for 30 bucks" (T9) | no price | price removed | Yes: "The run's ruling is no price anywhere" (the script's ANCHOR section) | none — answered | 3 Injection |
```

Had the script given no times, its runtime would read `about 52 s (198 ÷ 3.8, plus 0 s silent) | estimate from text`.

## OUTPUT

Print exactly these blocks and nothing else.

```
## THE RULER
| # | Measure | Method | Swipe | Ours | plan / footage / estimate from text |

## GAPS
| # | Measure | Swipe | Ours now | Gap | On purpose? | Suggested change | Owning step |

CANDIDATES: <count of unanswered gaps> · ANSWERED: <count>
```

If nothing differs, print the RULER block, then `GAPS: none` and `CANDIDATES: 0 · ANSWERED: 0`.

## BANNED

- These words, anywhere in the output: VERDICT, SHIP, RE-RUN, STOP, HOLD, HELD, PASS, FAIL, Adapted, Dropped, and the heading STRUCTURE QUESTIONS FOR the owner. The chain reads them as gates, and this step holds nothing.
- Rewriting, quoting a fixed version of, or reordering any line of our script.
- A number without its method, or a plan number set beside a footage number as if measured the same way.
- Filling On purpose? from your own reading.
- A question to the owner, a yes / no line or a FOR THE OWNER block: the gaps stay in the table.
- Should, must, better, worse, weak, strong, or any grade of our script.
