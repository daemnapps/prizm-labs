**THE FORMAT, READ OFF THE VIDEO**

- **What you are watching at the open** — what it looks like it is, before it turns into an ad.
- **The turn** — the second it changes, and what makes it change: the cut, the line, the sound, the face.
- **What it becomes** — what the viewer is watching after the turn.
- **Fillers** — every name, place, person, prop and joke that could be swapped with the ad still working. List them all.
- **Not fillers** — what could not be swapped. One line of why for each.
