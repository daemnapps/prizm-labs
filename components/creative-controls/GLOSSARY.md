# The creative glossary

The literature: what practitioners and researchers actually call the moves we make in copy, video and pictures. Every term with a plain definition, the field it comes from, the medium it applies to and how well it was checked. A term the controls already use is marked; the rest are names we had not used yet.

_Source:_ Researched 2026-10-23 on the owner's word ("research the actual naming conventions for creativity across these different domains ... put definitions to all of these things and get ones that I haven't even named"): Goldenberg, Mazursky & Solomon; Phillips & McQuarrie; Forceville; Smith & Yang; Pricken; McQuarrie & Mick; rhetoric; comedy craft; visual art and internet aesthetics; film and editing; platform craft; copywriting craft.
