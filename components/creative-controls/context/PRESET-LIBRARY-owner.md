## Preset Library

The preset layer between formats and writing: Higgsfield's shelves banked brand-free in `components/creative-controls/story_presets.json`, each row marked ours or gap. A run names a preset with `--preset <id>` (or `auto`); every writing pass gets its shape on top and fills it with the brand's research.

Page: built by `components/creative-controls/page/build_preset_page.py`.

## Formats we have nothing for

- Manga / comics — black-and-white or colour manga and comic pages

- Creator dub — a creator's video dubbed with lip sync
- Colour grades (34) — 34 colour grades as a video edit
- Lighting looks (62) — 62 relighting looks as a video edit
- Layer reveal — a hero painted into a scene, layers fade in under a loading bar
- Thumbnails — video covers and thumbnails
- Brand asset creation — logos, kits, mockups, packaging
