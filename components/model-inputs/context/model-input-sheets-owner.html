Rules that work
  • Make the whole scene as ONE still first: the person, her room and the product in the frame. The motion model only moves what the still shows.components/model-inputs/VIDEO-PATH-owner.md
  • The person in the still is built from the cast BASELINE (headshot, look, details, expressions), never the master.components/model-inputs/VIDEO-PATH-owner.md (the owner 2026-10-27); machine/swipe_swap_job.py
  • The swipe video drives only the movement: timing, gesture, expression and camera. The person, the room and the product do not come from it.kling.ai/quickstart/motion-control-user-guide
  • Build the person inside the shot's own look (same pose, light, contact shadows, depth of field, grain); a studio sheet laid in reads as pasted on. The photo look is the swipe's, never cleaner.components/video-production/VIDEO-RULINGS.md V38, V52
  • Match the still's framing to the video's: whole body with whole body, half body with half body, close-up with close-up. Same aspect on both.kling.ai/quickstart/motion-control-user-guide docs.comfy.org/tutorials/partner-nodes/kling/k
  • The still must show every limb the motion needs, arms and hands included, with clear space around the body for large movement.higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/ai-model kling.ai/quickstart/motion-control-user-guide
  • One continuous shot, one person, no cuts, camera movement or obstructions in the reference; if two people are present the larger is used. Cut the swipe at its cuts and run one shot at a time.kling.ai/quickstart/motion-control-user-guide components/video-production/VIDEO-RULINGS.md V39
  • Steady, moderate movement works best. Fast gestures smear in the source and again in the model: blur the source first and check fast hands in QC; use pro for a fast-hands shot.kling.ai/quickstart/motion-control-user-guide components/video-production/VIDEO-RULINGS.md V38 to V41 (18 Oct)
  • A prop is carried only if it is in the scene still, and the maker says face binding drops props, so check the product in every clip: whole, same size, not melted. If it did not survive, the product is laid in from the real photo at the edit.kling.ai/quickstart/motion-control-user-guide components/video-production/SHOT-ROUTES.md
  • The result comes back at the still's own shape; scale it back to 9:16, never crop it.components/video-production/VIDEO-RULINGS.md (18 Oct)
  • Check the face against the baseline before a clip is kept; motion control exists because object swaps kept the original face (9 of 9 swaps kept it, 9 of 9 transfers carried ours).components/video-production/SHOT-ROUTES.md
Avoid
  • Building the still from the master instead of the baseline.components/model-inputs/VIDEO-PATH-owner.md
  • A person built on a grey studio backdrop: it reads pasted on.components/video-production/VIDEO-RULINGS.md V38
  • Cropping the stretched output: the original caption shows at the edge.components/video-production/VIDEO-RULINGS.md (18 Oct)
  • A reference video with cuts, shot changes or camera moves: it can be truncated.kling.ai/quickstart/motion-control-user-guide higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/ai-model
  • Overly fast motion or a subject whose body or head is blocked.kling.ai/quickstart/motion-control-user-guide
  • More than one person in the reference: the largest in frame is used, not the one you meant.kling.ai/quickstart/motion-control-user-guide
  • Expecting our spoken line from motion control: it copies the swipe's mouth and carries no audio of ours.components/video-production/SHOT-ROUTES.md
  • Assuming a prop in the still survives the motion: the maker says face binding drops props; the product is checked in every clip and never drawn.kling.ai/quickstart/motion-control-user-guide components/video-production/SHOT-ROUTES.md
  • Under 3 s of continuous motion in the reference: the run fails and the credits are not refunded.kling.ai/quickstart/motion-control-user-guide

Rules that work
  • Each line is its own read with that shot's delivery as a tag; never one long take for the whole piece.components/video-production/VOICE-CHAIN.md
  • Choose the voice first; a tag only works if the delivery already exists in the voice. A whisper tag fails on a loud voice.elevenlabs.io/docs/best-practices/prompting
  • Match tags to the voice's character: a serious voice does not respond well to playful tags.elevenlabs.io/docs/best-practices/prompting
  • Keep tags short, clear and about emotion, tone, pace or action; describe voice quality so it cannot be read as a sound cue.elevenlabs.io/docs/best-practices/prompting

  • Use ellipses for weight, capitals for emphasis and normal punctuation for rhythm instead of break tags.elevenlabs.io/docs/best-practices/prompting
