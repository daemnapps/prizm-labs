# Complete delivery and revisions

Every graphic-design and video-editing handoff carries `DELIVERY-REQUIREMENTS.json` and `DELIVERY-CHECKLIST.md`. The issuing packer records every required deliverable slot and acceptance criterion before work starts. Undefined scope stays held until the issuing side defines it. A maker does not shorten or rewrite the issued requirements to make a return pass.

**Creative freedom:** the maker may change wording, a hook, a scene, composition or the approach to a variation. Record the change against the slot it fulfills. Keep accurate product/offer facts and fulfill the whole deliverable set. A creative change is not an excuse for a missing version, thumbnail, slide or other requirement. This replaces older handoff instructions that require every creative line and shot to remain verbatim; it does not change factual requirements or approved production routes.

1. Read the issued checklist and the latest `REVISION.md`, if present. Each video slot requires a finished video, a distinct cover and its production record. Each design/slide slot requires its own finished image.
2. Finish and inspect every item. Human quality checks require the reviewer's name, time and evidence; checking a box without reviewing the work is not completion. The machine separately verifies files, hashes, dimensions, playable media, duplicate files, cover pairing and current revision identity.
3. Copy `revisions/<revision-id>/submission.template.json` to `returned/<revision-id>/submission.json`. Fill every item with a handoff-relative filename and SHA-256, every required check with `checked: true`, reviewer, timestamp and evidence, and each creative change. List the image-production provenance files (`run.json`, `covers.json` and recipes) in `production_records` so the return reader can retrieve them. Keep the issued requirement hash and revision identifier intact. Existing usable assets can be referenced from earlier returns, with their hashes; do not delete earlier work.
4. Run `python3 components/briefs/delivery_contract.py check <handoff-folder>`. A failure remains **incomplete with the maker**. It does not become a delivery or enter the owner's approvals queue. Resolve every reported gap, then run `submit <handoff-folder> --dry-run`, followed by `submit <handoff-folder>`.
5. A revision keeps the same brief number and gets a new round: `R01`, `R02`, etc. The queue shows **back for revision** with that identifier. The request states what must be fixed; its submission refers to that exact round. Earlier requests and submissions remain available. A complete resubmission becomes **ready for review** with the revision identifier on its approval card. Old files or a previous round's checked checklist cannot complete a new round.

Physical file uploads can be work in progress. Only the validated submission counts as delivery. No incomplete submission is silently accepted, and no delivery or revision automatically launches an ad.
