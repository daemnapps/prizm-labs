# Labor Day 2026 — retired 2026-09-11

Four production runs whose pictures carry the promo bar

    LABOR DAY — FLEX $69.99 · WAS $99.99 · ENDS TUESDAY

Labor Day was Monday 7 September. The offer ended Tuesday the 8th. On the 11th
twenty-eight of them were sitting in a built Meta import sheet, three days
dead, and nothing in the pipeline had noticed — a picture carries its offer
burned into the image, and until now nothing knew an offer has an end date.

the owner caught it by eye, reviewing the kit: *"we are literally doing a bunch of
duplicates for some avatars"*. They were not duplicates. Seven concepts had
been rendered twice — once with this bar, once with the standing 90-day
guarantee — so the pairs read as waste when one of each pair was simply
expired.

**Nothing here ever reached Drive or the ad account.** The Drive folder holds
exactly the 110 live assets; these 43 were local only.

## The one thing worth recovering

`omar / weforgot / apology` exists **only** with this bar — every other concept
in these runs has a 90-day twin that ships. Re-bar those three pictures to the
guarantee and the concept comes back without a re-shoot.

## What stops it happening again

`../../meta-upload/offers.json` now declares, per brief, what bar its pictures
carry and when that offer dies. `build.py` drops any picture whose offer has
expired and lists it with the reason. `--allow-expired` overrides, for the
deliberate re-run of an old drop.
