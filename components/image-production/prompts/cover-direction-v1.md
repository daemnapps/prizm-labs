# The cover's standing direction, for a request that brings none

A cover request normally carries its own `direction` (the moment, the concept taken from the swipe, the one thing
pushed). A caller that runs with no person in the loop may bring none; the cover job then writes this one, so a cover
is still made from the rule and never from a blank.

The rule it states: a cover starts from the strongest moment of its own video, follows a concept from a real cover its
audience already sees, and pushes one thing far past real so the version's line is shown literally. The line is given
as meaning only; the picture model paints no words (the headline is set afterwards by the tool).

Slots: `{headline}` the version's headline; `{concept}` the sentence naming the move the followed swipe makes, left
out when the swipe index names none.

First draft, 2026-11-12. Not yet compared on real covers.
