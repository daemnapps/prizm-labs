# A cover with no product in frame stays without one

Added to the standing rules of a cover whose request names no product. A product on a cover comes only from its 3D kit
(the request names `product`); the picture model never draws one, even when the video frame or the set print shows it.

---

THE PRODUCT IS NOT IN THIS PICTURE. Do not draw the product, its packaging, its box or anything shaped like it, even
where the attached pictures show it. Fill the place it had with the scene.
