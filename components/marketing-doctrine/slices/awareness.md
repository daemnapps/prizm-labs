# Awareness — the five levels

Bound as `{awareness_levels}`. Source: the doctrine component; refs are line pointers into the book, for verification only.

Each level sits behind a wall from the level next to it. A line written for one level is invisible or stale to the others, so the level decides where the ad is ALLOWED to begin its argument — at the product, at the desire, or at the problem. [L667-972] [L884-896]

## 1. Most Aware (`most-aware`)

- **Knows:** Knows the product, wants it, simply has not bought yet.
- **Must:** State the product and the price or the deal, and stop.
- **Must NOT:** Explain anything; Re-sell a want that is already there
- **Where the ad may begin:** At the product itself — name and terms in the opening beat.
- Ref: [L667-695]

## 2. Product-Aware (`product-aware`)

- **Knows:** Knows the product exists but is not sold on it.
- **Must:** Do one of seven named jobs on the claim already in the market
  - the seven jobs: reinforce, sharpen, extend, document, new mechanism, remove a limitation, reposition
- **Must NOT:** Reintroduce the category from scratch; Talk as if the product were new
- **Where the ad may begin:** At the product, but on the gap between knowing it and wanting it.
- Ref: [L696-772]

## 3. Solution-Aware (`solution-aware`)

- **Knows:** Carries the desire and knows a result is possible, but does not know a product delivers it.
- **Must:** Crystallise the desire or its solution in one line, then prove it
- **Must NOT:** Mention price; Assume the product name means anything yet
- **Where the ad may begin:** At the desire or the result — the product arrives after the proof.
- Ref: [L773-829]

## 4. Problem-Aware (`problem-aware`)

- **Knows:** Feels a need but has not connected it to any product category.
- **Must:** Name the problem or the need directly, in the words the audience uses
- **Must NOT:** Assume a solution is already known; Lead with a category the audience has not entered
- **Where the ad may begin:** At the problem — the need, stated and felt, before anything is offered.
- Ref: [L830-872]

## 5. Unaware (`unaware`)

- **Knows:** Does not know the desire or the need exists — or will not admit it.
- **Must:** Open on pure identification: describe the audience to itself until it is recognised
- **Must NOT:** Mention price; Name the product; State the direct claim or the desire itself
- **Where the ad may begin:** At the person — a portrait, nothing sold in the opening at all.
- Ref: [L873-972]

**Entry is not destination.** The level sets the point of entry, not the destination. Copy opens where the audience already stands and walks them forward one rung at a time. [L2064-2069]

**The opening line.** The opening line has one job: force the next line to be read. It does not sell and does not carry the offer — the single exception is a Most Aware audience being shown a price cut. [L603-645]

**Dramatising it is a separate craft.** Once the content is fixed — the desire plus the entry rung — dramatising it is a separate craft: measure it, compare it, dramatise it, ask it, state it as a paradox. These are phrasings of an idea already chosen, never a substitute for choosing one. [L1673-1846]

Owning stages: 4a-read, 4b-hook, 4e-close
