# Verification — where proof lands

Bound as `{verification}`. Source: the doctrine component; refs are line pointers into the book, for verification only.

The placement and arrangement of proof so it is accepted — proof lands where doubt peaks, not where it is convenient. [L5694-5808]

Three things, related and NOT the same:

- **mechanization** — Logical demonstration — needs no outside facts. [L5694-5808]
- **documentation** — Any external proof: statistics, tests, testimonials, authorities. [L5694-5808]
- **verification** — Where and how that documentation is placed for maximum acceptance. [L5694-5808]

- **placement** — Proof goes where the audience already, unconsciously, demands it — never front-loaded because it is proof. [L5694-5808]
- **proof-is-selling-copy** — Proof copy is written as selling copy — dramatic, specific and short — never as dry fact. [L5694-5808]

- **Owning stage:** 4f-audit, 4e-close
- **Research question:** What independently verifiable proof actually exists, and at which beat of the current draft the demand for proof genuinely peaks.
- **Scene shape:** Proof lands as its own beat — a number, a document, a visible result — exactly where doubt would otherwise creep in; the delta runs claim to confirmed claim.
