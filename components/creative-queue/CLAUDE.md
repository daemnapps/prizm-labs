**The shape** (design: team/handoffs/2026-10-22-shared-queue-backend-bench.md, PLAN's adjudication):
1. Machines write OBSERVATIONS, one writer per source file, each with `as_of`. the owner's Mac writes `the owner-mac`.
2. People write DECISIONS, one append-only log per person per UTC day; git's union merge keeps both sides'
   lines (the repo-root `.gitattributes`, lead-applied with QB-1 (queue core)).
3. `index.json` is a pure function of both: per-field latest-MADE wins by the writer's timestamp; `observed_stage`,
   `requested_stage` and `execution` are kept apart; removal is a tombstone; each source's age is shown.
4. Approval is a decision. A paid or outbound run happens only on a person's press on the machine that holds the
   tool, and writes an `execution` receipt. Nothing here executes anything.

the owner and the email lead can change anything (the developer, decision log 2026-10-22); any role split is a later layer on top.
