# Pacing and sound

Bound as `{cutting}`.

Every cut gives something new: new information, a reaction, or a new size. Cut when the point lands. Sound is planned with the picture, never added as an afterthought.

## Pace

| Term | What it does |
|---|---|
| New information | No shot outstays its point |
| Size change | Move between sizes to refresh the eye |
| Reaction | The listener's face carries the beat |
| Pace by stakes | Tightest before the product and the ask |
