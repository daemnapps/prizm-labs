# Shot language

Bound as `{shot_language}`.

Name every shot by how close it is, the angle, how much the lens sees and how the camera moves. Size sets how close the viewer feels: wide gives facts, close gives feeling.

## Size

| Term | What it does |
|---|---|
| Wide | The room and where everyone stands |
| Full | Head to toe; body and clothes read |
| Medium | Waist up; talk and hands |
| Two-shot | Two people; the relationship |
| Close-up | A face or object; a thought changes |
| Extreme close-up | One detail; texture or proof |

Source: https://www.studiobinder.com/blog/ultimate-guide-to-camera-shots/

## Angle

| Term | What it does |
|---|---|
| Eye level | Equal; a person talking to you |
| Low angle | Power or size |
| High angle | Small, watched or overwhelmed |
| Overhead | Hands, surfaces, steps of a process |
| Odd angle | Stops the scroll; use with a reason |

Source: https://www.studiobinder.com/blog/ultimate-guide-to-camera-shots/

## Move

| Term | What it does |
|---|---|

| Push-in | Tension or attention rises |

| Pan or tilt | Follows or finds something |
| Tracking | Moves with a person |
