# Blocking and continuity

Bound as `{staging}`.

Say who stands where, who moves, and what must match from shot to shot. A wardrobe, prop or position that changes without a story reason is a defect.

## Blocking

| Term | What it does |
|---|---|
| Positions | Who is where in the room at the start of the shot |
| Eyelines | Where each person looks |
| One clear move | One action per shot, with its start and end |

Source: https://www.studiobinder.com/blog/ultimate-guide-to-camera-shots/

## Continuity

| Term | What it does |
|---|---|
| Wardrobe | Same clothes in wides and close-ups |
| Props | Same object, size, state and hand |
| Screen direction | People stay on their side and keep moving the same way |
| Light match | Same light direction through a scene |

Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Model_sheet

These are defaults. The swipe's own read wins over a default, and the video rulings win over this slice.
