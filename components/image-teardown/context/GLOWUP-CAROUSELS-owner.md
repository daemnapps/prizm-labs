## The strategy (the owner's Tella review, 2026-10-25)

Transcript: `runs/tella/Brand A/2026-10-25-men-s-grooming-creative-strategy/transcript.md`.

> "Dudes needs to be in every single one of these categories."

> "The category or the problem, what people currently do, and then what they should do. And what they should do should always just be a Dudes product."

> "We need this full experience for this to actually work."

| Face area | The problem | What guys do now | The Brand A fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nose | Oily nose, blackheads | Pore strips, squeezing | FLEX + WAR ( Deep Cleans Pores, Regulates Oil ) |
| Forehead | Shine, breakouts | Wiping the oil off, no moisturiser | PRIME ( Regulates Oil, Won't Clog Pores ) |
| Cheeks | Acne | Cutting sugar and nothing else | CORE ( Cuts Oil Production At The Source ) |
| Jawline | Bumps, ingrown hairs | Picking them with tweezers | FLEX ( Helps Defeat Acne & Ingrown Hairs ) |
| Neck | Razor bumps | Dry shaving against the grain | FLEX + WAR ( Reduces Acne & Razor Bumps ) |
