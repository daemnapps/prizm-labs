# Creative controls (the owner)

Page: https://claude.ai/artifact/XRDxT6HRWkPi6HbQTzYcHi · home: `components/creative-controls/` (dials `controls.json`, names `glossary.json`, rendered `slices/controls.md` and `GLOSSARY.md`, reader `read.py`)

One set of dials for copy, video and pictures. A reader measures the level an asset is at; a chain sets the level it should be at. Default stance: absurd, maxed out, hyper-specific, native at the top of the funnel.

## How it runs

1. A swipe comes in (picked in the Swipe Bank, or a link).
2. The reader scores it on every dial, with the reason for each level.
3. The image chain's brand step (stage 3 v4) sets ours: the preset's level (Maxed unless the order says otherwise), never below the swipe, one line per dial naming what in the picture puts it there.
4. The brief (stage 6 v9) turns each dial into a picture direction and ends section 3 with a Dials line the picture check holds the draft to.

## How to start it

- **Make this (in the Swipe Bank)**: The next time you message this work, the swipe is opened, read on every dial, and briefed at Maxed: our objects swapped in by rhyme, every dial at or above the swipe.
- **Run brief 229 at native**: The brand step and the brief run again on the Native preset: no offer, no call to action, the post look, everything else maxed. Also: "at problem aware", "at proof".
- **Absurdity down to 2 on 229**: One dial changed for that brief, everything else kept. Any dial by its name: "edge up", "more rhyme", "less words on it" (anchorage).
- **Move the sliders here, press Save**: Those levels become the preset for every brief from the next run. They are picked up the next time you message this work.
- **Score these (with links or pictures)**: The reader scores each one on every dial and adds it to this page, so you can see where it sits against what we make.
- **Add a dial for …  /  add the name …**: It goes into the creative-controls home once, and every chain that reads it (pictures now; video and copy next) gets it on its next run.

## Guards (always on)

- **Relevance**: Every absurd or swapped element is about the problem, the result or the product. Unexpected and irrelevant is the one quadrant that hurts the message (Heckler & Childers).
- **Legibility**: The problem and what is ours read inside a second, however loud the rest is (Pieters, Warlop & Wedel).
- **Benign**: Every violation carries its safety: distance, a character, obvious exaggeration (McGraw & Warren).
- **Claim-safe**: Exaggerate the premise and the emotion, never the medical or physical outcome.
- **Receipt**: Every hyper-specific detail comes from the avatar's own words or the brand's own files; none is invented.
- **Never sterile**: Every ad clears the floor on intensity, absurdity, humor, dramatisation and stopping power, at every awareness level. A proof or offer ad gets its proof inside an absurd, dramatised picture, never a plain product shot.
- **Reads cold**: The words on the ad make sense to a stranger on one read, before any joke lands (the owner, 2026-10-23, on a picture otherwise called great: "the text on that image doesn't make sense"). A borrowed sentence shape is kept only when our words still say something literally true and clear inside it; never fill the swipe's sentence with our nouns like a word game. If the line needs the swipe to be understood, rewrite it.
- **One idea**: The words and the picture say the same thing. A stranger who reads the line and then looks at the picture understands why that picture is there (the owner, 2026-10-23: "tie the image and copy together to make sure they actually make sense"). A line about something the picture does not show fails, however good it is alone.
