## Dials

- Absurdity: 0 real · 1 heightened · 2 exaggerated · 3 absurd (impossible but readable) · 4 surreal. At or above the swipe's level.
- Dramatisation: what is pushed and by how much.
- Emotion: the one feeling, and where it shows.
- Standout element: the first thing the eye hits, at a stated size and place.
- Realism: lived-in marks and phone texture that keep it from slop.

## Into the chain

1. Labelling (2b) reads the swipe's spice. 2. The brand step (3) sets ours from the bank, with reasons. 3. The brief (6) writes the dials into the picture. 4. A picture review for stills (absurd on purpose passes, softened fails, slop fails). 5. Every run banks what it used, with its receipt.
