Rules carried over from the ads
  • Lowercase. - inside a value, _ between values.
  • An ID or version number is never reused or re-pointed.
  • Avatar and angle only from the signed lists. Nothing coined.
  • Avatar, headline, offer, swipe: in the page list, not the link.
  • A page brief names its page; it never picks a landing page.
  • Emails stay out (date-driven).
