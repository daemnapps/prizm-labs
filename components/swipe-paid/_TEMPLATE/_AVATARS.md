# _AVATARS — the swiped brand's people (template)

Extracted, not invented: who THIS brand sells to, rebuilt from their own
ads, pages, and comments. Same anatomy as our avatars so the two sides
read alike: core avatar(s) with the 6 categories (desire first,
demographics last), then sub-avatars where their creative clearly
narrows. Every claim cites the ad/page/comment it came from.

Structure per brand folder:
```
avatars/
  core-avatars/
    <avatar-name>/
      profile.md
      sub-avatars/
```
