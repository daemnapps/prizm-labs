# _CONNECTIONS — the tie-back (template)

How this swiped brand connects to OUR brands. One table, lives as
`connections.md` in the brand's folder:

| their element | ours it connects to | why (the match) |
|---|---|---|
| (an angle / avatar / page / format) | <brand> · <avatar or funnel spot> | category or avatar match, one line |

The point (the owner, 2026-08-25): once a swiped brand is fully extracted —
angles, creatives, pages, funnels, avatars, language — any of our brands
can sit next to it and point: "make this, this, and this for us," based
on where our funnel is. The connections table is where that pointing
happens. Example: a competitor (men's serum, Italy, crushing) maps to
Brand B — their avatars vs our core-avatars, their funnel spots vs ours.
