# _LANGUAGE-BANK — the swiped brand's language (template)

What we can find of how their customer talks and how the brand talks to
them: phrases from their ad copy blocks, their page copy, their comment
sections. Tiered by evidence like ours (verbatim customer > brand copy),
every entry with its source. Lives as `language-bank.md` in the brand's
folder. This is THEIR bank — it never blends into any of our brands'
banks; our tools quote it as swipe evidence only.
