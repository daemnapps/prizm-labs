The daily pull — designed, waiting on the go

What it does: every morning, re-sweep each profiled brand ordered by first-seen and stop at the newest ad already held — so a quiet day costs a few credits, not a full sweep. New ads join their angle or open a new one; a new landing page gets captured; the profiles and this page rebuild; anything newly past the heat bar lands in the queue.

What it costs: the original full sweeps ran ~450–1,016 AdPlexity credits each. A daily delta is a fraction of that, but it spends credits every day across every profiled brand — which is why it is not scheduled yet.

The cascade: a queue item, on your word (or automatically, if ruled), runs the video teardown → the copy machine on its record → the copy library rebuild. Every piece of that chain exists today; the trigger is the only missing link.
