# winners

**What it is.** One brand's loop from ad results back to the writing prompts, in five steps: Results, Audit, Bank, Feed, Score. It replaces the lab creative audit and the money loop.

**Engine + owner.** `winners.py` (the one command) over `audit.py`, `statics.py` (the winning image ads, 2026-11-23: `winners.py statics`, read by ad-variations' static plan), `bank.py`, `feed.py`, `score.py`, `creators.py`, `leaderboard.py`, `five.py`, `winner_views.py`, `messages.py`, `shapes.py`, `system.py` (`winners.py system`, where a brand stands on every step). Owner: the owner.

**Definitions + curator.** The fixed on-screen types and four groups in `audit.py`, and the merging rules in `base()`, `script_key()` and `dedupe()`; `board_template.html`; the bar in each brand's `bank.json`, set by the brand owner and open until set. Creator videos (rows with a `trybe_id`) are judged on the bank's `creator_bar` when it is set, else on `bar` (`bank.bar_for`); setting it never moves a paid row. **The creator bar is the one winner bar for creator videos** (2026-11-15, the creator system's main session: spend ≥ $50, ≥ 1 purchase, ROAS ≥ 1.0, set on both brands with `winners.py bar --creators`): the creator weekly review and the ladder read a video's `state` from the bank instead of keeping their own bar. Curator: the owner. Messages are free text and reuse the brand's existing names from its tag memory.

**Plane it emits.** `runs/winners/<brand>/`: `bank.json`, `feed.md`, `tags.json`, `audit/<since>_<until>/` (`cells.json ranked.json merges.json window.json board.json board.html summary.md to_tag.json`, `teardowns/<NN-name>/{teardown.md,transcript.txt}`), `score/<asof>/{score.json,score.md}`, `creators/<asof>/trybe.json` (the creator results pull), `brief-next.json` + `brief-next.md` (what to brief next), `brief-five.json` + `brief-five.md` (the five always-on creator briefs: proven ads across paid and creator, ranked by purchases, no two of one format and angle; fields in `five.py`), `leaderboard.json` + `leaderboard.md` + `leaderboard/<date>.json` (creators and clippers ranked; the dated copies are what next week's move counts from), `sources/<source>-<date>.json` (dated snapshots read off dashboards with no API, e.g. an affiliate app; shown with their read date), `campaigns.json` (retired stand-in; the campaigns now come from `runs/creator-machine/<brand>/hub/programs.json`), `views/{yoy-<from>-<to>,store-<date>}.{md,json}`, `shapes/{calls.json,shapes.json,shapes.md,page.html}` (story shape per winner, `shapes.py`), `who-<date>/{who,who-desk}.{json,md}` (sales by who each ad was for, `who_view.py`; who comes from `components/briefs/aim.py`, the ad's card in `runs/meta-upload/<brand>/ADS-INDEX.json`, and for the avatar alone the ad set it ran in). Pictures, videos, frames and contact sheets stay out of git. Runs visible to the system: none yet (stated, O12).

**What the manifest declares per entry.** `bank.json`: `bar`, `creator_bar` (optional), then row by row: `id` (`<brand>-<first Meta ad id>`), `state` (`candidate` or `winner`), `core`, `name`, `ad_ids`, `ad_names`, `words`, chain-made fields (`brief_key`, `run`, `hook_prompt`, `brief_prompt`, `borrowed_from`), creator fields (`trybe_id`, `creator`, `tags` = the per-video read's `format`, `angle`, `hook_line`, `worked`), `source` (paths relative to `runs/winners/<brand>/`) and `snapshots` (one per audit, score or creator window).

Tagging needs a person's Claude looking at the pictures. `bank`, `feed`, `score`, `bar` and `status` need no eyes.

Runs by: person

**Who reads it.** The hook stage's `{winners}` slot reads `feed.md` (`components/video-teardown/machine/extra-stages.json`); its "Creator videos" section lists the creator videos under the bar. The proven gate (`components/video-teardown/machine/proven.py`) reads `bank.json`. The creator swipe pick reads `brief-next.json` (formats, angles, hook lines to brief more of; shape in `creators.py`). The creator weekly review (`lab/the owner/creator-machine/machine/weekly.py`) runs `creators` and `leaderboard` and reads `bank.json` states and `brief-next.json`. The brief-writing step reads `brief-five.json` (the five always-on briefs). The brand's creator hub page and the Creator System page read `leaderboard.json` (fields in `README.md`).

**What it reads.**
- The warehouse copy of the Meta ad data, through funnel-tracking's `views._psql` (an internal, flagged in the fit check) and `brand_sources`. `audit.warehouse_ads()` is the one read, used by both the audit and the score.
- Meta itself, through meta-upload's `Meta(brand)`, for days the warehouse lacks and for pictures, copy and video links. Read only; nothing in an ad account changes.
- `components/image-teardown/briefs.json`, `components/naming/names.py`, and run records under `runs/video-writing/` and `runs/video-teardown/`; for a brief made elsewhere, the prompt records in its run folder in any lane.
- funnel-tracking's `history.pull()` for `store` (the warehouse, no HTTP).
- funnel-tracking's `trybe.py` CLI for `creators`: Trybe's brand API (GET) and the warehouse's Meta ads whose name ends in a video's trybe id. Never the ad account.
- For `leaderboard`: the payout programs, `runs/creator-machine/<brand>/hub/programs.json`, and creators' public handles, `runs/creator-machine/<brand>/inbox/inbox.json` (local), as data. Report links are used to pull and never written out.
- swipe-organic's `content_rewards.py --no-media` CLI for `leaderboard`: each campaign's public report (free; never `--deep`, which spends Apify), filed under `runs/content-rewards/<brand>/<pool>/<date>/`.
- The creator machine's per-video reads, `runs/creator-machine/<brand>/*/checks/*.json`, as data (workspace rule 9).
- `runs/media-buying/<brand>/filter/*/verdicts.json`, only when the warehouse can't be reached.

**Keys.** By name only: `SUPABASE_DB_URL` through funnel-tracking's loader; the brand's Meta token through meta-upload's brand config. None here.

**Rules.**
- No brand name in code. A brand enters only as data.
- The bar is the brand owner's. Nothing sets it on its own.
- A re-bank never drops words already in a row, and never moves a row id.
- The feed and Core read the bank's widest audit window; a shorter audit never replaces them.
- Teardowns spend, so `videos` and the teardowns after it run only on a person's go.
- Reports carry facts, never a verdict.

**Tests.** `python3 -m unittest discover -s components/winners/tests`.

**Results back to writing and the queue (2026-11-18).**
- `feed_sections.py` writes `taught.md` beside `feed.md` (feed.md itself is unchanged, since the live hook prompt reads it whole): the top ad per angle (type, angle, concept, the customer words its writing run traced), the desk's calls and where they differed from its suggestion (`components/media-buying/machine/calls.py`, reading `runs/media-buying/<brand>/decisions.jsonl`), the send-back reasons from the approval page (`runs/meta-upload/<brand>/SENT-BACK.json`, counts only) and the score per prompt version (the newest `score/<asof>/score.json`). Offline; a source that can't be read costs its own section one line.
- `queue_source.py` is the results' queue source: every winner in the bank becomes one `iteration` piece in `runs/queue-shadow/observations/winners.json`, through creative-queue's `qcore.write_observation`; the live queue is refused while its switch is off. An open bar adds nothing.
- `messages.py` (2026-11-25, `winners.py messages`): every winner through the same gates (proof, teardown, message, group, rules) to the next brief. A winner's message is its own teardown's THE CORE MESSAGE, its spec's load-bearing list and its doctrine's desire, carried by `winner-index.json` (`message_read`, built by video-writing's `winner_index.py`, the one reader). `runs/winners/<brand>/messages/messages.json` holds only what a person decides: the group, what the brand's rules cut, the briefs. `check` fails on a winner left ungrouped; `start` refuses one that is not through. The writing reads the same message: v2 through `{winner_lane}`, v1 at every writing pass of a run started with `--winner`.
- `shapes.py` (2026-11-26, `winners.py shapes todo|check|build`): the story shape of every winner, for any brand. A call per winner (arc from creative-controls' `arcs`, or none; fit clear / loose / none; devices from `story_devices`; production, script and hook format from video-production's `formats/names.json`; teller; flag; the transcript lines that prove it) is read off the teardown the chain already wrote, no video and no model. `runs/winners/<brand>/shapes/calls.json` holds only those calls and the brand's notes (`near` presets with a reason, caveats, proposed wiring). `build` writes `shapes.json` (the roll-up by arc: winners, sales, spend recorded and derived, cost per sale, return, devices, the story presets of the same arc and the nearest by shared devices, plus creative-controls' `research/arc-evidence.json`), `shapes.md` (team copy, founder-told proof lines kept out) and `page.html` (the owner's page). `check` fails on an uncalled winner or an id not in the lists; `todo` lists the uncalled with the files to read. Needs the brand's `winner-index.json` (video-writing's `winner_index.py build --brand`). Folds count once; retires are listed, not ranked.
- `system.py` (2026-11-26, `winners.py system --brand <b>|all`): every step from ad results to a brief for any brand, in order (results, audit, bank, bar, teardowns, proof, index, messages, shapes, profiles, brief), each `ready`, `owner` (only the brand owner moves it), `spend` (on a person's go), `todo` (free), `stale` or `blocked`, with the exact next command. Files only; writes `runs/winners/<brand>/system.{json,md}`.
- **Proof for any brand** (2026-11-26): the winner index ties each Core teardown run to its bank row through the brand's registry when it has one, else through the audit video the run tore down (`<since>_<until>/videos/NN-<name>.mp4` = the row whose `source.teardown` is `teardowns/NN-<name>/`). A second run of the same post or video folds under the original (Core-named, then a finished teardown). The record carries `bank_id`; the variation proof gate (`proven.py`) reads it too, and the writing's winner lane reads the winner's built story shape as its section plan. A brand needs no hand-made registry.
- **Results enter in one place**: the score's results read and the audit's pull (`audit.warehouse_ads()`, the warehouse, else Meta read-only). Every step after reads the bank and the score, so a new results source (for example an app connected to the ad account) replaces that one read and nothing downstream changes.
- `breakdown.py` and `proposed/winner-breakdown-v1.md` are held for review and not live: a winner taken apart in one fixed order, filed to `breakdown/<row id>.{json,md}` and onto the top-ads board. Nothing calls them.
