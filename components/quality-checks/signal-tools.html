What our avatar viewers already check

The 5 viewers read a description of the ad, second by second. They don't see pixels. The models do.

QuestionAvatar viewers (live)The four checks
Did their thumb stop?✅ in the first 2 seconds, compared with the real posts around it—
When did they leave?✅ the second they swiped, plus a retention curve✅ Will they keep watching, read off the real video (sound and picture)
When did it feel like an ad, and why?✅ the second, and what gave it away—
Would they tap or tell a friend?✅ yes or no, and why, in their words—
What will they remember?✅ the line or picture, and what they'd text a friend—
Where exactly are their eyes?❌ can't, they don't see pixels✅ Where do eyes go
Does the face show the right emotion?❌ they only read "a man smiles"✅ What does the face show
Is anything under the app's buttons?❌✅ Hidden by the app

The viewers judge the brief before anything is made. The four checks look at the real frames and the finished clip.
