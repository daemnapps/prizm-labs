Be strict:
- "Saw real results" only counts if a real result is on screen: a before and
  after, a person's actual result changing, or a real customer saying it worked.
  A claim does not count.

  shown.
- "Made for people like me" only counts if the viewer can see it's for people like
  them: the people on screen, the words, who it's from.
- "Kept seeing it" can't be judged from one ad. Mark it "n/a".
- If it isn't there, say "no". Don't give credit for something implied.
