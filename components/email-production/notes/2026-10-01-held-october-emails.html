Two causes, and most of them are the files

Many holds are not wrong facts. The brand files have not kept up. the email lead's GLOWBOO coupon for the Brand B Halloween sale is ruled, but brands/Brand B's offer bank still says "we don't use discount codes", so a correct email is held. A review count or a usage tip that is true but written nowhere is held the same way. the developer's call (1 Oct): updating the brand files first just to clear the check would slow output too much right now.

Some are invented. The writer also adds counts, timings and history no file states ("my father … formulated" contradicts story.md). How the 49 fact holds split between the two is unknown until a person rules on them.

The same thing happens in the other checker. components/qc-checks reads prices and names from brands/<brand>/qc-dictionary.json; when that file is behind it flags correct work (the $39.99 face-scrub price was still on the banned list, I-134 (face-scrub price)). qc-checks never stops work, so there it costs trust in the findings rather than speed.

The UNFILLED notes are mostly the same gap

When the writer meets a part of its template it cannot fill from the files, it leaves a marked gap, [UNFILLED — …], instead of inventing. The check holds any finished email that still carries one. Across the email lead's runs there are 68 distinct notes, of four kinds:

So invented facts and UNFILLED notes are two answers to the same missing fact: one guesses, one leaves a hole. Both clear the same way.

The fix: a person's ruling becomes a source of facts

Held email (or a qc-checks finding)→the email lead rules on each problem
Her rulingWhat happens to the emailWhat happens to the facts
Wrong — fix the emailShe edits the draft; the check runs again on her copy; a pass makes the Klaviyo draft.Nothing.
True — the files are behindGoes ahead now; the Klaviyo draft is made.The fact joins the brand's verified facts: her name, time, evidence (a link or the ruling it came from), and an optional end date (a coupon ends). From the next run, the writer may use it and neither check holds it.
For an UNFILLED note: fill / not ours"Fill": she supplies the fact and the check re-runs. "Not ours": the part is dropped and the email goes ahead."Fill" adds a verified fact. "Not ours" adds a recorded "the brand doesn't have this", and the writer skips that part from then on.
True this onceGoes ahead with her reason recorded.Nothing; it does not carry to other emails.

One verified-facts list per brand, read by both checkers (quality-checks and qc-checks), not one each — two stale sources is how the $39.99 false finding happened. It lives on the records plane, not in brands/, so no machine writes the brand folder. Once a week the email lead, the brand owner, folds the week's verified facts into the brand files (offer bank, product files, qc-dictionary.json) by hand, and they leave the list. The check stays exactly as strict; only its sources grow.

Guards: a verified fact belongs to one brand only; a fact needs evidence to be verified; the drafts page shows "verified by the email lead, 1 Oct" on any email that leans on one; time-bound facts expire. Kill: if more than about 1 in 10 "true" rulings in the first two weeks later prove wrong in a sent email, a verified fact also needs the brand owner's confirmation before it counts.
