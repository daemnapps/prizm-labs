# The Media Industry Map

Live page: https://claude.ai/artifact/29rhL2CBaH85f1VB4zfD27 · October 2026

Media is one business that keeps changing shape: make something people pay attention to, then sell that attention or turn it into a sale.

> **Correction up front:** by dollars, media (~$3T/yr globally) is not the biggest consumer industry. Food, housing and healthcare are bigger. Media wins on **time**: it's the biggest claim on consumers' waking hours, and every other industry buys its way in through it.

## 1 · The skill: your edge is where skills overlap
Top 25% at four rarely-combined skills beats world-class at one (the "talent stack", or T-shaped).
- **Direct response**: hooks, awareness levels, offers, proof
- **Media economics**: how attention gets made, distributed and paid for
- **Consumer culture**: what people watch, who they trust, how they buy
- **Machine judgment**: taste turned into repeatable prompts and rules

How skills get learned: deliberate practice (Ericsson), meaning work just past your ability with fast feedback. Your version: read output, then tighten the prompt. The trap: learning that never becomes a prompt rule within a week.

## 3 · How it works
Loop: **Make → Distribute → Capture attention → Earn trust → Monetize** (ads, subscriptions, commerce, licensing).
1. **Two customers.** The audience pays with time, the advertiser with money.
2. **Distribution beats content.** Aggregators take the profit (Thompson's aggregation theory).
3. **Hits rule.** Power law: make many cheap bets and double down on winners.
4. **Abundance moves the value.** Cheap content shifts value to taste, trust, data and owned audiences.
5. **Rented vs owned audience.** Followers are rented; lists and customers are owned.
6. **Formats travel, brands don't.** Structure transfers across categories.

## 4 · Content farms: what killed them
- **Demand Media (2009–11):** millions of cheap articles written for search; IPO at $1.5B; killed by Google Panda. Built on one algorithm with no quality floor.
- **BuzzFeed (2012–23):** grew on Facebook distribution; collapsed when reach was cut; News shut down in 2023. Rented audience.
- **Survivors:** volume plus a quality bar, owned audiences, and commerce revenue.

**For the build:** a quality judge before anything ships; spread across platforms; every ad feeds an owned list; swipe structure, never surface copy.
