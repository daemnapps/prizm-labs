# The contractor app — idea, in progress

Damon, 18 Oct 2026. An idea to work through, not a build.

## The problem

Outside contractors (clippers, editors, creators) make our content. Their
automations should scale on **our** context and tools. Today the only way to hand
that over is a repo. A repo changes all the time, and most people can't read one.

## The idea

A content production app for contractors.

- **A link, not a download.** It opens in the phone or desktop browser and adds
  to the home screen like an app. There's no app store, and nothing to install
  first.
- **Credentials per person.** Each contractor signs in as themselves and sees
  only what is theirs. Access can be turned off for one person without touching
  anyone else.
- **Private servers host the information.** It is served from our own machines,
  not a public page and not a shared login.

## What a contractor sees

- **Their briefs.** What to make, for which brand, by when.
- **Brand context in plain words.** The product, who it's for, the angles, and
  what never to say.
- **The swipes to copy.** The originals their briefs are built from.
- **One place to deliver.** Finished files go in, named and filed for them.

## What their automations get

- **A key per contractor.** Their tools pull the brand context and their briefs
  straight from the app, so a clipping or editing machine runs on our real
  context, not a copy that goes stale.
- **Read what's theirs, write only deliveries.** A key can never reach another
  contractor's work or anything internal.

## Open questions

- **Sign-in.** An emailed one-time code? A magic link? Something else?
- **Which brand material** an outsider may see, and what stays internal.
- **How a delivery comes back:** straight into the edit line, or into a review
  first.
- **Pricing and terms.** Whether contractors are paid per brief and per
  delivery, and whether that's tracked here.
- **Who approves sharing with outsiders.** Internal rule: anything leaving the
  company needs a lead's yes.

## Shape so far

Our existing tools stay the source: the briefs, the brand files and the swipe
library. The contractor app is a new front door on top of them, never a second
copy.
