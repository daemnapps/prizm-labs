# Portal (formerly POV Funnel Lab)

Live page: https://claude.ai/artifact/Q7ZaFfQtER6VQ4MWC7Q8yL (v12, 8 Oct 2026). Page source is private; this mirror carries the general sections. Brand-specific scripts and avatar detail live on the page only.

## The idea: you are the player

The ad is a moment of her day seen through her own eyes (Ray-Ban Meta style: hands, feet, room; her face only in a mirror, window or selfie camera). The click hands her the controls of that same moment, and she plays it through to the offer.

POV ad → same eyes, now she steers → diagnose → learn → qualify → reward → hero offer + subscription + bumps

The shows (game shows, AFV, Wheel of Fortune, Chuck E. Cheese, POV sports) are format research, not the funnel.

## Rule one: build the world from what the avatar already plays and watches

| Avatar | World | Moves to borrow |
|---|---|---|
| Women 55–65 | Her bathroom mirror on an event morning | Tap to inspect, wipe the fog, puzzle board, progress bar |
| Women 25–45 | Merge + story (Gossip Harbor: ~79% women, avg 32) | Merge two into one (= bundle bump), decorate, chest opens |
| Younger men | Barbershop walk-in, open-world energy (own art, no GTA marks) | Walk up, interact prompt, mission complete, loot |

## Quiz → world: same jobs, done with her hands

1. **Enter**: the first frame after the click is the ad's last frame
2. **Diagnose**: look in the mirror, tap where the problem is
3. **Educate**: look out the window, see her real city and today's UV
4. **Educate**: pick up the product, see the ingredients and texture
5. **Qualify**: random generator for a bonus, on top of her real answers
6. **Reward**: the gift lands and the meter fills
7. **Offer**: hero offer, subscribe and save, merge into the multi-pack, upsell

## POV ad grammar

1. Hands or feet in frame within 2s, plus a caption naming her moment
2. One slow head turn: where and when
3. One small true detail of her day
4. The product comes by hand, never held to the lens
5. Sound carries the ritual (grains, water, breathing)
6. The mirror shows her face for the first time, after the change
7. Touch test, with an inner-thought caption
8. End on her counter with the offer line

Eye height. Slow whole-head turns, holding each look 2–3s. Keep a slight bob. Cut on head turns, blinks or steam. Caption every beat like a diary.

Evidence: Ray-Ban Meta creator impressions grew ~6.6× in a year (CreatorIQ). Meta data puts 9:16 + a visible person + sound + story at 16% lower CPA. No clean published ad result isolates POV yet, so test it against the best UGC on the same offer.

## Portal v7 to v12: what the build taught us (8 Oct 2026)

**Format rules**
- **Built for women 40+ on a phone.** The type matches the brand's own quiz: a 32px headline (26px on small phones), 17px body, 58px answers and a 62px button. It is checked on iPhone SE, standard and Pro Max, with no screen needing to scroll.
- **Her view stays readable.** Text sits in a solid card under her view, never on video. Each scene is framed on its subject, so faces, products and tap points are never under the logo or the card.
- **One mechanic everywhere:** tap an answer, then tap Continue. Tap-to-discover points sit on real objects in the scene. The qualify step reveals the fixed offer stack on three reels she sets off herself; the wording is plain ("Reveal what I get", "All yours"), never gambling words.
- **The last screen** is the funnel's own offer carousel. The button is the quiz's proven CTA, and it always lands on the same offer box the quiz uses, so the test is fair.
- **Heritage versions** have the elder and the founder speak the buyer's language with their own accent. Captions match the audio word for word, and each line is checked by transcribing it back.

**Production rules**
- **Stills:** maximum quality at 2K. The product always comes from the real packshot and the real texture photo.
- **Clips:** a Google video model (Omni Flash) or Seedance; avoid models that make awkward motion.
- **Every clip gets a frame sweep before it ships.** Any object that changes shape (a sponge into a cap, a root reshaping, a teapot vanishing) means the scene ships as a still until a clean render passes.
- **iPhone playback:** clips always play muted, which iPhones allow, and the voice player is unlocked on her first tap. Otherwise some scenes freeze on the still.

**Measurement**
- Every scene, answer, discovery and milestone goes to the data layer for the tag manager.
- Microsoft Clarity runs on the Portal pages, with labels per scene, per answer and per milestone, for heatmaps and session recordings.

**QC**
- The platform's own campaign QA ran with real test orders (test card): checkout, accept both upsells, decline both upsells. All passed, with discounts, totals and purchase events correct.

## v6: Portal (8 Oct 2026)

Damon named the format **Portal**. One engine, many versions; every word, scene and offer line lives in a version file.

- **Same rule on every screen:** her view on top, a solid card below, one button in the same place, no italics.
- **Mechanics:** pick answers, explore (tap points in her view to discover one line each), learn, a qualify screen where the fixed offer stack unlocks line by line, her answers read back, then the offer.
- **Fixed offer stack:** 1 free full-size tube (Buy 2, Get 1) · free travel pouch · 60-day money-back guarantee.
- **Tracked:** time on each screen, answers, discoveries, voice lines played, back, sound, leaving the tab, qualified, offer viewed and clicked with total time. Sent to the data layer and to Meta as custom events.
- **Quality bar:** stills at maximum quality and 2K; the product always comes from the real packshot. Scenes she taps, and label close-ups, stay as stills.
- **Heritage versions:** an elder speaks her own language in her own voice, with captions. Never dubbed into English. A native speaker approves the lines before launch.

## v4: the POV Spot Check, ready to test

The live quiz's questions and answers, word for word, lived as a first-person morning. 13 screens over 9 scenes: 7 four-second clips with sound and 2 product stills. Shots where the label faces the camera, and the scrub texture, stay as stills so the product is exact. It ends with her answers read back, a qualify draw for a bonus, and the real offer box.

**Test:** same ad, 50/50 vs the current quiz. Both use the same words and the same offer box. Read purchase rate, CPA, AOV, sub take-rate, completion and 60-day refunds.

**Before live:** pick the bonus pool and make it reach the order; settle the ingredient-list conflict (chamomile on quiz/landing vs the FAQ list); build as a new page beside the quiz; Meta events per step (page + server); a POV ad that ends on the opening frame.

Every image and video prompt is shown verbatim on the live page (Prompt library).

## How real it can look (v3)

The prototype is laid out like Facebook Stories, not a game: full-screen photo-real first-person shots, big words, one big tap per screen, progress bars at the top. A 60-year-old buyer already knows those moves.

1. **Level 1 (live now):** photo-real AI stills, ~150 KB each, product built from the real packshot.
2. **Level 2 (next):** each still becomes a 4-second POV clip with sound (Kling 3.0 / Veo 3.1). Mirror shots stay stills or real footage.
3. **Level 3 (best):** real Ray-Ban Meta footage from one creator in her 60s. The same footage cuts the ads, so ad and page are one continuous moment.

## Gift with purchase

- "3 months of Spotify free" can't be bought as a trial; gift cards cover full-price months only (~$39). Real trials come via Spotify partner deals.
- Tremendous or Tango auto-send $5–10 digital gift cards at face value.
- An iPhone "with purchase" needs a sweepstakes: free entry route, official rules, NY/FL registration + bond over $5,000.
