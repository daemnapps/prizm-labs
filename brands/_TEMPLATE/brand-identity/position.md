## The method, for the next brand

The transferable part, in order:

1. Find the problem the premium players won't name, because naming it is
   beneath their positioning.
2. Name its **mechanism**. Mechanism disqualifies everything the reader has
   already failed with.
3. Read the **market's stage** off what the reader has already been pitched —
   the bank's objection counts and the competitor reads — and let that decide
   what the opening leads with.
4. Take the language wholesale from the market. Never translate it into brand
   voice.
5. Own a cultural territory the category has left empty, and make the proof come
   from inside it.
